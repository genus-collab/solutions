# Formal quintic, genus 2: constant symplectic colour-mixing factorisations R = R' M (2026-11-04)

Folder: hodge_vertex_quintic/. Follows FORMAL_QUINTIC_genus2.md (engine, conventions, conifold frame Cf with alpha = beta = 0), SCALAR_SCAN_genus2.md (covariant scalar frames) and DIAG_SCAN_genus2.md (diagonal scalar frames). New code: fq_sser.py (rows in the conifold frame as Laurent series in s, normalised basis, the matrices M), fq_mvertex.py (the vertex CohFT M.omega), fq_msum.py (the outer graph sum), fq_mfit.py (interpolation, Galois reconstruction, F_p linear algebra), fq_msolve.py (the systems), fq_mfourier.py (Z/5 Fourier structure), fq_mreport.py (validation blocks), driver genus2_symplectic.py -> logs/genus2_symplectic.log (both primes 2^89-1 and 2^61-1, 221 s, every check PASS) and symplectic_result.json (all polynomial coefficients in Q(zeta), ranks, dimensions). No existing module was changed. Everything is an exact finite computation (F_p with rational reconstruction; Q(zeta)-valued entries reconstructed from their Galois conjugates). Genus 2 only; AI-checked only.

## 1. Set-up

**Normalised basis.** e~_i = Delta_i^{1/2} e_i, so eta(e~_i, e~_j) = delta_ij, the unit is 1 = Sum_k Delta_k^{-1/2} e~_k and the TFT is omega_{g,n}(e~_i^{(x)n}) = Delta_i^{(2g-2+n)/2}. In the conifold frame Delta_i^{1/2} = C_0 zeta^{3i/2}/L with zeta^{1/2} := zeta^3, i.e. Delta_i^{1/2} = zeta^{4i} x, x := Cf(C_0)/L = s f_0(s^5), f_0 = 1 + (2/625)u^3 + ... (checked against Cf(Delta_i) = zeta^{3i} x^2). Rows Q_a^{(i)}(z) := eta(H^a, R(z) e~_i) = P_a^{(i)}(z)/Delta_i^{1/2} with P from fq_rows; symplecticity Sum_ab Q_a^{(i)}(z) eta^{ab} Q_b^{(j)}(-z) = delta_ij, eta^{ab} = (1/5) delta_{a+b = 3 mod 5}.

**The family.** M(z) = exp(m_1 z + m_2 z^2 + m_3 z^3) with constant (q-independent) 5x5 matrices in the normalised basis, symplectic: m_1^T = m_1, m_2^T = -m_2, m_3^T = m_3 (transpose = eta-adjoint since eta is the identity there), so M(z)^T M(-z) = 1. Parameters: 15 + 10 + 15 = 40, of which only m_1 and m_2 (25) can matter at genus 2 (s.2). The factorisation is R = R' M, R' = R M^{-1}, i.e. Q'^{(i)}_a(z) = Sum_j Q^{(j)}_a(z) (M^{-1}(z))_{ji} (convolution in z). Odd and even powers of z are both allowed (unlike the scalar case, where only odd powers are symplectic), and M mixes the colours. The covariant scalar frames of SCALAR_SCAN are the points m_1 = a_1 diag(zeta^{-i}), m_2 = 0, m_3 = a_2 diag(zeta^{-3i}); the diagonal frames of DIAG_SCAN are the diagonal points.

**The graph sum.** By the group property of the R-matrix action (PPZ Prop. 2.14), R.omega = (R' M).omega = R'.(M.omega): the stable-graph sum with rows R' and the colour-MIXING vertex CohFT Omega^M := M.omega. Explicitly, Omega^M_{g,n}(e~_{i_1}, ..., e~_{i_n}) prod psi_l^{d_l} is the PPZ sum over stable graphs of genus g with n legs, with monochrome TFT vertices (colour j_v, factor Delta_{j_v}^{(2g_v-2+n_v+m_v)/2}, point-class integrals), legs (M^{-1}(psi_l))_{j_v i_l}, kappa-leaves T(z) = z(1 - M^{-1}(z) 1), edges (delta - M^{-1}(psi) M^{-1}(psi')^T)/(psi + psi'), summed over the colours of all half-edges and leaves. Since Sum_v (2g_v-2+n_v+m_v) = 2g-2+n+m and each leaf of the unit carries Delta^{-1/2} = x^{-1} zeta^{-4k}, every term is x^{2g-2+n} times an F_p constant, so Omega^M_{g,n} = x^{2g-2+n} OmegaHat with OmegaHat computed over F_p from the constant TFT Delta^hat_j^{1/2} = zeta^{4j}. The total M-order of a term is the psi-deficit D = 3g-3+n-Sum d (<= 3 at genus 2), so inner graphs have at most D edges. The outer sum per stable graph Gamma uses the outer edges E'_{ij}(psi, psi') = (delta_ij - Sum_ab Q'^{(i)}_a(-psi) eta^{ab} Q'^{(j)}_b(-psi'))/(psi + psi'), [psi^m psi'^n] = Sum_{k<=n} (-1)^k G'_{m+1+k, n-k}, and the outer dilaton leaves T'(z) = z(1 - R'^{-1}(z) 1). From R'^{-1}(z) = R'(-z)^* (symplecticity) and eta(1, .) = eta(H^0, .) one gets (R'^{-1}(z) 1)_i = Q'^{(i)}_0(-z), hence T'^{(i)}_d = (-1)^d Q'^{(i)}_{0,d-1} (d >= 2); the alternative form from R'^{-1} = M R^{-1}, T'_d = -[z^{d-1}] (M(z) Q_0(-z))_i, was checked equal, and at M = 1 both reduce to Dil_i(d)/Delta_i^{1/2} of fq_rows. The leaves are treated as extra legs of Omega^M carrying the vector T'(psi), exactly as gsum_q.term_value does for R.omega. All decorations with psi-deficit are summed (the Hodge pruning of fq_graphs.Decorated is not valid for M.omega, which has non-zero genus-0 corrections), over all 5^{#half-edges + #leaves} colourings as tensor contractions; one evaluation of all 7 graphs through O(s^12) takes 0.1 s.

**Target.** FORMAL_QUINTIC s.3 gives the colour-summed polar part of each stable graph in the point frame as c_Gamma t_c^{-2} + c'_Gamma t_c^{-1} with c_Gamma = 1807/3072000, -811/256000, -57/64000, 843/1024000, 3/51200, 87/32000, 9/12800 (graphs 1..7: genus-2 vertex; genus-1 loop; genus-0 double loop; g1-g1; g0 loop - g1; theta; dumbbell). The question: is there a constant symplectic M for which the polar part of every stable graph (summed over all its colourings, the only meaningful object when M mixes colours) equals c_Gamma t_c^{-2}? In s = 1/L, with t_c = s^5 + (7/10) s^{10} + ..., this means: the s^{-10} coefficient is c_Gamma (it is M-independent, s.2), the s^{-5} coefficient equals -(7/5) c_Gamma, and the coefficients of s^{-9}, ..., s^{-6}, s^{-4}, ..., s^{-1} vanish. The s^{-5} target is equivalent to c'_Gamma = c_{-5} + (7/5) c_Gamma = 0.

## 2. Valuation bookkeeping with colour mixing

Each normalised row coefficient has val Q^{(i)}_{a,n} = -4n + (0,-1,3,2,1)_a - 1, independent of the colour (checked for n <= 4), and Q_{a,n} is s^{val} times a power series in u = s^5 (every Cf image of a numerator monomial with L-power m is s^{-m} times a u-series, SCALAR_SCAN s.1; the referee re-derived the single-class property for n <= 4). Multiplying by the constant coefficient [z^k] M^{-1} (weight k, where m_j has weight j) replaces Q_{a,n} by a combination of the Q^{(j)}_{a,n-k}: the valuation rises by exactly 4k and the exponent class mod 5 drops by k. At a vertex, a psi-deficit D is compensated by inner M-orders of total weight D, which lower the half-edge and leaf orders by D in total and raise the valuation by 4D. Since every weight-0 decoration of a genus-2 graph has valuation exactly -10 (FORMAL_QUINTIC s.3), the weight-w part of every colour-summed graph lies in s^{-10+4w} Q(zeta)[[u]], and the total weight is at most 3g-3 = 3. Hence:

- weight 0 (M-independent): s^{-10}, s^{-5}, s^0, s^5, ... -- every u-series coefficient of every stable graph is a frame invariant among constant symplectic factorisations, in particular both polar coefficients in t_c and the constant term;
- weight 1 (m_1): s^{-6}, s^{-1}, s^4, ...;
- weight 2 (m_2, m_1 m_1): s^{-2}, s^3, ...;
- weight 3 (m_3, m_1^3, m_1 m_2): s^2, s^7, ... -- never polar.

So the s^{-5} coefficient, and with it the 1/t_c coefficient c'_Gamma, cannot be moved by any constant symplectic M, colour-mixing or not. A polar u-series coefficient first becomes reachable when a monomial of weight 5j can sit at a negative order: a weight-5j part lies in s^{-10(g-1)+20j} Q(zeta)[[u]], so at genus 3 weight 5 reaches only the constant term s^0 and at genus 4 weight 5 reaches s^{-10} = t_c^{-2}. (A q-dependent M is outside this scan.) All of this is verified in the computation: s.3 (random full M), s.4 (the fitted monomial content) and the u-series check below.

## 4. The interpolated structure

The polar coefficients s^{-10}, ..., s^{-1} of the 7 colour-summed graphs were fitted as polynomials in the 40 entries of (m_1, m_2, m_3) over the 161 monomials 1, m_1 (15), m_2 (10), m_3 (15), m_1 m_1 (120), from 170 random symplectic M (Vandermonde rank 161, the 9 surplus rows consistent) and verified on 10 further random M for all 70 (graph, order) pairs. The weight-3 monomials m_1^3 and m_1 m_2 were not in the basis: their absence from every polar order is implied by the class bookkeeping (weight 3 lives at s^2, s^7, ...) and is confirmed by the consistency of the surplus and verification points, which would fail if any monomial outside the basis contributed. The m_3 monomials were in the basis and never appear. The monomial content is exactly as predicted:

Colour-shift covariance (i -> i+1 with z -> zeta^{-1} z, acting as m_k -> zeta^{-k} P m_k P^T) gives c_{shift mu} = zeta^w c_mu for a weight-w monomial; it holds on all fitted coefficients. ## 5. The solve

**The u-series coefficients.** The t_c^{-1} coefficients c'_Gamma = c_{-5} + (7/5) c_Gamma are M-independent: -791/15360000, -1771/3840000, 41/12800, 743/15360000, -41/153600, 37/160000, -173/64000 (graphs 1..7; they sum to 0, the total having no 1/t_c term). The full system (s^{-9}..s^{-1} of all 7 graphs with target c_{-5} = -(7/5) c_Gamma), version (a) (edge graphs 2..7 only) and version (b) (the s^{-5} targets only) are all INCONSISTENT, each exactly at the pairs (Gamma, s^{-5}), with residual c'_Gamma; no other (graph, order) pair obstructs. So the target "polar part = c_Gamma t_c^{-2}" is unreachable for every stable graph by any constant symplectic factorisation; the 1/t_c coefficients (and, by s.3, the constant terms) of the stable graphs are invariants of the point frame.

**What can be achieved: the subleading system** s^{-6} = s^{-1} = s^{-2} = 0 for all 7 graphs (all polar coefficients outside the u-series). It is consistent (M = 1 solves it) and its solution set keeps every stable graph's polar part equal to the point-frame one, c_Gamma t_c^{-2} + c'_Gamma t_c^{-1}. In the Fourier modes L_{d,a} (m_1) and B_{d,a} := Sum_i zeta^{ai} m2_{i,i+d} (d = 1, 2; m2_{i,i+d} := -m2_{i+d,i} when i+d wraps) the covariance rule says: the weight-1 coefficients involve only the modes L_{d,1}, the m_2 parts only B_{d,2}, the m_1 m_1 parts only products L_{d,a} L_{d',a'} with a + a' = 2 mod 5 -- all three CHECKED on the fitted polynomials, not assumed. Then:

- Linear part: the 14 weight-1 forms (s^{-6}, s^{-1} of 7 graphs) have rank 3 and span exactly the three modes L_{0,1}, L_{1,1}, L_{2,1}. Solution K = {m_1 : L_{d,1}(m_1) = 0, d = 0, 1, 2}, dimension 12 (RREF basis in Q(zeta) in the log, e.g. k_1 = m1_00 - zeta m1_44, k_8 = m1_13 + (1 + zeta + zeta^2 + zeta^3) m1_24). The covariant scalar direction m_1 = a_1 diag(zeta^{-i}) has L_{0,1} = 5 a_1 and is not in K unless a_1 = 0, in agreement with SCALAR_SCAN s.4; the Hodge frames F4 (a_1 = -1/12) and F0 (a_1 = -3/20) are therefore outside K.
- m_2 part of s^{-2}: the 7 forms have rank 1, all proportional to b := B_{1,2} + kappa B_{2,2} with kappa = c_{02}/c_{01} = (zeta - zeta^3)/(1 + zeta + zeta^2 + 2 zeta^3) = -(1 + zeta + zeta^2) (equal to the logged residues at both primes); zero for graphs 3, 6, 7. Kernel of b: dimension 9 (RREF basis in Q(zeta) in the log).
- m_1 m_1 part of s^{-2}: on K, with x_d = L_{d,0}, y_d = L_{d,2}, z_d = L_{d,3}, w_d = L_{d,4}, each graph's quadratic form is Q^Gamma = x^T C^Gamma y + z^T E^Gamma w (3x3 matrices C^Gamma, E^Gamma over Q(zeta), logged as residues with the Q(zeta) coefficients of the orbit representatives of the m_1 m_1 monomials). The s^{-2} equations read beta^Gamma b(m_2) + Q^Gamma(m_1) = 0; eliminating b with graph 1 (beta^1 != 0) leaves 5 independent quadrics on K (graph 7's follows from Sum_Gamma = 0).

Solution set of the subleading system: {m_1 in K on the common zero locus of the 5 quadrics} x {m_2 : b(m_2) = -Q^1(m_1)/beta^1} (a 9-dimensional affine space) x {m_3 free}. The quadric locus contains the four 6-dimensional linear subspaces {x = 0 or y = 0} x {z = 0 or w = 0} (on which every Q^Gamma vanishes identically), and at random points of each of them the Jacobian of the 5 quadrics has rank 5 at both primes, so the locus is smooth of LOCAL dimension 7 there; generic points of K are not on it. Hence the solution set has local dimension 7 + 9 = 16 in (m_1, m_2) at those points. The global geometry of the locus (other components, possibly of different dimension) was not computed. A concrete 6-parameter family: m_1 with only the Fourier modes a in {2, 4}, i.e. m1_{i,i+d} = (1/5)(zeta^{-2i} y_d + zeta^{-4i} w_d) (x = z = 0, so Q^Gamma = 0), together with any m_2 in the 9-dimensional kernel of b and any m_3: every such M leaves the polar part of every stable graph at its point-frame value.

## 6. Relation to the scalar and diagonal scans

SCALAR_SCAN (covariant scalar, 2 parameters) and DIAG_SCAN (diagonal scalar, 10 parameters) are the sub-families m_2 = 0, m_1 and m_3 diagonal; their vertex tables are reproduced here (step 2) and their frames through the independent N-ring route (step 3). Those scans imposed conditions per COLOURED graph (115 of them) and found the weak systems solved only by a_1 = 0; with colour mixing the coloured graphs are no longer defined and the natural objects are the colour sums per stable graph, which is why the subleading system here has a 16-dimensional (local) solution set rather than the single point of the diagonal case. The negative conclusion is the same in all three scans and has the same cause: the s^{-10} (and now also the s^{-5}) coefficient is a weight-0 quantity, untouched by any constant frame change; the colour-mixing scan adds that the full u-series part of every stable graph is invariant. Even q-dependent covariant scalars cannot remove the leading pole of all edge graphs (SCALAR_SCAN s.5); q-dependent colour-mixing M was not examined.

## 7. Caveats and referee items

- Genus 2 only; constant M only. At genus 3 weight-5 monomials reach the constant term, at genus 4 the polar order t_c^{-2}; nothing here bears on those cases. Everything is exact finite data at two primes; no human has read this.
- The dimension 16 is a local statement (Jacobian rank at random points of the four linear subspaces); the 5 quadrics define the locus implicitly and its global geometry is open. The matrices C^Gamma, E^Gamma are Q(zeta) elements with several terms, logged as F_p residues together with the Q(zeta) coefficients of the original m_1 m_1 monomials from which they are built; kappa is exact as stated.
- The colour-mixing vertex CohFT M.omega is validated by the string and dilaton equations, the M-independence of the total, the fitted polynomial structure, and the referee's independent recomputation (fresh Q(zeta) code, no project imports: conifold periods, rows, graphs 1 and 4) of all weight-0 and weight-1 polar coefficients of graphs 1 and 4, including the colour-mixing leg, kappa-leaf and inner-edge terms at genus 1 and 2, all equal to the log. The N-ring cross-checks cover diagonal M only. Graphs 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 and all weight-2 coefficients (m_2 and off-diagonal m_1 m_1 parts of s^{-2}) rest on the project's own validation; the referee redid the Fourier and rank analysis of them exactly over Q(zeta) from the logged data (ranks 3 and 1, kappa, the 126 matrix entries by residue, the 5 reduced quadrics and the local dimension 7), not from the rows.
- fq_fp.ratrec at 2^61-1 reconstructs only denominators below ~1.07e7; the entries with denominators 15360000 print as residues at that prime and are compared by residues in block (6). The random-M graph lines of the log print only the polar orders (kmax = -1); the absence of an s^0 term there is formatting, not a zero.
- Not a bug of an existing module, but noted: fq_graphs.Decorated's Hodge pruning must not be used for colour-mixing vertex CohFTs; fq_msum enumerates its own decorations with psi-deficit.
- Referee pass (AI, fresh code): verdict sound with fixes, no major item. Minor items applied here: the genus bound for reaching a u-series coefficient (genus 3: constant term only; polar first at genus 4), the local character of the dimension 16 and the implicit description of the quadric locus, the explicit statement about m_1^3 and m_1 m_2, the scope of the validation of M.omega, and the off-by-one in the precision comment of fq_sser.py (val Q_{2,0} = +2, bound O(s^(prec_abs - 12)); docstring corrected, no code change). The referee's stronger statement, invariance of every u-series coefficient including the constant term, was added to s.2 and verified numerically (s.3). No OPEN items.
