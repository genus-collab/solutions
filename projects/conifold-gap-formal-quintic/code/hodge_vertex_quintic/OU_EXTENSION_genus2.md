## 1. The hatted theory: every graph term is u^{-2} times a u-series

Notation: s = 1/L, u = s^5 (conifold coordinate), zhat = z/s^4; normalised canonical basis e~_i = Delta_i^{1/2} e_i with Delta_i^{1/2} = zeta^{4i} x, x = s f_0(u); rows Q[a][i][n] = [z^n] eta(H^a, R(z) e~_i) with val_s Q_{a,n} = -4n + c_a - 1, c = (0,-1,3,2,1); eta^{ab} = (1/5) delta_{a+b = 3 mod 5}, and c_a + c_b = 2 on those pairs.

**Hatting.** Rescale the flat basis Hhat^a := s^{1-c_a} H^a and the Givental variable z = s^4 zhat. eta is preserved on the eta-pairs (0,3), (1,2), (4,4). The rows become

    Qhat[a][i][n] := Q[a][i][n] s^{4n+1-c_a},

the TFT becomes Deltahat_i^{1/2} = zeta^{4i} xhat with xhat := x/s = varpi^cf_0(u), and since 1 = s^{-1} Hhat^0 the unit is 1hat = s 1 with components Deltahat_k^{-1/2} = s Delta_k^{-1/2}: the CohFT rescaling Omega_{g,n} -> s^{-(2g-2+n)} Omega_{g,n} (referee's derivation). Verified at both frames and both primes: all 175 rows (n <= 6) and xhat are pure u-series (only s^{5k} coefficients) of valuation 0 with non-zero constant term, Qhat|_{u=0} = the conifold symbol R^_{a,n}^{(i)} (fq_symbol.Symbol.Rh) for all (a, i, n), xhat|_{u=0} = 1; minimal s-precision 22, so u^0..u^4 are known.

**The valuation count.** For a decoration of a genus-g graph (E edges, m kappa-leaves, half-edge exponents a_h, leaf orders d_l, vertex psi-deficits D_v) the hatted factors are Ehat'(m,n) = s^{4(m+n+1)} E'(m,n), That'_d = s^{4d-3} T'_d and hatted vertex = s^{4 D_v - (2g_v-2+n_v+m_v)} x genuine vertex, with Sum_v (3g_v-3+n_v+m_v) = 3g-3-E+m = Sum_e (a_h+a_h') + Sum_l d_l + Sum_v D_v; the total s-exponent is 4 Sum D_v - (2g-2+m) + 4(3g-3-E+m-Sum D_v) + 4E + 4m - 3m = 10(g-1), i.e. 10 at g = 2, for EVERY decoration and every M(zhat) constant in u (the FORMAL_QUINTIC s.3 count -10(g-1), read in the other direction). Hence fq_msum.GraphSummer run on the duck-typed HatRows (Q = Qhat, x = xhat; the same role as fq_leadsolve.FrozenRows, which is HatRows at u = 0) with any symplectic M whose coefficient matrices are read as [zhat^k] (i.e. M(z) = Sum_k m_k s^{-4k} z^k) returns

    G_Gamma(u) := s^{10} T_Gamma  in Q(zeta)[[u]],

and the genuine graph term is T_Gamma = u^{-2} G_Gamma(u): T_Gamma is REGULAR at the conifold point iff [u^0] G_Gamma = [u^1] G_Gamma = 0. In t_c = u (1 + c_1 u + c_2 u^2 + ...) (c_1 = 7/10, c_2 = 41/75 at alpha = beta = 0) exact series reversion gives

    T_Gamma = a_0 t_c^{-2} + (a_1 + 2 c_1 a_0) t_c^{-1} + (a_2 + c_1 a_1 + (2 c_2 - c_1^2) a_0) + ...,   a_k = [u^k] G_Gamma.

HatRows keeps the rows as Laurent series in s, so the u-series property of every row and sum is CHECKED at runtime (is_u_series), not assumed; the count itself is used by the code and was re-derived by hand by the referee (s.7).

**Checks validating HatRows** (both frames, both primes): (a) s^{-10} x (hatted sums at M = 1) = the point-frame s-series sums GraphSummer(SRows, 1) through s^10, all seven graphs; (b) [u^0] of the hatted sums at M = 1 = the frozen sums 1807/3072000, -811/256000, -57/64000, 843/1024000, 3/51200, 87/32000, 9/12800 (total 1/1200), and at two random symplectic M = fq_leadsolve.frozen_sums; (c) the hatted sums at random M are pure u-series. One all-graph hatted sum costs 0.06-0.1 s.

## 3. The two linear conditions and their solutions

**The O(u) correction.** The general symplectic M(zhat; u) with M|_{u=0} = M_0 is M = M_0 exp(uN) + O(u^2), N = nu_1 zhat + nu_2 zhat^2 + nu_3 zhat^3, nu_1, nu_3 symmetric, nu_2 antisymmetric (40 parameters; fq_leadsolve.params_to_mats conventions): the RIGHT placement. Equivalently M = exp(uN') M_0 with N = M_0^{-1} N' M_0: the LEFT placement. Every genus-2 graph term is a polynomial in the entries of the coefficient matrices of M of total M-weight Sum_e (a+a'+1) + Sum_l (d-1) + Sum_v D_v = 3g-3 = 3 (weight k for [zhat^k]), and [zhat^k] exp(eps N) has eps-degree <= k, so the frozen coefficient G^0_Gamma[M_0 exp(eps N)] and the full hatted sum G_Gamma[M_0 exp(eps N)](u) are polynomials of degree <= 3 in eps. Verified: interpolation from eps = 0..9 over F_p, the coefficients of eps^4..eps^9 vanish for all 40 directions x 7 graphs, maximal degree seen 3, constant terms (1/1200, 0, ..., 0). Hence, exactly,

    [u^1] G_Gamma[M_0 exp(uN)] = g1_Gamma + D_N L_Gamma,    D_N L_Gamma := d/d eps|_0 G^0_Gamma[M_0 exp(eps N)],

and the full term with M = M_0 exp(uN) through any u-order is P_Gamma(eps; u)|_{eps = u}. The matrices M_0 exp(eps N), exp(eps N') M_0 and their inverses are built mod zhat^4 and wrapped in the duck-typed RawSympM (.R, .p, .M, .Minv, .is_identity = False; symplecticity asserted), the only attributes rows_prime, EdgeLeaf and MVertex use.

**The 2 x 40 matrix J.** J[Gamma][v] := D_{N_v} L_Gamma over the 40 basis directions. The rows for graphs 3, 5, 6, 7 vanish identically (as the O(u^E) argument requires); Sum_Gamma J[Gamma] = 0 (the total is M-independent), so J[1] = -(J[2] + J[4]); the 2 x 40 matrix with rows 2, 4 has rank 2: the two equations are independent on the full class. Every entry was reconstructed in Q(zeta) via Galois (sigma_m J_v = sgn J_{pi_m v}); 30 non-zero entries for graph 2 (no off-diagonal nu_3 entries), 40 for graph 4; full rows in the log (diagonal samples: J[2]: m1_ii = -zeta^i/400, m3_ii = -zeta^{3i}/48; J[4]: m1_00 = 1/3200, m3_00 = 1/1152). The nu_3 columns coincide with the m3 coefficients of the stored leading polynomials L_Gamma (symbol_result.json; L_Gamma is linear in mu_3). Cross-check: with N = dexp(Y) = Y - [mu, Y]/2 + [mu, [mu, Y]]/6 mod zhat^4 (so that exp(mu + eps Y) = exp(mu) exp(eps N) + O(eps^2); verified numerically), J . coords(dexp(Y_e)) equals the mu-gradient of the stored L_Gamma at the tautological point, 7 graphs x 40 directions, 0 failures at both primes. This is a consistency check against the 991-monomial fit; the independent confirmation is the referee's finite-difference J (s.7).

**The system.** All six edge graphs are regular at genus 2 iff

    J[2] . n = -g1_2 = -1/750,     J[4] . n = -g1_4 = 13/8000.

Classes (RIGHT: M = M_0 exp(uN) with N in the class; LEFT: M = exp(uN') M_0 with N' in the class, N = M_0^{-1} N' M_0 checked to lie in sp):

**(i) Covariant scalar**, nu_1 = b_1 diag(zeta^{-i}), nu_3 = b_3 diag(zeta^{-3i}), nu_2 = 0 (2 unknowns). Right placement:

    graph 2:  -(1/80)  b_1 - (5/48)   b_3 = -1/750
    graph 4:   (1/640) b_1 + (5/1152) b_3 = 13/8000        =>  (b_1, b_3) = (113/75, -21/125).

Left placement:

    graph 4:  -(1/1440) b_1 + (5/1152) b_3 = 13/8000       =>  (b_1, b_3) = (-113/75, 2/15).

Both 2 x 2 matrices are rational and invertible (rank 2, independent equations), the solutions unique and rational (also checked by hand). The b_3 coefficients agree between the placements (the zhat^3 part of N is unaffected by Ad(M_0) at this order), the b_1 coefficients differ; the right and left covariant lines span different N-spaces (combined rank 3). Interpretation: in the right placement this is exactly

    M = M_0(zhat) exp(u (b_1 L^4 z diag(zeta^{-i}) + b_3 L^12 z^3 diag(zeta^{-3i}))) = M_0(zhat) exp(b_1 zeta^{-i} z/L + b_3 zeta^{-3i} L^7 z^3),

**(ii) Diagonal**, nu_1 = diag(x_i), nu_3 = diag(y_i), nu_2 = 0 (10 unknowns); Fourier modes X_a = Sum_i zeta^{ai} x_i, Y_a likewise. **(iii) Circulant** (Z/5-covariant full N, fq_leadsolve.class_circulant: c_1(0..2), c_2(1..2), c_3(0..2), 8 unknowns). **(iv) Full** (40 unknowns). Rank 2, consistent, affine solution space of dimension 38 in both placements, left = right as sets (Ad(M_0) invertible, combined rank 40); the covariant solution is a particular solution.

## 5. The conifold-frame ambiguity (alpha, beta) and what it means

varpi^cf_0 = f_0 + alpha g + beta f_2 is not fixed by the pairing (FORMAL_QUINTIC s.1; fq_conifold.Conifold(R, Nu, alpha, beta)). At (alpha, beta) = (1/3, -2/7): varpi^cf_0 = 1 + (1/3) u - (11/210) u^2 - (6574/39375) u^3 + ..., t_c = u + (11/30) u^2 + (751/1575) u^3 + ... (c_1 = 7/10 - alpha = 11/30). The SYMBOL is independent of (alpha, beta): all R^_{a,n}^{(i)}, n <= 6, are identical, hence M_0 and J are unchanged (M_0 recomputed; one J column re-derived from the new rows as a control). xhat changes at O(u) (xhat_1 = alpha), and 140 of the 175 Qhat change at u^1 (referee's count): alpha and beta do move the rows at the order that matters.

What changes and what does not:

- Point frame (M = 1): the t_c^{-2} coefficients are unchanged; the t_c^{-1} coefficients change for graphs 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 (to -2371/3840000, -147/64000, -17/153600, 317/160000, 67/64000) and NOT for graphs 1 and 4 (-791/15360000, 743/15360000); the total stays (1/1200) t_c^{-2} + 0 t_c^{-1}, its constant 31/30000 -> -313/60000 (total u-series 1/1200 - (11/18000) u - (8581/1512000) u^2 - ...). The referee saw the same at (-5/2, 3) and (2, 1/5): exactly the two TREE graphs 1 and 4 (no cycle) keep their point-frame t_c^{-1} coefficient, every graph with a cycle changes. Pattern only; no explanation offered.
- With M_0: g1_2 = 1/750 and g1_4 = -13/8000 are UNCHANGED, g1_3 = g1_5 = g1_6 = g1_7 = 0 persist, and g1_1 shifts by exactly 2 alpha/1200: -7/8000 -> -23/72000 (Sum g1 = -2 c_1/1200 = -11/18000). The per-graph 1/t_c coefficients with M_0 (7/24000, 1/750, 0, -13/8000, 0, 0, 0) are unchanged; the vertex constant term changes 59/480000 -> 317/1440000 (its u^2 coefficient 67/288000 -> -2089/6048000).
- Consequently the covariant-scalar solutions are frame-independent: (113/75, -21/125) right and (-113/75, 2/15) left at both frames; the restricted matrices, ranks (2) and solution dimensions (0, 8, 6, 38) of all four classes are identical. The eps -> u verification passes at the new frame (edge graphs O(u^2); vertex [u^1] = -11/18000, 1/t_c coefficient 0; vertex u^2 coefficient 475621/2016000 right, 29626181/90720000 left).
- The referee extended this to (alpha, beta) = (-5/2, 3) (c_1 = 16/5) and (2, 1/5) (c_1 = -13/10): symbol, M_0, J, g1_2, g1_4 and both covariant solutions unchanged, g1_1 = -7/8000 + 2 alpha/1200, direct M(u) verification passing. Frame independence of the polar data stands on four frames, not on an argument.

What it means. The task anticipated that the O(u) data might depend on (alpha, beta); the polar data do not: g1_2, g1_4, J, the solution set and in particular (b_1, b_3) are frame-independent although alpha and beta change 140 rows at u^1. Had (b_1, b_3) depended on the frame, the numbers would have been artefacts of the choice of varpi^cf_0 and only the frame-by-frame existence of the 38-dimensional family would have survived; the observed independence is a genuine cancellation. Only the polar data are invariant: the vertex constant term, the total's constant and all u^2 and higher coefficients are not.

## 6. Relation to the earlier scans; what is still open

FORMAL_QUINTIC s.3 found every edge graph singular with the maximal pole t_c^{-2} in every frame tried; SCALAR_SCAN and DIAG_SCAN: no constant or q-dependent scalar (covariant or diagonal) removes the leading pole of all edge graphs; SYMPLECTIC_SCAN: no constant colour-mixing M reaches any u-series coefficient at genus 2; CONIFOLD_SYMBOL: the structural solution of the leading problem is M_0 = R~ mod zhat^4, and no diagonal zhat-constant M_0 solves it. The present note completes the picture at genus 2: once the leading pole is removed by the colour-mixing M_0, the remaining simple poles of the two one-edge graphs are removed by a q-dependent correction of M at relative order u, and within the covariant scalar class this correction is unique, rational and frame-independent. Read in the right placement, the honest analogue of the original Hodge-frame question has a positive answer at genus 2: M_0(zhat) times the q-dependent covariant scalar exp(b_1 zeta^{-i} z/L + b_3 zeta^{-3i} L^7 z^3), (b_1, b_3) = (113/75, -21/125), regularises all six edge graphs. The scalar factor alone (any scalar, any q-dependence) cannot, because the symbol is colour-mixing (CONIFOLD_SYMBOL s.1); M_0 is indispensable.

Sub-leading orders. Regularity of a genus-2 graph term is a statement about [u^0] and [u^1] only; the O(u^2) part of M is irrelevant for regularity at genus 2 and is not constrained here (the u^2 and higher coefficients of every graph depend on it, on the choice of N and on the placement). This is also why nothing beyond two linear equations appears: at genus 2 the system is exactly solvable and its solution set has codimension 2 in sp.

Open: (1) genus >= 3, where the pole order per decoration is t_c^{2-2g} (FORMAL_QUINTIC s.3), more u-orders must cancel, the M-weight rises to 3g-3 and more graphs enter; nothing here bears on it. (2) A geometric meaning of M_0 (not a QRR/Gamma constant, CONIFOLD_SYMBOL s.1) and of N, in particular of 113/75 and -21/125 (or -113/75 and 2/15); whether they, or the 38-dimensional family, admit a description such as the conifold vertex class of the local models is not addressed. (3) The vertex graph's constant term and its leafless / leaf split, which depend on the O(u^2) part of M and on the frame. (4) The frame independence of the tree graphs' point-frame t_c^{-1} coefficients.
