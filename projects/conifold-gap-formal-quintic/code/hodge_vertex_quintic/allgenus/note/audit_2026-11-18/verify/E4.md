## 3. The individual points

### 3.1 Flatness (b) [L5-3, U01-4] -- REAL (declaration/pin defect); fact standard and pinnable

What the pinned statements give:
- Teleman v3 Thm (2) in (1.15) (tel.txt l.578-580): flat-vacuum CohFTs on a fixed complex semisimple Frobenius
  algebra A form the orbit of T_z Sp_L^+ T_z^{-1} of I_A. Existence of some E (hence R) at ONE point; nothing about
  the dependence on the point, nothing about which E.
- GJR18 Sec 4.1 / Thm 4.1 (1812.11908.txt l.1316-1400): "there exists ... an R-matrix R_tau(z) in End(H) (x)
  Q(lambda)[[q,z]] satisfying the symplectic condition", and the graph sum. No flatness.
- GJR18 "(17), in the proof of Lemma 5.2": the constant C_alpha of the Birkhoff factorisation, i.e. the q^0 value.

Whether (b) is a "convention": no. Givental's convention fixes which object is called R (S = Psi R e^{U/z}, legs
R^{-1}(psi), sign of z). That the R with Omega = R.omega (unique by Teleman's classification) is the flat one is a
theorem (Givental's conjecture, proved by Teleman Sec 8; for this theory GJR18 Lemma 5.2). If (b) failed for the
R of the CohFT, def:set-rows would be void and lem:gau-colrows, thm:gau-R0 (iii), thm:con-glue would not apply.

Exact statements that give (b):
1. GJR18 v1, Lemma 5.2, eq. (16) (1812.11908.txt l.1954-1985): for R = R_tau of Sec 4.1 / Thm 4.1 at
   tau = (I_1/I_0)H, (z q d/dq + L_alpha) R_{i alpha-bar} = sum_j A_i^j R_{j alpha-bar}, A = tau-dot* .
   Translation (checked): R_{i alpha-bar} = (e-bar_alpha, R^* H^i) = eta(H^i, R e-bar_alpha) = Q^{(alpha)}_i;
   sum_j A_i^j R_{j alpha-bar} = eta(A H^i, R e-bar_alpha), A = C_1 H*, H*H^i = kappa_i H^{i+1}; L_alpha = zeta^alpha L
   at lambda = 1. So (16) is literally prop:set-cyclicLR (iii), (z D + zeta^alpha L) Q_i = C_1 kappa_i Q_{i+1}, which is
   all that the note uses of (b). (The square-root branch of Delta^{1/2} only rescales a column; (16) is linear.)
   Its proof (Birkhoff factorisation S C_alpha^{-1} = e^{u_alpha/z} R, "by the results of [16,12]" = Giv01, CG)
   also gives (17), the q^0 value: the same R satisfies (b) and (QRR).
2. GJR17, Sec 4.2, (12)-(13) (1709.07392.txt l.1561-1590): R is defined from the asymptotic fundamental solution
   S~ = Psi^{-1} R e^{U/z} of the QDE (10); Lemma 4.6, (19)-(20) gives its q=0 value; GJR17 Sec 5 applies
   "Givental-Teleman's reconstruction theorem" to this R.
3. General form: Teleman v3, Def 7.1 and Prop 7.2 (shift family uZ over a possibly formal neighbourhood U of 0 in A),
   Prop 7.7 (7.7.a) with Prop 8.2 (ii) (F = E_u Pi_u exp(-u/z) satisfies dF/dv = -(v._u)/z F), Prop 8.4 (solutions
   unique up to right multiplication by exp(H_1 z + H_3 z^3 + ...), constant diagonal), Prop 8.7 (ii) (flat-vacuum
   CohFTs over a fixed semisimple pointed Frobenius manifold are T_z^{-1} E exp h(z) T_z(I_A) with a FIXED E and
   constant odd h). [Later arXiv/journal numbering: Prop 7.5, 8.1, 8.3, 8.5.]
4. Givental math/0008067v4: Sec 1, Proposition (a)-(c) (asymptotic solution (4) S = (R_0 + zR_1 + ...) e^{U/z},

### 3.2 Constant-fixing [L5-3] -- covered

The only freedom left by (b) is R -> R diag(c_i(z)), c_i constant (Teleman Prop 8.4 / Giv01 Prop (c)); the note
proves the uniqueness it needs itself (lem:gau-wkb (ii): colour-i amplitudes with A_0 = L differ by k(z), constant;
the column is determined by row 0 through the forward relations). The constants are fixed by (QRR), and (QRR) is
declared for the R of the CohFT (GJR18 (17) is stated for R_tau; Giv01 Thm 1 + Remark for the R of Givental's
formula). Nothing beyond (b) + (QRR) is needed. (Note: Teleman Prop 8.7 (ii) is for families over a pointed Frobenius
manifold; q=0 is the large-radius limit, not such a point, so the constants must come from (QRR), as the note does.)

### 3.3 Coefficient ring, log-free [X5-6] -- refuted as a gap

(i) Declared: def:set-rows l.9 with GJR18 Sec 4.1, which literally displays R_tau(z) in End(H) (x) Q(lambda)[[q,z]].
(ii) Not even needed as an input. lem:gau-wkb (ii) holds verbatim over Bbbk[[q]][l] (D l = 1): its proof uses only
ker T^w_1 = Bbbk L, i.e. that the D-constants are Bbbk; on Bbbk[[q]][l], if sum_k a_k l^k is D-constant with top
degree K >= 1, then a_K in Bbbk and D a_{K-1} = -K a_K is a non-zero constant, impossible since D(Bbbk[[q]]) is in
q Bbbk[[q]]. Hence A^act = A^can D_0^{-1} is log-free whenever R satisfies (b) over Q(zeta)[[q]][l] (or any
differential extension with constants Q(zeta)). Same argument for full columns (formal-exponential argument:
Y_ij e^{(u_j-u_i)/z} constant forces Y_ij = 0 for i != j, as D(u_j-u_i) = (zeta^j-zeta^i)L is a unit).

### 3.4 All-genus divisor equation [U02-1, X5-6] -- real, minor in effect

Statement used: for 2g-2+n > 0 and the twisted classes Omega_{g,n,d} of def:set-fq,
pi_* Omega_{g,n+1,d}(gamma_1..gamma_n, H) = d Omega_{g,n,d}(gamma_1..gamma_n) (pi forgets the last point; the d = 0
term pushes forward to 0); equivalently the H-shifted family at t = cH equals the family with Q -> Q e^c.
Where: only in the parenthetical of thm:set-GT (s02_setup_5 l.42). No deduction of the note uses it, because (T) is
stated directly at Q = e^{T(q)}. But it is needed to obtain that form of (T) from the sources: the flatness statements
(Teleman Def 7.1/Prop 7.7: shift families; GJR18 Omega^{lambda,tau}, Lemma 5.2) concern the tau-shifted theory, and
"the theory at t = T(q)H with Q = 1" has a meaning only through this equation (T contains log q; same remark as U01
F5 in genus 0). (G0) covers genus 0 only. Standard: the K-class E_{g,n+1,d} = ft^* E_{g,n,d} (CG Sec 1, set-up of
Theorem 1, and its Appendix 1), the virtual class is pulled back along ft (Behrend's forgetting-tails axiom), and
int_d H = d; for the formal quintic it is used as "the divisor equation in Gromov-Witten theory" in LP20 Sec 6.2
(proof of Theorem 2, first equation, just before (35)). Repair: add it to (T) (or to (G0) as "(b') in all genera"), or delete "by the divisor equation" and
pin (T) to a statement made at Q = e^{T(q)}.

### 3.5 Teleman's hypotheses [L5-5] -- covered by (T) as pinned via GJR18 Sec 4.1; routine

Teleman: CohFT (all genera) with flat vacuum on a complex semisimple Frobenius algebra; families = shift families
over (formal) neighbourhoods of a point. Here: a CohFT over Q(zeta)[[Q]], family in the Novikov direction, base
q = 0 not a point of the Frobenius manifold along t = TH. The all-genus CohFT axioms and the flat unit for the twisted
classes (same mechanism: E_{g,n+1,d} = ft^*E_{g,n,d}, splitting via the normalisation sequence) and the applicability
of Givental-Teleman are asserted in GJR18 Sec 4.1 ("The (tau-shifted) lambda-twisted CohFT is semi-simple. Hence ...
the Givental-Teleman reconstruction theorem applies"), which is part of the (T) pin; semisimplicity over Q(zeta)[[q]]
is proved in the note (cor:set-cyclicPF5 (a)). The note uses these hypotheses only through (T). Independent routes
exist: (i) K = Q(zeta)((q)) embeds in C; Teleman over C plus uniqueness of R and Galois descent (C^{Aut(C/K)} = K)
give R over K; flatness in D = q d/dq then follows from Teleman's Sec 7 computation, which is algebraic, applied to
the derivation D, using D Omega = pi_* Omega(..., H) (3.4); (ii) Givental's Theorem 1 (generic lambda, Novikov
variable formal) with Coates-Givental Thm 1 and GJR17 Lemma 4.6 for the specialisation. Should be said in (T).

## 4. Is "Theorem A (and B, C) holds modulo the declared inputs" literally true?

Not as worded. The Conditionality paragraphs (s01_intro_3 l.19, s10_main l.33) list "the value (QRR)" and
"Teleman's reconstruction (T)" pinned to Teleman Thm (2) in (1.15) and GJR18 Thm 4.1; with these pins, (b) is
used on the chains of A, B and C but is neither proved nor contained in a pinned statement (it is called a

that omit (b) are: prop:set-cyclicLR "Assume (M) and (G0)" (s02_setup_2 l.7; (iii) needs (b)); cor:set-cyclicPF5
"Assume (M) and (G0)" (s02_setup_3 l.5; last sentence of (b)); thm:con-glue proof, last sentence (s08_connection_8
l.19: "the actual rows with their WKB form, which rest on (M) and (G0)"); s01_intro_3 l.17 ("Sec 3 uses no quoted
input beyond the genus-zero data of Sec 2").
Read generously (taking def:set-rows (a)-(b), thm:set-qrr and thm:set-GT together as the content of (QRR)+(T)), the
declared package does contain everything used; the defect is that this package is stronger than its pins and is
mislabelled. All missing facts are standard and pinned in Sec 3 above. Nothing downstream changes.
