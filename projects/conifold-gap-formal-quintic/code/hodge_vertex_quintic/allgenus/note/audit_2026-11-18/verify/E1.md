# E1: eq:con-bz in Theorem P(a) proved "factor by factor" outside the ring it names

Status: COMPLETE.

**Verdict: real-minor.** eq:con-bz is true. The factor-by-factor proof is correct once it is carried
out in the right ring. That ring is a polynomial extension of A_L(zeta)[[t]]((z)) by formal logarithm
symbols. The translation T_z = exp(z d/dt) is a ring endomorphism of it. The change of base point of
the Stirling series is an intertwining T_z o eps = eps o tau_theta of continuous ring homomorphisms,
checked on generators. Section 3 writes the repair out in full; it uses only lem:fsp-stirfe. The other
points (U13-1/10, U14-2/3, U14-5) are wording or constant-term slips that lem:fsp-const or a renamed
intermediate ring closes. Theorem P(a) (i)-(iv) stands. Nothing downstream (Theorem P(b), the glue,
thm:gam-D, Theorem C and through it A and B) is affected. The earlier classification "minor" is right.

## 2. The text under review

s08_connection_4.tex l.74-79 (proof of thm:con-Pa (i), Step 3). With a := (sigma^5-1) A_St/A_St(L,0)
and b_z := exp((S(L+t+z)-S(L+t))/z) a(L+t+z), the claim eq:con-bz is

    b_z = q prod_{k=1}^5 (5 sigma + k z) A_St/A_St(L,0)   in A_L[[t]][[z]],  sigma = L + t.

The text says it proves this "in A_L(zeta)[[t]][[z]], factor by factor". The six factors are
e^{sigma log q/z}, Gst(1+5 sigma/z) and Gst(1+(sigma-zeta^j)/z)^{-1}. Under sigma -> sigma+z they are
multiplied by q, prod_k(5 sigma/z+k) and z/(sigma+z-zeta^j).

The objections hold, and are only about how the step is written:
* None of the six factors or their translates lies in A_L(zeta)[[t]][[z]]. Each contains log z,
  log q, log(5L) and log(L - zeta^j), and three of them carry these symbols with a factor 1/z.
* Two of the multipliers are not in that ring either: prod_k(5 sigma/z + k) has a factor z^{-5}, and
  "multiplying by q" means e^{log q} = q.
* The text silently identifies two expansions. One is the Taylor translate under sigma -> sigma+z of
  the Stirling expansion based at 5 sigma, resp. sigma - zeta^j. The other is the Stirling expansion
  of Gst(Y + integer) under the conventions of def:fsp-stirling, i.e.
  log(Y+theta) := log Y + log(1+theta/Y), with mu(Y+theta) expanded in 1/Y.
* What is literally correct: the substitution points 1/Y = z/(5 sigma + k z) and
  1/Y = z/(sigma + z - zeta^j) do lie in z A_L(zeta)[[t]][[z]]. So the functional equations used do
  hold there.

## 3. Rigorous version of the factor-by-factor step

Notation (local): k := A_L(zeta) = Q(zeta)[L, L^{-1}, (L^5-1)^{-1}]. In k the elements 5L and
L - zeta^j are units, since (L-zeta^j)^{-1} = prod_{i != j}(L - zeta^i)/(L^5-1). Put W := 1/Y.
mu(Y) := sum_k b_k W^{2k-1}. All logarithms of elements of 1 + (t,z)R[[t,z]] are the usual series.
Over a Q-algebra R, log and exp are mutually inverse group isomorphisms between
1 + (t,z)R[[t,z]] and (t,z)R[[t,z]]. In particular log(xy) = log x + log y.

**(R1) The target ring and the translation.**
* Let 𝔅 := k[[t]]((z))[ell, lam_N, lam_0, ..., lam_4], a polynomial ring in seven indeterminates. They
  stand for log z, log(5L) and log(L - zeta^j).
* T_z : k[[t]] -> k[[t,z]], f(t) -> f(t+z) = sum_n f^{(n)}(t) z^n/n!, is a ring homomorphism (Leibniz)
  with T_z(z^n k[[t,z]]) contained in z^n k[[t,z]].
* It extends to a ring endomorphism of k[[t]]((z)) (fixing z, z-adically continuous), and to 𝔅 by
  fixing the seven indeterminates.
* It commutes with exp and log on (t,z)k[[t,z]], resp. on 1 + (t,z)k[[t,z]].
* It is the operation f -> f(c+t+p), p = z, of lem:fsp-transl (b).

**(R2) The Stirling symbol ring and its shifts.**
* Let 𝔖 := Q((W))[ell_Y] (ell_Y stands for log Y).
* For theta in Q let tau_theta be the ring endomorphism W -> W/(1+theta W),
  ell_Y -> ell_Y + log(1+theta W). It is well defined because W/(1+theta W) has W-valuation 1.
* tau_theta tau_theta' = tau_{theta+theta'}. On W:
  (W/(1+theta W))/(1+theta' W/(1+theta W)) = W/(1+(theta+theta')W).
* On ell_Y: log(1+theta W) + log(1+theta' W/(1+theta W)) = log(1+(theta+theta')W).
* tau_theta(Y) = Y + theta, and tau_1(mu) is "mu(Y+1) expanded in 1/Y" of lem:fsp-stirfe.

**(R3) Iterated functional equation.**
* Put G := (Y + 1/2) ell_Y - Y + mu(Y), which is log Gst(1+Y) - (1/2) log 2pi under def:fsp-stirling.
* Direct expansion gives tau_1 G - G = ell_Y + log(1+W) + rho(Y), with rho as in lem:fsp-stirfe. So
  lem:fsp-stirfe (rho = 0) gives tau_1 G - G = tau_1(ell_Y).
* By (R2), for every integer m >= 1:
  tau_m G - G = sum_{i=0}^{m-1} tau_i(tau_1 G - G) = sum_{i=1}^{m} (ell_Y + log(1 + iW)).

**(R4) Change of base point (the tacit step, made explicit).**
* For an affine a(sigma) = alpha sigma + beta (alpha in Q^x, beta in k) with rho_0 := a(L) in k^x, and a
  symbol lam_a, define the ring homomorphism eps_a : 𝔖 -> 𝔅 by
  W -> z/a(L+t) = z rho_0^{-1}(1+alpha t/rho_0)^{-1}, and
  ell_Y -> lam_a + log(1 + alpha t/rho_0) - ell.
* eps_a is well defined: W goes to z times a unit of k[[t]]. So Q[[W]] -> k[[t]][[z]] is a continuous
  substitution, W is sent to a unit of k[[t]]((z)), and ell_Y is a free variable.
* **Claim: T_z o eps_a = eps_a o tau_alpha.** Both sides are ring homomorphisms. Both are continuous on
  Q[[W]] (W-adic to z-adic; T_z preserves z^n k[[t]][[z]] and tau_alpha preserves W^n Q[[W]]).
  k[[t]][[z]] is z-adically separated. So it suffices to check the generators:
  - On W: T_z(z/a(L+t)) = z/(a(L+t) + alpha z), and
    eps_a(W/(1+alpha W)) = (z/a)/(1 + alpha z/a) = z/(a + alpha z).
  - On ell_Y: T_z gives lam_a + log(1 + alpha(t+z)/rho_0) - ell. eps_a(tau_alpha ell_Y) gives
    lam_a + log(1+alpha t/rho_0) + log(1 + alpha z/(rho_0+alpha t)) - ell. These are equal because
    (1+alpha t/rho_0)(1+alpha z/(rho_0 + alpha t)) = 1 + alpha(t+z)/rho_0.
* This is exactly the consistency of the Stirling expansion under a change of base point. The manuscript's
  substitution points are its images: eps_N(tau_k W) = z/(5 sigma + kz) and
  eps_{D_j}(tau_1 W) = z/(sigma + z - zeta^j).

**(R5) The six factors and their total.**
* Use eps_N := eps_a for a = 5 sigma (lam_a = lam_N) and eps_j := eps_a for a = sigma - zeta^j
  (lam_a = lam_j). Put Lam_q := -5 lam_N + sum_j lam_j. This choice of "log q" makes L critical; it
  replaces the branch argument of lem:con-form (d).
* Define in 𝔅: F_0 := sigma Lam_q/z, F_N := eps_N(G), F_j := -eps_j(G).
* A direct count gives

      Phi := F_0 + F_N + sum_j F_j = 2 ell + S^nat/z + S_1^nat + sum_k z^{2k-1} S_{2k}(sigma),

  where:
  - S^nat := sigma Lam_q + 5 sigma Lam_N - sum_j (sigma-zeta^j) Lam_j and
    S_1^nat := (Lam_N - sum_j Lam_j)/2,
  - Lam_N := lam_N + log(1+t/L) and Lam_j := lam_j + log(1 + t/(L-zeta^j)),
  - the coefficient of ell/z, the terms linear in sigma/z and the coefficient of ell (= 2) are
    computed as in def:gam-c and lem:con-form (a).
* d_t S^nat = 5 log(1+t/L) - sum_j log(1+t/(L-zeta^j)) has no symbols, vanishes at t = 0, and has
  derivative S_0''(L+t). So for n >= 1, d_t^n S^nat is the Taylor series of S_0^{(n)} at L, and these
  lie in A_L.
* S_1^nat differs from the Taylor series of S_1 by a constant.
* Hence (T_z - 1)Phi is E + (T_z - 1) log a_0, an element of (t,z)A_L[[t,z]], where:
  - E := (T_z S^nat - S^nat)/z = sum_{n>=1} S_0^{(n)}(sigma) z^{n-1}/n! is the exponent of b_z,
  - log a_0 := S_1(L+t) - S_1(L) + sum_k z^{2k-1} S_{2k}(L+t), i.e. a_0 = A_St/A_St(L,0).
* Factor by factor, using additivity of T_z - 1, (R3) with m = 5 and m = 1, and (R4):
  - (T_z - 1)F_0 = Lam_q.
  - (T_z - 1)F_N = eps_N(tau_5 G - G) = sum_{k=1}^5 [Lam_N - ell + log(1 + kz/(5 sigma))]
    = 5(lam_N - ell) + sum_k log(1 + (5t+kz)/(5L)).
  - (T_z - 1)F_j = -eps_j(tau_1 G - G) = -(lam_j - ell) - log(1 + (t+z)/(L - zeta^j)).
* Summing: the symbols cancel. ell gives -5 + 5 = 0, and Lam_q + 5 lam_N - sum lam_j = 0. With
  prod_j(L-zeta^j) = L^5 - 1, prod_j(sigma+z-zeta^j) = (sigma+z)^5 - 1 and (L^5-1)/(5L)^5 = q this
  leaves

      (T_z - 1)Phi = log[ q prod_k (5 sigma + kz) / ((sigma+z)^5 - 1) ]   in k[[t,z]].

  The argument of the log has constant term q(5L)^5/(L^5-1) = 1.
* Both sides lie in A_L[[t,z]]. A_L[[t,z]] -> k[[t,z]] -> 𝔅 is injective, so the identity holds in
  A_L[[t,z]]. No Galois argument is needed.
* Exponentiate and multiply by ((sigma+z)^5-1) a_0 (a unit, constant term L^5 - 1). Since
  T_z(sigma^5 - 1) = (sigma+z)^5 - 1:
  b_z = e^E T_z((sigma^5-1)a_0) = q prod_k(5 sigma + kz) a_0. This is eq:con-bz.

The rest of Step 3 is unchanged: lem:fsp-transl (b) with p = z and a in A_L[[t]][[z]] (hypotheses
checked) gives c<q prod_k(5 sigma+kz)> = c<sigma^5-1>, and Step 2 gives PF5^L(L c) = 0.

Remarks.
* This version is purely formal. It needs neither complex L nor branches, nor lem:fsp-refl /
  lem:con-form (d): the definition of Lam_q replaces "log q chosen so that L is critical".
* The repair proposed in U13_A ("at fixed L in C, in C[[t]][[z]], the computation is literal") is not
  literal as stated: log z and the 1/z in front of logarithms still need formal treatment. With
  C[[t]]((z))[ell] as the ring and logarithms taken additively, it is the same argument as above.
* Minor wording in the text: "the terms in log z cancel before and after the shift". The coefficient of
  log z is 2 both before and after (the z^2 of def:gam-c), and T_z fixes it. What cancels is the
  (log z)/z term, and in the multipliers z^{-5} against z^5. Harmless.
