## 2. Cohomology tensored with the ring

The note's CohFT over a commutative Q-algebra A has Omega_{g,n} in H^*(Mbar_{g,n},Q) (x)_Q Hom_A(V^{(x)n},A).
H^*(Mbar_{g,n},Q) is finite-dimensional, so H^*(Mbar,Q) (x)_Q A is a free A-module of finite rank, products,
pull-backs, push-forwards along gluing/forgetful maps and integration are the A-linear extensions of the
Q-linear maps, and every Q-linear identity among them (projection formula, (8), Lemma 2.11, the excess-intersection
pull-back rule of Sec. 0.3, vanishing above the dimension) holds after (x) A. For A = Q[[u]], Q(zeta)[[q]],
Q(zeta)((s)) no completion issue arises: tensoring a finite-dimensional space commutes with forming series.
Every PPZ sum is finite for fixed (g,n) (finitely many graphs; psi nilpotent; translation sums bounded by degree),
so no convergence is involved, also for Laurent-series coefficients with unbounded pole order in z-degree
(e.g. M = M0(tau z/s^4)). The only extra hypothesis needed over a ring is that eta be *perfect* (invertible Gram
matrix), for eta^{-1}; it holds in every use (identity in the colour basis; 5 x permutation in the flat basis;
-1/5 identity in the TR frozen basis) -- this is L1's M2. No issue.

## 3. Assessment of the earlier reports

- **The manuscript's justification** (s02_setup_4 l.79: "identities between finite sums, polynomial in the
  coefficients; stated there over Q, they hold verbatim over every commutative Q-algebra"; s10_main_2 l.27: "the
  proofs of their Props. 2.4, 2.12 and 2.14, and of Props. 2.8 and 2.9 ... hold verbatim over Q(zeta)((s))").
  X5 is right that "polynomial in the coefficients" is not by itself a reason: the CohFT data are constrained
  (gluing, unit axioms; symplectic condition), and a universal statement true for the constrained Q-points need
  not hold for A-points (x^2+1=0 has no Q-points). What is true is (A) below: the proofs are formal; or (B):
  in the cases used, the constrained data admit a free polynomial parametrisation, which makes the polynomiality
  argument correct. The conclusion of the sentence is true.
- **X5's repair** ("embed the rings in C; the statements over C imply them over A") is incomplete as stated:
  PPZ state Def. 0.3 and all propositions over Q (V a Q-vector space, classes in H^*(Mbar,Q)), not over C, so
  the embedding reduces A to C but not to Q; one still needs (A) or (B). (Also N (x) Q(zeta) need not be a
  domain, but no PPZ proposition is used there, so this does not matter.)
- **L1-1** (proof of the quoted Prop. 2.12 uses Props. 2.3, 2.7, Lemma 2.11): confirmed. PPZ's proof of 2.12 starts
  "By Proposition 2.9, T_aR Omega and RT_b Omega are equal CohFTs" (so 2.9, and 2.3, 2.7 for "CohFTs"), uses the
  graph description from the *proof* of 2.9, and Sec. 2.3 announces that Lemma 2.11 is used implicitly; the proof of
  2.3 uses the pull-back rule of Sec. 0.3. For a theorem quoted over Q its internal dependencies need not be listed;
  they matter only for the claim that the *proofs* carry over to other rings. So: (i) the sentence of (Q)
  "Props. 2.4, 2.8 and 2.9 enter only through the proof of Prop. 2.14" (s01_intro_3 l.9) is inaccurate (2.9 also
  enters the proof of 2.12); (ii) the list at s10_main_2 l.27 of proofs claimed to hold verbatim is incomplete
  (missing 2.3, 2.7, Lemma 2.11, the Sec. 0.3 rule, and 2.9 inside 2.12). All omitted proofs are of the same formal
  kind (checked, Section 4 (A)). With repair (B) none of them is needed.

## 4. Correct justifications (both checked)

**(A) The proofs are formal.** I read the proofs of Props. 2.3, 2.4, 2.7, 2.8, 2.9, Lemma 2.11, Prop. 2.12 (with (8))
and Prop. 2.14 in PPZ v3. Each step is one of: (a) a Q-linear identity in H^*(Mbar,Q) (pull-back rule of Sec. 0.3 by
excess intersection; psi_l . xi_Gamma*(alpha) = xi_Gamma*(psi_l alpha); (8); Lemma 2.11 via f_*f^* = id;
psi = 0 on Mbar_{0,3}; vanishing of classes above the dimension), extended A-linearly; (b) an axiom of the input
Omega (S_n-invariance, gluing, unit, Omega_{0,3}(v,v',1) = eta(v,v')); (c) algebra in End_A(V)[[z]] and
(V(x)V)[[psi,psi']] using only R(0)=1 (R^{-1} exists), the symplectic condition, eta perfect, and division by
psi+psi' of a series vanishing at psi'=-psi (valid over any commutative ring: psi+psi' is a non-zero-divisor in
A[[psi,psi+psi']]); (d) rational coefficients 1/m!, 1/|Aut|, binomial counts. Nothing uses that Q is a field or a
basis of V over a field. Hence all hold over every commutative Q-algebra with V free of finite rank and eta perfect.

**(B) Universal-identity argument (needs only the PPZ statements over Q).** In every use (table in Section 1) the
input is a TFT omega_y on A^r, orthonormal basis e_i, (omega_y)_{g,n}(e_i^{(x)n}) = y_i^{-(2g-2+n)}, zero on mixed
colours, unit sum y_i e_i, y_i in A^x; and the R-matrices satisfy R(0)=1, R(-z)^T R(z)=1. Over any Q-algebra such
R are exactly R = exp(r), r = sum_{k>=1} r_k z^k with r_k^T = (-1)^{k+1} r_k (exp/log are mutually inverse power
series with rational coefficients; exp(r(-z)^T)exp(r(z)) = 1 iff r(-z)^T = -r(z)); sanity-checked exactly
(audit/work/E6/explog_check.py: 3x3, mod z^6, both directions). For fixed (g,n), the conclusions used -- the unit
axiom of R.omega_y with unit sum y_i e_i, and R_a.(R_b.omega_y) = (R_aR_b).omega_y with R_a.(.) formed with that unit
-- are, in a Q-basis of H^*(Mbar_{g,n},Q), finitely many identities between Laurent polynomials with Q-coefficients
in y^{+-1} and finitely many free coordinates of the r_k (only z-degrees <= 3g-2+n enter). For y in (Q^x)^r and
rational r the data are a CohFT with unit and symplectic R-matrices in PPZ's sense over Q, so PPZ Props. 2.12 and
2.14 *as stated* give the identities at every rational point; Q is infinite, so they are identities of Laurent
polynomials, and they hold at every A-point by specialisation, for every commutative Q-algebra A (domain or not,
field or not, Laurent series or not). Instances: A = Q(zeta)[vs^{+-1}] with y_i = zeta^i/vs, r = log M0(z/vs^4)
(and its specialisations); A = Q(zeta)((s)) with y_i = C(Delta_i^{-1/2}) and r = log M, log X. (The manuscript itself
uses this free-variable argument correctly for the TFT axioms of C(omega), s10_main_2 l.21.)

The general sentence of lem:set-pushins (iii) (Prop. 2.12 for arbitrary Omega over arbitrary A) is covered by (A);
its only application (cor:mai-gap, Omega = C(omega)) is covered by (B).
