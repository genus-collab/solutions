## Verdict: real-minor (the earlier "minor" classification is right)

All six points are real. Two statements are false as literally worded (lem:fsp-param (i) for subrings without Q;
lem:fsp-res (i) if the P_+- may have coefficients of negative order), several are stated for a wider class of
amplitudes than they are proved for (lem:fsp-cov, lem:fsp-cfu, lem:fsp-param (iv), cor:fsp-movebase, lem:fsp-dq,
lem:fsp-pg (iii) q-version), and one object (E_{zC} with a q-dependent C on R_n(A_0)[[q]]) is never defined. Each
is closed by a routine definition or a one-line restriction of scope, and EVERY downstream use lies inside what is
defined and proved on the page, in fact inside the restricted scope (q-free / eps-free amplitudes, power-series
amplitudes with z-order bounded below, P_+- over Q, Q-algebras). No main result (A, B, C, Gamma) is affected.

The key observation for U16-5 is that no downstream use ever feeds cor:fsp-movebase or lem:fsp-dq an amplitude
whose z-order is not bounded below uniformly in q: the amplitudes are p^m (lem:con-S2), (r/r(L))^{1/2}b with b in
A_sigma[[z]] (lem:fsp-generic (iii), used in Theorem P(a)(i)), and (x/a)^{-1} (lem:fsp-param (iv) inside
lem:fsp-stirling). For such amplitudes the right side of cor:fsp-movebase is the stationary phase of def:fsp-wick over
A = A_0[[q]], the left side uses the q-FREE functional at c_0 coefficientwise in q (literal), and the only
non-literal step is the exponential identity in R_n(A_0)[[q]], which holds modulo every q^N (below).

## U16-5 (U16 M1): E_{zC} with q-dependent C on R_n(A_0)[[q]] is undefined

Where: def:fsp-ring "Parameters" (a_formalsp.tex l.16); lem:fsp-pg (iii) last sentence (a_formalsp_2.tex l.69,
proof l.78); cor:fsp-movebase (a_formalsp_3.tex l.13-32: E_{-zH_c^{-1}}[P] for P in R_n(A_0)[[q]], l.27-31; S_q[F;b]
and SP_c[F;a] for a in A_0[[x-c_0]]((z))[[q]], l.17-19); lem:fsp-dq (l.49-60) "every amplitude a in
A_0[[x-c_0]]((z))[[q]]"; lem:fsp-param (iii),(iv) (l.40, 42).

Confirmed: "coefficientwise in q" is literal only for q-free covariance. For a = sum_d q^d z^{-d}, exp(F_>=3/z)a has
infinitely many terms of weight <= 0 with non-zero coefficient in A, so a is not an amplitude over A in the sense of
def:fsp-wick (neither clause), and S_q[F;a] in cor:fsp-movebase is undefined for it. The exponential identity
(a_formalsp_3.tex l.25) multiplies a weight-convergent exponential (e^{F_{c,>=3}/z}) and q-adically convergent ones
(e^{(F(c+t)-F_0(c_0+t))/z} contains q z^{-1} of weight -1), and lem:fsp-ring (ii) states rules only for the first kind.

Repair (definition, checked). Put B_N := A_0[q]/(q^N). Then R_n(A_0)[[q]]/(q^N) = R_n(B_N) (a finite union of finite
sets is finite). For a in A_0[[x-c_0]]((z))[[q]], a mod q^N lies in B_N[[x-c_0]]((z)) (finitely many q-coefficients,
each of bounded-below z-order), i.e. it IS an amplitude over B_N in the sense of def:fsp-wick. Define
  SPn_c[F;a] := lim_N SPn_{c mod q^N}[F mod q^N; a mod q^N]  in  lim_N B_N((z)) = A_0((z))[[q]],
and likewise E_{zC}[P] := lim_N E_{z(C mod q^N)}[P mod q^N] for P in R_n(A_0)[[q]]. Compatibility in N is
lem:fsp-univ (i) for the ring maps B_{N+1} -> B_N. Equivalently E_{zC}[sum_d q^d P_d] = sum_d q^d E_{zC}[P_d] (U16's
form). Properties checked:
 - On R_n(A) and on genuine amplitudes over A it agrees with def:fsp-ring / def:fsp-wick (the z^k-coefficient is the
   same finite sum over the weight-k monomials, reduced mod q^N).
 - It is additive, A_0((z))[[q]]-linear, q-adically continuous, commutes with d/dq when C is q-free.
 - Exponentials: modulo q^N, with Y of weight >= 1/2 and X'' nilpotent (X''^N = 0), (Y+X'')^k/k! is summable in
   R_n(B_N) (Y^i X''^j, j < N, has weight >= i/2 - N*const), and e^{Y+X''} = e^Y e^{X''} by the Cauchy product. So the
   identity of a_formalsp_3.tex l.25 holds mod every q^N: both exponents equal (F(c+t)-F_0(c_0)-t^T H_0 t/2)/z.
 - lem:fsp-pg (iii), q-version: lem:fsp-pg (i) over R = B_N, I = (q) nilpotent (complete), then the monomial argument
   of the printed proof; holds for all P in R_n(A_0)[[q]].
 - cor:fsp-movebase then holds verbatim for all a in A_0[[x-c_0]]((z))[[q]]; lem:fsp-transl (a) is literal (q-free
   functional); lem:fsp-dq follows as printed (d/dq commutes with the q-free functional at c_0).
Independent check (wolframscript, work/E2/movebase.wls): 1D, F_0 = -t^2/2+t^3/3+t^4/5, F_1 = q(t+t^2/2)+q^2 t^3, LHS of
cor:fsp-movebase coefficientwise vs S_q[F;a] with c(q) by Newton iteration, for a = 1+t+zt^2 (q-free) and
a = (1+t) sum_{d<=3} q^d z^{-d} (non-uniform z-order, extended definition): differences vanish for q^0..q^3,
z^{-3}..z^3. Negative control (Hessian prefactor dropped): [q^1 z^0] difference = 3/2.

Downstream uses (all within the restricted, literally defined case):
