## 2. The six points

### U09-6: DNOPS (2-33) misprinted -- REAL; as written: fixable (not just minor)
(a) The printed (2-33) is false for the model curve. For i != j the leading terms are B_00(c_i,c_j) on the left
and (R^{-1}_1)^i_j = -B_00 on the right (by (2-31)), B_00 = 1/((c_i-c_j)^2 (dw_i/dp)(dw_j/dp)) != 0; for i = j the
right side is not even a power series. Numerically (edge233.wl, kappa = 1, v = 1, total degree <= 3): residual of the
printed identity = 1 on every diagonal block, 0.11 / 0.29 off the diagonal.
(b) What the note says: s06_model_3.tex l.28 (proof of thm:mod-tr, Step 1) "by the sentence preceding [DNOPS
Thm 8.1] this curve satisfies (2-30)-(2-33)", and l.23 states the quoted theorem for "a compact curve ... if R is
symplectic and (2-32) holds", i.e. (2-33) is discharged by DNOPS's claim "always satisfied if the curve is compact".
Both source claims are, as printed, claims about a false identity. The correct global statement (Eynard App. B of
arXiv:1110.2949; LPSZ) is not among the declared inputs, and DOSS states only the local converse (Thm 4.1 DEFINES
the local curve by (4-1)). So, as written, the hypothesis (2-33) of the quoted theorem is justified only by a
false-as-printed citation: rule 4 "unjustified as written". (A lenient reading -- use only the conclusion
"stores the correlators of a CohFT via (2-35)" of the sentence before Thm 8.1 as a black box -- would make it a
mis-reference; the note does not argue that way, and the paraphrase at l.23 relies on the compact-curve claim.)
(c) Correct form (the PPZ edge of R; DOSS (4-1) with (3-2), (3-57); DOSS (3-61)-(3-62), (3-66)), in the
normalisation of DNOPS 2.6 with the formal double Laplace transform L_i(x)L_j (coefficientwise G_a(th1)G_b(th2) of
lem:mod-laplace), psi_a = th_a^{-1}, B^sing_i := dw_i dw_i'/(w_i-w_i')^2:
   (*)  (psi1+psi2) L_i(x)L_j[ B - delta_ij B^sing_i ] = delta_ij - sum_k R^{-1}(psi1)^i_k R^{-1}(psi2)^j_k .
(Remark: with the diagonal double pole regularised (analytic continuation, = contour value, gives
L(x)L[B^sing] = -1/(psi1+psi2)), the full-B version has NO delta and a MINUS sign; the printed (2-33) matches
neither. Either way the printed formula is wrong; (*) is the form the TR graph expansion uses, DOSS (3-51), (3-56).)
(d) Repair (proof of (*) for the model curve, any kappa; checked):
  Step 1. On P^1, D(B) := d_1(B/dx_1) + d_2(B/dx_2) = sum_k dxi^k(p_1) dxi^k(p_2).
  Fix p_2 generic. As a 1-form in p_1: no pole at p_1 = p_2 (with f = 1/x', the coefficient
  2(f(p_2)-f(p_1))/(p_1-p_2)^3 + (f'(p_1)+f'(p_2))/(p_1-p_2)^2 is regular, = f'''/6 on the diagonal);
  holomorphic at 0 and infinity (f = p^2/(kappa(p^5-v)) has a double zero at 0 and is O(p^{-3}) at infinity);
  at p_1 = c_k only the double pole -(1/x''(c_k)) dp_1 dp_2/((p_1-c_k)^2 (c_k-p_2)^2), which is the principal part
  of dxi^k(p_1)dxi^k(p_2) since dxi^k = (dp/dw_k)(c_k) dp/(c_k-p)^2 and (dp/dw_k)^2 = -1/x''(c_k). A holomorphic
  1-form on P^1 vanishes. (Checked exactly: residual 0 to 78 digits at random points, edge233.wl.)
  Step 2 (i != j). B is holomorphic at (c_i,c_j); lem:mod-laplace (i) in each variable (F = B/dx_1 has a simple
  pole, F dx_1 = B residue-free) gives L_i(x)L_j[D(B)] = -(th1+th2) L_i(x)L_j[B]; the right side of Step 1 gives
  sum_k L_i[dxi^k]L_j[dxi^k] = th1 th2 sum_k R^{-1}(psi1)^i_k R^{-1}(psi2)^j_k, as L_j[dxi^k] = -th R^{-1}(psi)^j_k
  (the definition in prop:mod-Rtr). Divide by -th1 th2: (*).
  Step 3 (i = j). In the coordinate w = w_i, x = x(c_i) - w^2/2: D(B^sing) = dw dw'/(w^2 w'^2) (direct computation
  with f = -1/w), so D(B_reg) = sum_k dxi^k(x)dxi^k - dw dw'/(w^2 w'^2) near (c_i,c_i). B_reg is holomorphic, so as
  in Step 2 the left side transforms to -(th1+th2) L(x)L[B_reg]; the right side to th1 th2 (sum_k R R - 1), using
  G_{-2}(th) = -th. Divide by -th1 th2: (*).
  Numerical check (edge233.wl, 60 digits): (*) holds for all 15 pairs (i,j), total degree <= 3, residuals
  1e-45 ... 1e-57. Consequence: (*) at psi2 = -psi1 gives the symplecticity of R_TR again.
(e) Downstream: prop:mod-Rtr unchanged; thm:mod-tr Step 1 then rests on DOSS Thm 4.1 as recalled in DNOPS 2.6
with (2-32) (prop:mod-Rtr (ii)) and (*) (above) -- both proved in the note. The sentence before DNOPS Thm 8.1,
Prop. 4.3 and (8-1) become unnecessary (the theorem can even be applied directly at kappa = 1, making Step 2
optional). Theorem A, Cor. B, Thm C unaffected.

### X3-5: index reading of (2-31) -- minor (confirmed)
The printed (2-31) fixes which index is the Laplace point (upper j). DNOPS's convention (Remark 2.1: left action;
p.7/p.14: (Psi 1)_k = Delta_k^{-1/2}; Sec. 3: (2-32) is "R^{-1} on the unit vector", (3-4)) makes the upper index
the row; this is the note's reading (prop:mod-Rtr, s06_model_2.tex l.43; b_signs.tex l.14 "the row index being the
outgoing colour"). Intrinsic confirmation (proved here from Step 1 above, by L_{c_j} in one variable and iteration):
  L_{c_j}[B(.,p)](th) = sum_k R^{-1}(psi)^j_k sum_d psi^d d((d/dx)^d xi^k)(p),
i.e. the TR leg at a vertex near c_j is R^{-1}(psi)^j_k in exactly the note's reading (leg.wl: residual 1e-54;
the transposed reading fails, residual 0.07-1.2). Missing only a one-line justification with these pins.

### L3-2: 1/|Aut Gamma| missing in DNOPS 2.3 -- minor (confirmed)

are in (Q) ("with its graph weights [DOSS 2.3]") and in the action actually used (s06_model_3.tex l.23 "edges as

### L3-3: Prop. 4.3 (1) vs (2) -- minor (confirmed)
Item (1) as printed does not mention y and is contradicted by DNOPS Example 4.4 unless y = z; the note's
parenthetical reason ("dy/df = p^2/(4(p^5-v)) has poles only at the zeros of df") is the hypothesis of item (2),
which holds (B on P^1 needs no A-normalisation). Pin: Prop. 4.3 (2). Redundant anyway: compatibility is a
necessary condition (DNOPS Thm 4.1) and (2-32) is proved directly in prop:mod-Rtr (ii).

### U09-3 / L1-5: dilaton sign -- minor (confirmed)
(Q) itself declares the correction ("dilaton leaves taken with the sign of PPZ Def. 2.13"), and its support is
cited where it is used: proof of prop:mod-Rtr (ii) (s06_model_3.tex l.19) and the remark (s06_model_2.tex l.55)
cite DOSS 2.3. Checked: DOSS (2-22) and (2-23) contract (R(-z))^i_. in the same way, with the leg vector resp. the
unit; with L(z) the leg matrix (= R^{-1}_PPZ(z) by DOSS Remark 2.1 and symplecticity, R(-z)^T = R(z)^{-1}),
sum_{k>=2} psi^k(-[z^{k-1}]L(z)1) = psi(1 - L(psi))1 = PPZ T(psi). The remark's second sentence ("not a
convention ... preserves the unit") presupposes a CohFT with unit on the TR side (DNOPS (2-11)) and needs
R_1 1 != 0 (with the opposite sign the action differs by a translation whose z^2 term 2R_1 1 breaks the unit axiom
on M_{0,4}); true here, not load-bearing. Pin: DOSS (2-22), (2-23), (2-26).

## 3. Answer to the escalation question
With the corrections below, (Q) gives exactly what thm:mod-tr and prop:mod-Rtr use: the TR correlators of
(P^1, kappa W_v, p, B) are given by (2-35) with alpha = R_TR.TFT^TR (PPZ unit-preserving action, 1/|Aut|),
TFT^TR(e_j^n) = Delta_j^{(2h-2+n)/2} (DNOPS p.7/p.13: e_j normalised canonical, unit sum Delta_k^{-1/2}e_k;
DOSS (2-26), (4-3)), R_TR from (2-31) in the left-action reading, (2-31)/(2-32) read as formal Laplace series
(lem:mod-laplace; G_{-2} = -th forced by R(0) = 1, cf. DOSS (3-64)). The hypotheses are: compact curve, simple
critical points with distinct values, dy != 0 there (def:mod-curve); R_TR symplectic with R_TR(0) = 1 and (2-32)
(prop:mod-Rtr); and (*) -- which the note currently takes from a misprinted source and must prove (Section 2,
U09-6 (d)). Hence the model CohFT M_0.TFT^m is rigorously identified with TR on x = W_v, y = p, with the
normalisations of thm:mod-tr, once that lemma is added.
