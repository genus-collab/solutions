## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

(a) ev : N -> Q[[q]][l] IS injective. X4's argument (m2) is correct; below is a complete proof. It needs one
external theorem that the note does not declare (Zariski density of the hypergeometric monodromy of PF4 in Sp_4,
Beukers-Heckman, with Schlesinger / Picard-Vessiot theory). So the note is right that injectivity is "neither
needed nor proved"; it is true, and with it F_g^N is the unique element of N with ev = F_g, so F_g^cf is an invariant
of the q-series F_g, not of a choice of representative.
(b) Theorem A, Corollary B (both versions), Theorem C, Theorem Gamma and rmk:int-constant state exactly what is
proved: every statement is anchored in def:set-confexp / def:mai-Fcf, and no proof step uses injectivity of ev or any
analytic continuation. The proof chain is unaffected. What remains is wording: C_{alpha,beta} is a formal change of
isotropic frame on the ring representative (the holomorphic limit in the conifold frame), provably different from
analytic continuation (Section 2.2); the note never says so, and three sentences (abstract, def:set-points l.28,
sec:sgn-lit l.106/108) read naturally as more than is proved. They should be reworded (Section 3). Classification:
real-fixable at wording level; affects no main result.

## 1. (a) Injectivity of ev

### 1.1 The presentation (def:set-confexp, s02_setup_6.tex l.8-14)
N = Q[L^{+-1}, l, c_0, c_1, c_2, c_3, i_0, i_1, i_2][c_0^{-1}, W^{-1}], W = c_0^2 + c_0 i_1 - c_1 i_0: a localisation of
the polynomial ring P = Q[L, l, c_0..c_3, i_0..i_2] (9 free variables) at the multiplicative set generated by L, c_0, W.
The derivation (D L = (L^6-L)/5, D l = 1, D c_j = c_{j+1}, D c_3 from PF4 c_0 = 0, D i_j = i_{j+1}, D i_2 = i_3 from the
concomitant relation) plays no role in injectivity: injectivity is a statement about the underlying ring map.
ev: L -> L = (1-5^5 q)^{-1/5}, l -> l, c_j -> D^j C_0, i_j -> D^j I~_1 (I~_1 of def:set-genus0, so I_1 = C_0 l + I~_1).
ev(c_0) = C_0, ev(W) = C_0^2 C_1, ev(L) are units of Q[[q]], in particular non-zero in the domain Q[[q]][l].

### 1.2 Reduction
Since N = S^{-1}P and ev(S) consists of non-zero elements of a domain, ev(x/s) = 0 iff ev(x) = 0; so ev is injective on
N iff it is injective on P iff the nine elements
  L, l, C_0, DC_0, D^2C_0, D^3C_0, I~_1, DI~_1, D^2I~_1
are algebraically independent over Q. Put K = C(q) with D = q d/dq. Since q = (1-L^{-5})/5^5, Q(L) is algebraic over
Q(q) and L is transcendental over Q; hence (transcendence-degree additivity) the nine are independent over Q iff the
eight l, C_0^{(0..3)}, I~^{(0..2)} are independent over Q(q), and this follows from independence over K = C(q).

### 1.3 Proof
Work in Omega = C((q))(l), D l = 1. Its constants are C (there is no y in C((q)) with Dy = 1; the argument of
lem:gau-const, with l in place of ell, applies verbatim to C((q))[l] and extends to the fraction field in the usual way).
Let C_0, I_1, I_2, I_3 be the Frobenius solutions of PF4 at q = 0 (in C[[q]][l]) and E = K<C_0, I_1, I_2, I_3> in Omega:
a Picard-Vessiot extension for PF4 over K (no new constants). Let V be its solution space and G = Gal(E/K).

Step 1 (the group). PF4 is the hypergeometric operator with alpha = (1/5,2/5,3/5,4/5), beta = (1,1,1,1): irreducible
(alpha_j - beta_k not in Z), self-dual, primitive (Beukers-Heckman Thm 5.8: beta = {0,0,0,0} mod 1 is not invariant under
a shift by 1/k, k>1, and (x-1)^4 is not of the other imprimitive shape), infinite (unipotent monodromy at q = 0 with a
single Jordan block), with special eigenvalue exp(2 pi i sum(beta-alpha)) = 1, i.e. the conifold monodromy is a
transvection. Beukers-Heckman Thm 6.5: the Zariski closure of the monodromy group is Sp_4(C). PF4 is Fuchsian, so by
Schlesinger's theorem G is that closure: G = Sp_4(C), dim 10, trdeg_K E = 10.
The invariant form is Conc: for sigma in G, sigma Conc(y,y') = Conc(sigma y, sigma y') (the jet matrix has entries in
Q[u] subset K and sigma commutes with D), and Conc(y,y') is a constant for y, y' in V (lem:gau-conc (i), lem:gau-adj),
hence fixed by sigma. Conc is non-degenerate on V (det Conc_x = (det Wr_x)^2 u^4 != 0, lem:gau-conc (iii)-(iv)), so G =
Sp(V, Conc) (Schur: V irreducible, so the invariant alternating form is unique up to scalar).

Step 2 (the pair). C_0 and I_1 are linearly independent and Conc(C_0, I_1) = 0 (proved in the justification of ev in
def:set-confexp; sanity check work/E7/conc_check.py). The pointwise stabiliser H in Sp_4 of an isotropic pair (e_1,e_2)
consists of e_i -> e_i, f_j -> f_j + sum_k S_jk e_k with S symmetric 2x2 (preservation of Conc forces the f-part to be the
identity and S = S^T): dim H = 3. F := K<C_0, I_1> = K(C_0^{(0..3)}, I_1^{(0..3)}) is a differential subfield with
Gal(E/F) = H (sigma fixes F iff it fixes C_0 and I_1, as sigma commutes with D), so F = E^H and
trdeg_K F = dim G - dim H = 7.

Step 3 (log q). l is not in E: otherwise K(l), generated by the solutions 1, l of D^2 y = 0, would be a Picard-Vessiot
subextension with group G_a (l is transcendental over K), hence by the fundamental theorem G_a would be a quotient of
Sp_4(C); but Sp_4(C) is perfect, so it has no non-trivial abelian quotient. And l is not algebraic over E: if
X^n + a_{n-1}X^{n-1} + ... is its minimal polynomial over E, differentiating gives (n + D a_{n-1}) l^{n-1} + ... = 0, a
polynomial of smaller degree, so D(-a_{n-1}/n) = 1, and l + a_{n-1}/n is a constant of Omega, i.e. in C; so l is in E,
excluded. Hence trdeg_K F(l) = 8.

Step 4 (the generators). F(l) = K(l, C_0^{(0..3)}, I~^{(0..3)}) since I_1^{(j)} = l C_0^{(j)} + j C_0^{(j-1)} + I~^{(j)}.
In Conc(C_0, I_1) = 0 the terms with l form l * Conc(C_0, C_0) = 0 (the jet matrix is antisymmetric), and the
remaining relation is linear in I~^{(3)} with coefficient Jet_{03} C_0 = -u C_0 != 0; so I~^{(3)} is in
K(C_0^{(0..3)}, I~^{(0..2)}). Thus the eight elements l, C_0^{(0..3)}, I~^{(0..2)} generate a field of transcendence
degree 8 over K and are algebraically independent over K, hence over Q(q). By 1.2, ev is injective. Since Q(zeta) is
flat over Q, ev (x) Q(zeta) : N (x) Q(zeta) -> Q(zeta)[[q]][l] is injective as well.

### 1.4 X4's text (m2), checked line by line
"injectivity equivalent to algebraic independence of the nine": correct (1.2). "L algebraic over Q(q), l an
indeterminate": correct. "Galois group Sp_4 (Beukers-Heckman; Schlesinger)": correct (Step 1). "field of C_0, I_1 and
derivatives = fixed field of the stabiliser of an isotropic pair, dim 10-7 = 3, trdeg 7": correct (Step 2; the orbit of
isotropic pairs has dimension 4 + 3 = 7). "log q not in the PV field (Sp_4 has no quotient G_a), so adjoining it raises
the degree to 8": correct, with the routine 'algebraic implies contained' step of Step 3 that X4 omits. "I~^{(3)}
rational in the others": correct (Step 4). No error; the only omitted details are routine. U18_A (l.91) and U18_B
(F2) sketch the same argument.

### 1.5 Consequences
* F_g^N is the unique element of N (x) Q(zeta) with ev = F_g; so F_g^cf = C_{alpha,beta}(ev^{-1}(F_g)) depends only on F_g
  and the frame. The "representative-dependence" (U18-1/2, X2-2, X4 m2, U02 F6, X5-11) is void.
* Every identity proved for the evaluated series transfers to N; e.g. R~ is symplectic in N (x) Q(zeta)[[z]] (the note
  says "not known", s10_main.tex l.71, and instead derives symplecticity of C(R~) from D = 1, which is also correct).
* None of this is used by the note, and none of it is needed: Theorem A and Corollary B are statements about the
  explicit representatives fixed in cor:gau-rowsNN ("These representatives are used throughout") and about F_g^N.
  Using injectivity in the note would add an undeclared external input (Beukers-Heckman + Picard-Vessiot), so the
  sentence "Whether ev is injective on N is neither needed nor proved" (s10_main.tex l.81) is accurate and appropriate.

## 2. (b) Does any statement claim more than is proved?

### 2.1 The theorem statements: no
* thm:int-main / thm:mai-main (s01_intro_2.tex l.9-23, s10_main.tex l.9-21): T_Gamma is built from "the lowered rows
  ... expanded in the frame (def:set-confexp)", i.e. from C_{alpha,beta} of the representatives of cor:gau-rowsNN. This
  is exactly what the proof treats (via thm:flh-main, cor:red-can). (X4 m5, the TFT must also be the expanded one, is a
  separate minor point; def:set-hat l.37 says every series of that subsection is expanded.)
* cor:int-gap / cor:mai-gap (s01_intro_2.tex l.27-36, s10_main_2.tex l.1-34): F_g^cf is by definition
  C_{alpha,beta}(F_g^N) (def:mai-Fcf), and l.36 of s01_intro_2 says so in words ("the image under C_{alpha,beta} of the
  genus-g graph sum written in N, whose evaluation is F_g by (T) and (QRR)"). The proof (Steps 1-3) uses only that C is
  a ring homomorphism, D = 1 (for symplecticity of C(R~)), PPZ Prop. 2.14 over Q(zeta)((s)), and Theorem A. No
  injectivity, no analytic continuation. With 1.5, "the conifold expansion of F_g" (s10_main_2.tex l.7) is moreover
  canonical. Not overclaimed.
* rmk:int-constant (s01_intro_2.tex l.70-73), rmk:mai-constant: claim only that the constant c_g equals that of
  [CGYZ26, Conj. 1.5] in the same normalisation of the vanishing period, and explicitly that nothing about the quintic
  follows. Not overclaimed.

### 2.2 The formal nature of C_{alpha,beta} (X4 m3): correct, and not disclosed in words
C_{alpha,beta} sends (C_0, I_1) to the conifold pair (varpi_0, varpi_1) and L to s^{-1}. It is NOT analytic continuation
along any path: the analytic continuation of C_0 to a neighbourhood of u = 0 is a solution of PF4 there, i.e. an
element a f_0 + b f_1 + c f_2 + d y_3 (y_3 = varpi_1 log u + rho_3). If d = 0 it would be a power series, hence invariant
under the local monodromy at u = 0; it is also invariant under the monodromy at q = 0 (C_0 is a power series in q);
these two loops generate pi_1(P^1 - {0, 5^-5, infinity}), so C*C_0 would be a monodromy-invariant line, contradicting
irreducibility (alpha_j - beta_k not in Z). So d = Conc(C_0^an, varpi_1) != 0 (lem:gau-iso (ii)), the continuation of C_0
contains varpi_1 log u, and the continued pair (C_0, I_1) is never a conifold frame pair. Hence the expansion is the
standard B-model "holomorphic limit in the conifold frame" applied to the ring representative (the notion of
[CGYZ26, Def. 1.2] as recalled in sec:sgn-lit), and Corollary B is a statement in that sense. The note defines the map
correctly (def:set-confexp; lem:gau-confexp) and never says "analytic continuation"; but it never says that it is
not, and "What is not claimed" (2) ("away from u = 0") does not cover it.

### 2.3 Sentences that read as more than is proved
(i) Abstract, main.tex l.18: "Near the conifold point u=1-5^5q=0 we factorise the R-matrix as R=R' M_0(tau(u) zhat)".
    The R-matrix is a series over Q(zeta)[[q]]; the factorisation proved is C(Q) = R' M of the frame-expanded rows
    (def:set-outer, cor:mai-natfact). Read literally (identity of R, or of its continuation) it is not what is proved.
    Same abstract: "Summing over graphs gives the conifold gap" carries no indication that the gap is the formal frame
    statement. (Side remark outside E7: "the term of every leg-free stable graph" is the term of the outer sum R'.(M.omega),
    not of the Givental-Teleman sum of R on omega named two sentences earlier; the latter terms are not regular.)
(ii) def:set-points, s02_setup_3.tex l.28: "Near q=0 the functions of the note lie in Q[[q]][l]; near the conifold point
    they are Laurent series in s ...; the passage is the conifold expansion". Literally false for the functions
    themselves (2.2: C_0 continues with a log u term); true for their conifold expansions.
(iii) sec:sgn-lit, b_signs_2.tex l.100-108. l.100 proves that C and the CGYZ expansion agree on the differential
    subring S of N generated by c_0^{+-1}, (W/c_0^2)^{+-1} over Q[L^{+-5}] (correct). l.106 concludes "for every pair of the
    family of [CGYZ26, Rem. 1.4] ... the formal quintic has the conifold gap with the constant of [CGYZ26, Conj. 1.5]",
    and l.108 says "whether F_g^N lies in the algebra of [CGYZ26, Def. 1.1] is not needed". In the note's sense (C' applied
    to F_g^N) l.106 is proved. In the sense the paragraph invites (CGYZ's expansion of the function F_g), it needs exactly
    F_g^N in S, which l.108 declares unnecessary: unjustified as written, though true. Repair (X4 m4), checked here:
    - F_g^N is l-free (cor:gau-rowsNN (ii)) and fixed by a_k: i_j -> i_j - k c_j, l -> l + k (def:set-confexp; I checked
      a_k(i_3) = i_3 - k c_3 from the concomitant relation, linear in the i_b, with antisymmetric jet matrix). With
      x = i_0/c_0, pi_j = i_j - x c_j (j = 1,2), the l-free subring is N_0 = B[x], B = Q[L^{+-1}][c_0^{+-1}, c_1, c_2, c_3,
      pi_1, pi_2][W^{-1}], W = c_0^2 + c_0 pi_1 (x-free), and a_k fixes B and sends x to x - k; so N_0^{a} = B.
    - sigma: L -> zeta L (fixing zeta, c_j, i_j, l) is a differential automorphism of N (x) Q(zeta) (D L, D c_3, D i_2 have
      coefficients in L^{+-5}). By Theorem C, A^act_n = A^can_n = L^{4n+1} ph_n(L^{-5}) (thm:gau-R0 (ii)), so sigma
      multiplies Q^(0)_{a,n} by zeta^{a+1-n} (row 0 directly; rows a+1 by the recursion with sigma-invariant coefficients
      c_0^2/W, W^2 L^{-5}/c_0^2, c_0^{-1}); hence sigma maps colour i to colour i+1 (rows, Psi_Q entries zeta^{j(a+1)} m_a,
      Delta_i^{1/2} = zeta^{4i} c_0/L), F_g^N is sigma-invariant, and its L-degrees are divisible by 5.
    - So F_g^N lies in Q[L^{+-5}][c_0^{+-1}, c_1, c_2, c_3, pi_1, pi_2][W^{-1}] subset S (pi_1 = (W - c_0^2)/c_0,
      pi_2 = (DW - 2c_0 c_1)/c_0), F_g = ev(F_g^N) lies in the algebra of [CGYZ26, Def. 1.1], and its CGYZ expansion
      equals C(F_g^N). (CGYZ26 itself not consulted; its content taken as recalled in sec:sgn-lit.)
(iv) The introduction (s01_intro.tex l.18, l.28; s01_intro_2.tex l.27-36) is accurate given def:mai-Fcf and "What is
    not claimed" (3); l.18 "the row coefficients lie in N (x) Q(zeta)" is literally true by Section 1 and is meant in
    the sense of cor:gau-rowsNN anyway.
