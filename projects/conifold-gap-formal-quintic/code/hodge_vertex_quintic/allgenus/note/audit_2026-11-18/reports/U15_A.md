## Findings (all minor)

M1 (minor; s09_gamma.tex l.12, sec:gam-series). "At each of the three points L=1, L=zeta^i and L=0 ...
and thm:gam-lmi applies at each of them": true (at zeta^i with s_*=zeta^i, a_*=-1, M_*=0), but the
proof of thm:gam-main (iii) does not apply LMI at zeta^i; it uses the rotation c(z;zeta L) = c(zeta^{-1}z;L)
and (ii). Harmless; the sentence could say "applies at 1 and 0; the points zeta^i follow by rotation".

M2 (minor; s09_gamma_3.tex l.28, thm:gam-main (iv)). The identification of G_inf with
[GJR18, Lemma 7.7, (37)] "at m=5, k=1 and equivariant parameter 1" is an external comparison that the
note does not (and does not need to) prove; it enters no proof (stated at l.12 of s09_gamma_4.tex).
Not checkable from the manuscript. Fine as a remark; the "(thm:gam-ginf)" in the same parenthesis may be
misread as attributing the GJR18 formula to thm:gam-ginf.

M3 (minor; notation). Clashes: delta is -M_*z/a_* in Section 9 but delta(sigma) = (sigma^6-sigma)/5 in
lem:fsp-generic; lambda is local (log(-q)) while lambda_i are torus weights in the intro; Q (sans-serif)
for the chart parameter vs Q (script) for rows; phi is the LMI phase but also the map in lem:fsp-cov.
Each is declared locally; no ambiguity in the proofs.

M4 (minor; s09_gamma_2.tex l.21, Step 0). "the Taylor coefficients of phi and of the F_m/F_0 are
holomorphic on all of D": what enters are F_m^{(j)}(L)/(j! F_0(L)); holomorphic on D because F_0 has no
zero on D. Imprecise wording only.

M5 (minor; s09_gamma_4.tex l.56, cor:gam-free). Free coefficients are defined in lem:gau-HPF (iii) only
for Phi with ph_n in Q[[u]]; ph^act_n are a priori elements of Q(zeta)[[q]] (expansions at u = 1).
After D_0 = 1, thm:gau-R0 (iii) gives A^act_n = A^can_n in Q[L,L^{-1}], so ph^act_n is the
q-expansion of the polynomial ph^can_n, and [u^{4m}]ph^act_{5m} is meaningful through that
identification. The text's "(by thm:gam-D, see the proof)" is correct but terse.

M6 (minor; s09_gamma_4.tex l.72, rmk:gam-prior). A \draftnote ("Describe [MR1901075, §10] neutrally:
decide whether it is presented there as an outline or as a proof") is left in the text. Editorial.

M7 (minor; s01_intro_3.tex l.21 vs a_formalsp_5.tex l.69). The intro lists [DLMF 24.4.1] as the only
quoted input of Theorem Gamma (b); in lem:fsp-dlmf5118 the identity B_k(theta+1)-B_k(theta) =
k theta^{k-1} is derived in one line from the generating function (the definition), so it is not
really an input. Harmless overstatement of conditionality.

No fatal / gap / fixable findings.
