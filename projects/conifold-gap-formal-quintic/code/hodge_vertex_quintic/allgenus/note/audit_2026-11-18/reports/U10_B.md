## Overall verdict

The unit is sound. Every statement of the three files is correct as stated and proved by the argument given,
up to minor imprecisions listed below. I found no gap, no fatal error and nothing that needed a repair. The one
external input of the unit, ABDKS25 Theorem 7.1 with (7.6), (7.7), (7.9), (7.10), is transcribed correctly
(checked against the v3 text, including the factor 1/hbar^2 and S(u) = (e^{u/2}-e^{-u/2})/u), and its hypotheses
(x rational with dx having only simple zeros; y = z; Bergman kernel of P^1; the Theorem 1.14/1.8 hypotheses for the
trivial side (z, x(z)): zeros of dx simple, z regular there) hold for x = W_v, v != 0. The sign dictionary
omega_{h,n} = (-1)^n omega^{(h)}_{n,0} agrees with ABDKS (1.1) (numerator int_z^{sigma_i z}, denominator
omega_{1,0}(sigma_i z) - omega_{1,0}(z) with omega_{1,0} = -y dx, i.e. kernel -K_b) and with their (7.16).

* **lem:mod-closure** (l.11-22): verified. (i) is the termwise antiderivative of -v^c p^{a-c}(p^3 - v p^{-2}) dp, with the
  two exceptional cases correctly read as -v^c log p, +v^{c+1} log p. (ii): d(-log p G) = -G dp/p - log p g dx; the
  right side has no p^0(log p)^0 term (the a = c terms give v^c log p), so it is I(log p g) by uniqueness of

  I(log p g) equals I(g) (not needed). Checked computationally for k <= 7.

3. Formal operations: all sums in the unit are finite at fixed (h, n) or fixed weight; the iterated residues commute
   because the denominators are products of one-variable factors; "lies in Q[v]" is a statement about a rational
   function of (v, p) identified by Zariski density in v != 0.

   the "independent of the primitive" claim in closure(ii), the "only pure parts contribute" claim of Step 3: all done

## Findings (all minor)

* **m1** (s06_model_7.tex l.61, prop:mod-period Step 1): "the finite sum over 1 <= j <= h and k_1+...+k_j = h with
  r = 2h-1+j": for a fixed r the index j is determined; the sum is over ordered j-tuples of positive integers. Wording only.
* **m2** (s06_model_6.tex l.43, l.50, thm:mod-lattice (c)): "(L/Q(v)[x])_w" presupposes a grading on L/Q(v)[x]; Q(v)
  is not graded, so the weight-w piece is defined only through the identification L/Q(v)[x] = Q[v][p]/Q[v] of
  lem:mod-classL (iv). Say so.
* **m3** (s06_model_6.tex l.34, lem:mod-modv): the congruence for phi^2_k is stated "as pbar = -p^4/4 modulo Q[v,x]";
  propagating it to all k needs that I preserves Q[v,x] + vM (closure (iii): I(Q[v,x]) in Q[v,x], I(vM) = vI(M)), and the
  triangularity in weight 4n needs that the log-component of phi^2_{n-1} vanishes mod v (I on Q[p,pbar] produces log p only
  with a factor v^c, c >= 1 here). Both one-line facts are omitted.
* **m4** (notation): I(F) (antiderivative, def:mod-legs/closure) clashes with I_m, I^C_m (integrals, def:set-model,
  def:gau-van, used in the same proof l.39 of s06_model_5); B denotes the log p-coefficient, B_n the generating terms,
  B_{2h} Bernoulli numbers and B the Bergman kernel; w is weight, the ABDKS variable w_i and (in lem:mod-laplace) a local
  coordinate; in lem:mod-genfn (l.30) "B/p" means B(x(p))/p. No ambiguity in substance.
* **m5** (s06_model_7.tex l.87, rmk:mod-hock): a \draftnote remains in the text.
