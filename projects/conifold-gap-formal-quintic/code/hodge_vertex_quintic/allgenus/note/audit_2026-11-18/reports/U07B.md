## 2. Attacks made and their outcome

### 2.1 Convention traps
- (Sp) lowered vs vector form. prop:gau-symp proves `P(-z)^T eta P(z) = eta` for P acting on lowered components; the R-matrix action needs
  `P^vec(-z)^T eta P^vec(z) = eta`, i.e. `P(-z)^T eta^{-1} P(z) = eta^{-1}` on lowered components. These agree only because
  `eta^{-1} = eta/25` (eta = 5 x an involutive permutation matrix). The note states and uses exactly this (conv:tra-forms; lem:flh-inputs (iii)). OK.
- z vs -z. `Omega_s(f,g) = Res eta(f(-z),g(z))dz`; `Pi_i(q) = -[z^i]<Y^C(-z),q(z)>`. Re-derived: shift rule, eq:tra-values,
  eq:tra-pairing `Omega_s(z^{-k}J^C,q) = (-1)^{k+1}Pi_{k-1}(q)`, and Lemma T(b) `Pi_i(q_T) = (-1)^{i+1}[z^{i-1}]Y'_0`. All signs correct.
  Sign anchor: P = 1 gives q_T = -z1, Pi_0(-z1) = v = T_1 (= -[z^{-1}]Y^C_0). Section 6 (s06_model_5 l.39-41) uses the same sign:
  `Pi_0(psi^n H^b) = -(eps_b/n!) Res p^{nu_b} W^n dp`, consistent with eq:tra-values.
- R vs R^{-1}, transposes. Edge `(eta^{-1} - R^{-1}(psi) eta^{-1} R^{-1}(psi')^T)/(psi+psi')`, leaf `R^{-1}(z)(-z1)`: the PPZ conventions of
  def:set-graphsum. `E_P = E_{R^vec} = E_{P^vec}` because `P_0^vec` is an isometry; `q_T = (P^vec)^{-1}(-z1) = -(z/d)(R^vec)^{-1}1`; checked.
  In s07 the edge/leaf of `R'' = P_can Psi^m` (lowered matrices) reduce to `E_{P_can^vec}`, `q_T`: the lowered matrix of a linear map and its
  vector form are the same map, so no convention clash. OK.
- Dilaton factor d = (P_0)_{33} = (P_0^vec)_{00}; d p_{0,0} = 1 from (Sp) at z^0, entry (0,3). OK.
- Fifth roots: the unit uses only integral powers of v and the rational series v(u) = u tau^{-5/4}; no root choice enters. OK.

## 3. Findings (all minor)

N1. b_signs.tex l.22: "The condition (A) is stated in lowered form, \cref{lem:tra-E,lem:tra-T} in vector form." Lemma T is mixed: q_T and (a) are
in vector form, but (b) and (d) are computed with the lowered Y' = P Y^C and the lowered entries p_{k,b}. Description only; no effect.

N2. s04_transport_4.tex l.35-40 (paragraph "The leaf point", after def:tra-edge): it begins "Let P be a dressing with (Sp)" and says "neither (Sp)
nor (A) is used here", but its last sentence identifies E_P, tilde q_T with the edge and leaf point of the PPZ action R^vec.Omega^mod, which is
defined only for symplectic R^vec, i.e. uses (Sp). The computation of q_T indeed uses only the grading. Wording.

N3. s04_transport_4.tex l.138 (lem:tra-F): "polynomials in the entries of the P_k, the (P^{-1})_k and d^{+-1}": only P_0 (through
(R^vec)^{-1} = (P^vec)^{-1}P_0^vec) and non-negative powers of d occur (the second half also noted by U07_A). Harmless over-statement.

N4. s04_transport.tex l.8 and prop:tra-can l.151: "annihilates the vanishing period" is an informal name for (A) (by cor:tra-edges it is
equivalent to (Pi_0 (x) 1)E_P = 0, so the phrase is justified a posteriori); and since -z + K = K (cor:tra-slice), "q_T in K" and
"tilde q_T in -z+K" carry the same content. Terminology.

N5. Confirmed U07_A M1 (mis-references): s04_transport_2.tex l.26 "(\cref{def:tra-tower})" and l.28 "(ii) of \cref{def:tra-tower}" point to
the unnumbered paragraphs after the definition (s04_transport.tex l.54-72); s04_transport_2.tex l.75 "(\cref{lem:tra-frames}~(c))" for the
u-order bound, which is part (b) with ord_u v >= 1; s04_transport.tex l.15 "the pairing ... of \cref{def:set-frob}": def:set-frob only refers to
def:set-fq, the formula is s02_setup.tex l.32.

N6. Confirmed U07_A M2 (regrouping of summable families needs the one-line remark that the family of sub-sums is summable),
M3 (domain of Theta not named in lem:tra-theta), M4 ("every non-zero coefficient" in lem:tra-coeff).

N7. def:tra-dressing example (i) (s04_transport_4.tex l.14), refining U07_A M6: no rationality hypothesis is missing, since
P P_{11}(0,z)^{-1} does not depend on the choice of the regular gauge P (thm:gau-U (b): P = P^natural c(z), and the factor c cancels), so it
equals the rational P^natural P^natural_{11}(0,z)^{-1}; the only unstated point is the one-pole shape of the pulled-back model for general v with
v'(0) != 0 (prop:gau-equalprod (i) is stated for v = u + O(u^2)), immediate. Not used in the chain.
