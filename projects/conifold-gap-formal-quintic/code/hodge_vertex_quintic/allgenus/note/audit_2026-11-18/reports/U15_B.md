## 5. Findings (all minor)

**M1 (minor) -- "thm:gam-lmi applies at each of them" (s09_gamma.tex, l. 12, sec:gam-series).**
The text says LMI applies at L = 1, L = zeta^i and L = 0; true, but (iii) is proved by the rotation
argument, not by LMI at zeta^i. Harmless; could say "applies at L = 1 and L = 0; L = zeta^i follows
by rotation".

**M2 (minor) -- wording of the Euler integral (s09_gamma_2.tex, l. 27, Step 1 of thm:gam-lmi).**
"motivated by the Euler integral of Gamma(M*+a/z) without its factor z^{M*+a/z}": the formal integrand
x^{M-1}e^{(a log x - x)/z}dx represents z^{M+a/z}Gamma(M+a/z) (Gamma *with* that factor; equivalently the
Euler representation z^{-s}int x^{s-1}e^{-x/z}dx without its prefactor z^{-s}). Only motivational; the
formal argument uses lem:fsp-stirling and is correct.

**M3 (minor) -- letter clash in lem:gam-deltasrc (s09_gamma.tex, l. 21-26).** The source constant is
called c, which is also the critical-point slot of SPn_c and the name of the saddle series c(z;L).
No ambiguity in the proof.

**M4 (minor, editorial) -- unresolved \draftnote in rmk:gam-prior (s09_gamma_4.tex, l. 72).**

**M5 (minor) -- dependency statement in the proof of thm:gam-main (s09_gamma_3.tex, l. 35).**
"for the statements of (iv) these proofs use only (i), (iii), thm:gam-lmi and lem:fsp-dlmf5118":
(iii) itself rests on (ii) (absence of poles at fifth roots of unity), so (iv)'s c_n in Q[L] uses (ii)
as well (or, alternatively, thm:con-Pa (iv) gives c_n in Q[L,L^{-1}] directly). No circularity, since
(ii) is proved first.

**M6 (minor) -- attribution in thm:gam-main (iv) and thm:int-gamma (b).** That G_inf is
"formula (37) of [GJR18, Lemma 7.7] at m = 5, k = 1, lambda = 1" was not checked against the source;
it enters no proof (the note proves the value independently).

**M7 (minor) -- cor:gam-free, definition of the free coefficients of Phi^act (s09_gamma_4.tex, l. 56).**
lem:gau-HPF (iii) defines free coefficients for a solution of HPF with coefficients in Q[[u]]; for
Phi^act this holds only after D_0 = 1 (thm:gau-R0 (iv) then gives Phi^act = Phi^can in the conifold
expansion). The text says so ("by thm:gam-D, see the proof"); fine, but the meaning of Phi^act as an
element of Q(zeta)((s))[[zh]] (conifold expansion of a series in Q(zeta)[[q]] through its Laurent-polynomial
representative, thm:gau-R0 (iii)) is implicit. Not in the main chain.
