### s06_model_5.tex

**def:mod-weights (l.2-9).** Well defined. V_w agrees with the V_w of conv:tra-forms. Lambda is Q[v]-linear by
definition; "TR leg of X is -Lambda(X)" is exactly prop:mod-LD (TR leg of v^m z^k H^b is -rho_b^{-1} v^m phi^b_k).
Q[p,pbar] = span{v^m p^j : j >= -m} is the subring generated by p and pbar (p^a pbar^c = v^c p^{a-c}); it
contains Q[v,x] (x = p^4/4 + pbar, v = p pbar); v, x algebraically independent (lem:mod-classL(i)), so the
abstract Q[v,x] embeds. Directness of M: log p is transcendental over Q(v)(p). The log p-coefficient B is
well defined. d(w), e(w) fine. Minor: the target "{legs}" of Lambda is undefined; it is M by lem:mod-closure(iii)
(m-1 below).

**lem:mod-closure (l.11-22).** VERIFIED.
(i) -v^c p^{a-c}(p^3 - v p^{-2}) dp integrates to -v^c p^{a-c+4}/(a-c+4) + v^{c+1} p^{a-c-1}/(a-c-1), which is the
stated formula (v^c p^{a-c+4} = p^{a+4} pbar^c, v^{c+1} p^{a-c-1} = p^a pbar^{c+1}); exceptional cases give
-v^c log p, +v^{c+1} log p; no p^0(log p)^0 term arises, so this is I. (The monomial p^a pbar^c is not unique,
v p^a pbar^c = p^{a+1} pbar^{c+1}, but the formula is consistent under this.)
(ii) d(-log p G) = -G dp/p - log p g dx, so -log p g dx = d(-log p G) + G dp/p; the termwise integral of
G(x(p)) dp/p has no constant term; the result has no p^0(log p)^0 term, hence equals I by uniqueness of the
antiderivative up to a constant in Q[v]. Independence of the choice of G checked.
(iii) all inclusions checked; I(g) = -G(x) + [p^0]G(x(p)) since d G(x(p)) has no p^{-1}dp. phi^b_0 in M,
induction. Q[v,x]-module structure clear.

**lem:mod-genfn (l.24-31).** VERIFIED. d calB_n/dp telescopes to x^n phi_0'/n! (re-derived). calB_n in M since M
is a Q[v,x]-module (over Q). No log in d calB_n/dp forces dB(x(p))/dp = 0, so B in Q[v]; then B = [p^{-1}] of
x^n phi_0'/n!. Inversion via e^{-eps x}; the log-coefficient commutes with Q[v,x]-multiplication. Monomial by
weight (p^{-1}-coefficient of a homogeneous Laurent polynomial of weight 4n + w_b - 1 is c v^{(4n+w_b)/5}, possibly
zero). Checked on b = 1, k = 1, 2 by hand.

**lem:mod-resid (l.33-44).** VERIFIED. [z^{-n}] IC_m = (-1)^n/n! Res p^m W^n dp agrees with the explicit series of
def:gau-van; with YC_b = eps_b IC_{nu_b} and eq:tra-shift, Pi_0(psi^n H^b) = -eps_b/n! Res p^{nu_b} W^n dp. Case
by case: b=0: R = -Pi_0; b=1: R = Pi_0; b=3: R = -3 Pi_0; b=4: R = -2 Pi_0; b=2: Res W^n(p^3 - v p^{-2})dp =
Res d(W^{n+1})/(n+1) = 0 gives R = -Pi_0. All match rho = (-1,1,-1,-3,-2) of prop:mod-LD. Check: R^1_5 = v^4/96
= pi_1 v^4.

**prop:mod-dii (l.46-58).** VERIFIED. Shift rule used only for i <= k. Log-freeness iff all Pi_i(X) = 0 since
Pi_i(X) in Q[v] and powers of x are Q(v)-independent; this is X in K_0 (def:tra-slice, graded setting).
