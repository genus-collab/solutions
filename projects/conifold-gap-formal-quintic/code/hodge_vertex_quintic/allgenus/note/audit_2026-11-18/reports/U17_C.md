### lem:fsp-piola (a_formalsp_4.tex l.8-15): VERIFIED
Statement is the standard adjugate (adj(J)_{ki} = (-1)^{i+k} det J^{(i,k)}, row i and column k removed).
Proof: omega_i is closed (each d psi_j closed; Leibniz). The coefficient of dy_1..^dy_k..dy_n in the wedge of the rows

sum_k (-1)^{k-1} d_k det J^{(i,k)} = 0, and the factor (-1)^{i+1} gives (-1)^{i+k}. Case n = 1: adj = 1, trivial.
No characteristic or completeness hypothesis is needed beyond B commutative.

## Findings (all minor)

### F1 (minor). "For every amplitude" in lem:fsp-cov / lem:fsp-cfu is proved only for power-series amplitudes.
Location: a_formalsp_4.tex l.19-23 (statement of lem:fsp-cov), l.30 (proof cites lem:fsp-univ), l.39-43 (lem:fsp-cfu).
def:fsp-wick (a_formalsp.tex l.38) admits as amplitude "any a with exp(F_{>=3}/z) a(c+t) in R_n(A)". For such a general
a the composite a o phi is not defined in the text, and the polynomiality in s in the proof of lem:fsp-cov is obtained
from lem:fsp-univ, which is stated only for a(c+t) = sum_{m>=m0} a_m(t) z^m, a_m in A[[t]]. So the proof covers
amplitudes in A[[x-c]]((z)). Closure: (1) composition with a map without constant term is well defined on R_n(A) (an
input monomial t^alpha z^j produces only y^beta z^j with |beta| >= |alpha|, and each output coefficient is a finite sum);
(2) the s^r-part of a o psi_s and of P_s/z has weight >= r/2 above the base weight, so each z^k-coefficient of N(s) is a
polynomial in s; (3) the identities (a), (b) and lem:fsp-ibp (stated for general amplitudes) then go through. More simply,
restrict the statement to a in A[[x-c]]((z)): every use in the note (lem:fsp-fubini; lem:fsp-cfu in lem:con-id (b);
s08_connection_5 Step 2; lem:con-S2; lem:con-id (a); s09_gamma_2 Steps 1-2; s09_gamma_3 (iii)) has an amplitude in
A[[x-c]][[z]] or A[[x-c]]((z)). No downstream effect.

### F2 (minor). lem:fsp-res (i): the coefficients of P_+ and P_- must be constants (ord >= 0); this is not stated.
Location: a_formalsp_5.tex l.90 and proof l.98. "P_+/- polynomials without constant term" does not say over which ring.
If a coefficient had negative ord (e.g. P_+ = zh X), then exp(-P_+/zh) = exp(-X) has coefficients of order 0 for all
j, violating ord(c_j) >= eps|j| - M. The proof's bound ord >= k uses coefficients in k (or k[[zh^{-1}]]). Repair: say
P_+/- in k[X]. In the only use, f_0 = 1/X + X^4/4 over Q. No effect.

### F3 (minor). lem:fsp-res (iii), last sentence: the image of an exponential under Omega -> Omega(X+chi) is asserted,
not proved.
Location: a_formalsp_5.tex l.94 ("so for Omega = e^{-f(Y)/zh} beta(Y) it is e^{-f(X+chi)/zh} beta(X+chi)") and proof
l.98 (only multiplicativity and the case X^{+-1} are argued). A ring homomorphism that agrees with substitution on
Laurent polynomials need not commute with the exponential e^{-f_0/zh}, which is neither a Laurent polynomial nor
u-adically small; some continuity or a differential argument is needed. This is the step used in prop:con-res to identify
Res_Y[e^{-W_V/zh} fa_Y dY] with Res_X[e^{-W^/zh} fa^ dX]. Closure (checked): for g in R_res let
T(h) := sum_j h^{(j)} chi^j/j!, first with chi a formal constant, in R_res[[chi]]. Then d_chi T(h) = T(h'), and T is a ring
homomorphism. Both T(e^g) and e^g exp(T(g) - g) solve Y' = T(g') Y in R_res[[chi]] with value e^g at chi = 0 (the
first because (e^g)' = g' e^g, the second directly, T(g) - g lying in chi R_res[[chi]]). The coefficient recursion
(n+1)Y_{n+1} = sum T(g')_i Y_{n-i} has a unique solution, so T(e^g) = e^g exp(T(g) - g). Substituting chi -> chi(X,u) in
u k[X^{+-1}][[u]] (a ring homomorphism R_res[[chi]] -> R_res[[u]]) gives
Omega(X+chi) = e^{-f_0/zh} e^{-(f(X+chi) - f_0)/zh} beta(X+chi), i.e. exactly the convention of (ii). No effect.

### F4 (minor). lem:fsp-fubini: two routine steps are left implicit.
Location: a_formalsp_4.tex l.77 ("the linearity of E^v over functions of w and z") and l.56/l.64.
(a) lem:fsp-ring (iv) states E_{zC}[fP] = f E_{zC}[P] only for f in A((z)). The proof pulls e^{F^flat_{>=3}/z} in
R_{n2}(A), with infinitely many negative powers of z, out of the partial functional E^v. Closure: write
g = sum_gamma g_gamma (monomials w^beta z^j); the family (g_gamma P) is summable (weights >= wt(g_gamma) + min wt P);
E^v is the identity on monomial factors in w, z and commutes with summable sums (lem:fsp-ring (iv)); E^v does not lower
weight, so sum_gamma g_gamma E^v[P] = g E^v[P].
(b) The outer stationary phase SPn_{w_0}[F^flat; ...] needs w_0 to be a non-degenerate critical point of F^flat. This
is not stated, but follows from (ii): det S = det H / det H_vv is in A^x.
