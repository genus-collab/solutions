## 6. Findings

### F1 (minor). PPZ results used over coefficient rings other than those of the source; justification not a proof
Location: s02_setup_4.tex l.79 (def:set-graphsum), s10_main_2.tex l.27 (cor:mai-gap Step 2); used for PPZ Prop 2.12
(lem:set-dilaton(ii), lem:flh-inputs(i), cor:mai-gap) and Prop 2.14 (cor:mai-gap, over Q(zeta)((s))).
The note says the PPZ statements are "identities between finite sums, polynomial in the coefficients" and therefore
hold over every commutative Q-algebra. Polynomiality alone does not give this: the CohFT data are not free variables
(they satisfy the gluing and unit axioms), so a polynomial identity proved for CohFTs over Q does not formally extend.
What is true is that the PPZ arguments are formal manipulations. Closure: every ring on which a PPZ statement is used
(Q(zeta)[[q]], Q(zeta)((s)), Q[[u]], Q(zeta)[varsigma^{+-1}], Q(v)) is a domain of characteristic 0 of cardinality
at most the continuum, hence embeds in C; CohFTs, the R-matrix action and the identities commute with base change
along an injective map, and H^*(Mbar,Q) (x) A -> H^*(Mbar,Q) (x) C is injective; so the statements over C (or over any
field of characteristic 0) imply them over A. Minor, but the sentence should be replaced by this argument.
