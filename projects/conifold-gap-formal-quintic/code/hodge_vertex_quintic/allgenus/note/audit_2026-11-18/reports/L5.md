## 1. Overall verdict

Every quoted statement in the L5 list says what the manuscript takes from it, in the manuscript's
conventions. In particular the value (QRR)

  R(q=0) = diag(h_{w_i}),  h_w(z) = exp(-sum_k b_k z^{2k-1} sum_{a in w} a^{1-2k}),
  w_i = {zeta^i - zeta^j}_{j != i} U {-5 zeta^i},

is correct in Givental's convention (columns R e~_i e^{u_i/z} solve z d_T v = H*v; legs carry
R^{-1}(psi)): the sign of the exponent, the twist weight -5 zeta^i (minus the Euler-class Chern root
5 zeta^i), R versus R^{-1}, z versus -z and the basis all agree in GJR18 (16)-(17), GJR17 (12)-(13),
(19)-(20), Giv01 Sec. 1 Proposition and Sec. 2.2 Theorem 1 + Remark, and CG Corollary 1 (details in
Sec. 3). I also re-derived the q=0 value independently from Mumford's GRR for the constant-map
(Q=0) theory with Teleman's Prop. 8.7(i): it gives exactly diag(h_{w_i}) in the PPZ convention
(Sec. 3.6). This matters: by thm:con-glue and Theorem Gamma (a), Theorem C (D_0 = 1) is
*equivalent* to the q=0 value being exactly h = h_{w_0}; a sign error in (QRR) would have made
Theorem C false and the window (F_g) of Theorem A unavailable. No such error exists.

(T) is quoted in the right version (Teleman's flat-vacuum orbit statement, Theorem (2) in (1.15),
which needs no homogeneity; Teleman's Theorem (1), and PPZ's Theorem 5, would require homogeneity,
which the formal quintic at lambda = 1 does not have). However, Theorem (2) is an existence/orbit
statement over C; the property actually used, that the R-matrix of the CohFT is flat along the line
(def:set-rows (b)) and is determined by that flatness plus its value at q=0, is in Teleman Prop. 8.4
+ Prop. 8.7(ii) (Sec. 8.6, inhomogeneous theories) and in GJR18 Lemma 5.2 (16), neither of which is
cited for it; the manuscript calls (b) a "convention". This is a citation/declaration defect, not a
mathematical one: the needed statement is in the works already cited. The hypotheses of Teleman's
theorem (all-genus CohFT axioms with flat unit, semisimplicity) hold: semisimplicity over
Q(zeta)[[q]] is proved in cor:set-cyclicPF5 (a); the all-genus CohFT axioms with flat unit are
asserted in GJR18 Sec. 4.1 but are not part of (G0) (genus zero only), so they enter only through
(T) "with GJR18 Sec. 4.1".

No finding is fatal, gap or fixable. Eight minor findings (Sec. 2).

### F1 (minor). (T): Teleman's Theorem (2) does not contain the flatness (b) or the fixing of the constants; (b) is a theorem, not a convention
- Location: sections/s01_intro_3.tex l.10 and l.13 ((QRR), (T)); sections/s02_setup_4.tex l.7-15
  (def:set-rows); sections/s02_setup_5.tex l.40-47 (thm:set-GT); sections/b_signs.tex l.12.
- Claim: R is "the R-matrix of the semisimple CohFT ... in Givental's convention: (a) symplectic;
  (b) the columns R e~_i e^{u_i/z} solve the QDE; (c) legs R^{-1}(psi)"; (T) = "Teleman, Theorem (2)
  in (1.15), with GJR18 Sec. 4.1, Theorem 4.1: the CohFT is the graph sum with the R-matrix of
  thm:set-qrr".
- Problem: Teleman v3 Theorem (2) in (1.15) says only that flat-vacuum CohFTs on a fixed complex
  semisimple Frobenius algebra A form the orbit of T_z Sp_L^+ T_z^{-1} of I_A (existence of some
  E in Sp_L^+). It says nothing about the dependence of E on the point of the Frobenius manifold,
  nor which E. GJR18 Theorem 4.1 likewise only says "an R-matrix R_tau ... satisfying the symplectic
  condition". The fact used everywhere in the note (WKB form of the rows, cor:set-cyclicPF5 (b),

  (solutions unique up to right multiplication by exp(H_1 z + H_3 z^3 + ...), constant diagonal)
  and Prop. 8.7 (ii) (flat-vacuum CohFTs over a fixed semisimple Frobenius manifold are
  T_z^{-1} E exp h(z) T_z(I_A) with a fixed E and arbitrary constant odd h), and, for this theory,
  GJR18 Lemma 5.2, (16) (whose proof, (17), is cited only for the value at q=0). "Givental's
  convention" fixes the meaning of the symbol R (S ~ Psi R e^{U/z}, legs R^{-1}); that the R of the
  CohFT then satisfies (b) is a theorem.
- Repair (checked): add to (T) (or (QRR)) "GJR18 Lemma 5.2, (16)" and "Teleman, Prop. 8.4 and
  Prop. 8.7 (ii)"; reword def:set-rows (b) as part of the quoted statement. The content is in works
  already cited; nothing downstream changes. Equivalently: define R as the unique solution of (b)
  with R(0)=1, (a), and q=0 value diag(h_{w_i}) (unique by lem:gau-wkb (ii)), and let (T) assert

- Chain: A, B, C (through (b) and (T)). Confidence that this is a real (citation-level) defect: 0.8.

### F2 (minor). Hypotheses of Teleman's theorem: all-genus CohFT axioms with flat unit, base ring
- Location: s01_intro_3.tex l.12-13; s02_setup.tex l.95-108 (thm:set-G0); s02_setup_5.tex l.40-47.
- Claim: (G0) "the genus-zero twisted classes form a CohFT with unit"; (T) applies Teleman.
- Problem: Teleman's flat-vacuum statement requires the full CohFT axioms in all genera with flat
  unit (his 1.8 (i)-(ii)) and is stated for a CohFT on a complex semisimple Frobenius algebra (and
  for families over open or formal neighbourhoods of a point of the Frobenius manifold, Sec. 7). The
  formal quintic is a CohFT over Q(zeta)[[q]] in the Novikov direction, whose base point q=0 is not a
  point of the Frobenius manifold along t = T(q)H (T = log q + ...). (G0) covers genus 0 only; the
  all-genus axioms and the flat unit are asserted in GJR18 Sec. 4.1 ("We now define the
  corresponding CohFT ... the unit is 1"; "is semi-simple") without proof, and the extension of
  Teleman's classification to the formal base is used there without comment.
- Closure attempted: the facts are standard (the twisting class e(R pi_* f^*O(5)) and the virtual
  class pull back under forgetting a point; gluing by the normalisation sequence; Teleman's proof is
  algebraic over a split semisimple algebra; semisimplicity over Q(zeta)[[q]] with idempotents and
  Delta_i^{1/2} in Q(zeta)[[q]] is proved in cor:set-cyclicPF5 (a), def:set-frob). They are covered
  by the declared input (T) "with GJR18 Sec. 4.1". So the conditionality statement is honest, but
  (T) should say explicitly that it includes: Omega_{g,n} (all g) is a CohFT with flat unit over
  Q(zeta)[[q]], semisimple, and Teleman's classification applies over this ring.
- Chain: A, B, C. Confidence: 0.6 (as a declaration defect; mathematically standard).

### 3.6 Independent cross-check of the q=0 value (Mumford + Teleman Prop. 8.7 (i))
