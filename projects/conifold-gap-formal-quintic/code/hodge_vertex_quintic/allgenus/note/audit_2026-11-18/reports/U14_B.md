## 4. Findings (all minor)

### m1. Branch rules in lem:con-id (b) not reconciled (minor)
`s08_connection_6.tex`, l. 33-35, `lem:con-id` (a), (b); `def:con-PhiSP` (`s08_connection_4.tex` l. 31-35).
Claim: SP at X = 1 of e^{-What/zh} ahat dX equals SP at Y_c of the reduced form (fsp-cfu), then (a)
is inserted and the result divided by N_zh = (2pi zh/5)^{1/2} e^{-What(1)/zh}.
Problem: `def:fsp-wick` says an identity of full symbols is an identity of normalised series *together
with an explicit identity of symbols under a stated rule for the square roots*. Three branch rules are
chained here: fsp-cfu/fsp-cov for X -> Y gives det(-H_X)^{1/2} := Y'(1) det(-H_Y)^{1/2}; fsp-cov for
Y = Y_c p and fsp-homog with mu = Y_c^4 give det(-H_Y)^{1/2} = Y_c 5^{1/2} (with mu^{1/2} = Y_c^2);
`def:con-PhiSP` silently uses Hess^{1/2} = 5^{1/2}(1-u)^{-1/2} at X = 1. These agree iff
Y'(1) Y_c = (1-u)^{-1/2}, which the text never states.
Repair (checked): the identity is true. Differentiating What = W_V o Y twice at X = 1 with
W_V'(Y_c) = 0 gives 5/(1-u) = What''(1) = W_V''(Y_c) Y'(1)^2 = 5 Y_c^2 Y'(1)^2, and Y_c, Y'(1) lie in
1 + uQ[[u]], where square roots are unique. Alternatively argue on normalised series only:
fsp-cfu gives SPn_{X=1}[-What; ahat] = Y'(1) SPn_{Y_c}[-W_V; f_Y]; reduction and (a) give
SPn_{Y_c}[-W_V; Y^m] = Y_c^m eps_b Fs_b(tau zh); dividing by ahat(1;0) = (1-u)^{-1/2} gives (b) because
Y'(1)(1-u)^{1/2} = Y_c^{-1}. Checked to u^5 (Section 3, item 1); (b) itself checked to u^2 (item 3).

### m2. Ring of the second homomorphism in Pa (ii) Step 3 misdescribed (minor)
`s08_connection_5.tex` l. 10. The second map is described as expansion of (1-s^5X^5)^{-1} "in Q[X][[u]]"
followed by expansion at X = 1 coefficientwise in s; its domain contains s^{-1} and X^{-1}, so the
intermediate ring must be Q[X^{±1}]((s)). With that ring the map is a ring homomorphism into
Q((s))[[X-1]] and the argument (two homomorphisms agreeing on s, X) is correct.

### m3. fsp-cov is applied to S_0 itself over A = Q((s)) (minor)
`s08_connection_5.tex` l. 8. S_0(L) involves logarithms and is not in A, so S_0 is not a phase "over A"
in the sense of `def:fsp-wick`; one must first replace S_0 by the formal phase S_0 - S_0(L) given by
its Taylor data (as the proof of (i) does, `s08_connection_4.tex` l. 61), which is allowed by
`lem:fsp-const`. The text invokes `lem:fsp-const` only in Step 5. Harmless reordering.

### m4. Form of the inverse map in lem:con-cfu asserted without reason (minor)
`s08_connection_5.tex` l. 41: "its inverse is of the same kind". Closes in two lines: the map
m(x) = x(1 + g(u, x^5)), g(0,0) = 0, has m'(0) in Q[[u]]^x, so it has a compositional inverse n in
Q[[u]][[x]] = Q[[u,x]]; from m(zeta x) = zeta m(x) one gets n(zeta y) = zeta n(y), so only exponents
≡ 1 mod 5 occur, n(y) = y(1 + h(u, y^5)); returning to X = x/s, Y gives X_k(T) with deg X_k <= k.
(Checked to u^2 in item 3.)

### m5. Field of the gauge in lem:con-id (c) and prop:con-res (minor)
`s08_connection_6.tex` l. 22-26 and `s08_connection_7.tex` l. 18. "Graded regular gauge" is defined over
k[[u]] for any field k (`def:gau-onepole`); (c) asserts G_b in Q[[u]][[zh]] and (d) is stated over Q.
For a Q(zeta)-gauge, (d) holds verbatim over Q(zeta), so nothing breaks; and the only application
(P^SP = s(z) Pc, s in 1 + z^5 Q[[z^5]]) is rational. Say "rational" or "over k" in the statements.

### m6. Glue (a): the identification of the constant series with D_0 (minor)
`s08_connection_8.tex` l. 19. The text re-derives that A^can and A^act differ by a constant series and
then identifies it with D_0 through the s-embedding comparison; but D_0 is *defined* by
A^can = A^act D_0 in Q(zeta)[[q]] (`thm:gau-R0` (iii)), so this is by definition. Redundant, correct.

## 5. Points examined adversarially and found sound (no finding)

- **Signs/conventions.** Phase +S_0 with e^{S_0/z} (Mellin-Barnes) versus -What with e^{-What/zh}:
  S_0(L/X) - S_0(L) = -s^4 (What(X) - What(1)) re-derived (second derivatives agree:
  5L/(1-L^5) = -5 s^4/(1-u)). Covariances C = (1-u)/5 at X = 1 and C = 1/5 for W_1 are positive,
  consistent with the e^{-W/z} convention. Dictionary H^b <-> eps_b p^{nu_b} dp is the same on the SP
  side (lem:con-S2) and the residue side (Y^C_b = eps_b I^C_{nu_b}); eps_b^2 = 1 and the powers of s
  cancel in prop:con-res.
- **z versus zh.** ẑ = z/s^4 throughout; (u X zh)^{2k-1} = (s X z)^{2k-1}; the relabelling
  iota(E, beta, alpha) = (E + (4beta+alpha)/5, beta, alpha) and dx = s dX give exactly
  [u^N zh^j] Vhat = [u^{N+(1-4j)/5} z^j] V.
- **Grading.** f_Y(zeta Y; zh) = f_Y(Y; zeta zh) (ahat depends on X through X zh and X^5; X(zeta Y) =
  zeta X(Y), X'(zeta Y) = X'(Y)); the reduction relation preserves (Y-degree) - (zh-degree) + 1 mod 5;
  G_b has zh^k only for k ≡ -b and u-divisibility u^{(nu_b+4k)/5} with nu_b + 4k >= 0 (k = 0 forces b = 0).
  (d) of lem:con-id is then a correct triangularity argument (Fs_b(0) = eps_b, tau a unit).
- **Formal operations.** Every infinite sum is u-adically finite at each order (V has Xi = O(u);
  pi_m polynomial in zh at each u-order; Y^C in Q[z^{-1}][[u^{1/5}]] with order >= 4n/5); fsp-res (iii)
  applies with chi = Y(X) - X in uQ[X][[u]], and the ring homomorphism Omega -> Omega(X + chi) is
  continuous at each u-order, which gives S(e^{-W_V/zh}) = e^{-What/zh} in the R_res convention
  (f_0 = 1/X + X^4/4). Exact forms: E in Y Q[Y,zh][[u]] is an amplitude at Y_c over Q[[u]] (fsp-ibp)
  and lies in the class Q of fsp-res (ii).
- **External/earlier statements used within hypotheses.** lem:gau-poly (i), (iii): solutions with
  ph_0 = 1 and coefficients in Q[[u]] (both PhiSP and Phican). thm:gau-U (b) and prop:gau-graded (ii):
  s(z) in 1 + z^5 Q[[z^5]] is a unit of Q[[z^5]]. thm:gau-G (a): Pc Y^C(v(u)) = Y^C_Q. lem:gau-vanq (a),
  (d): only y^{(1)}_0 = -t_c reaches u^1. lem:gau-wkb (ii) with k = Q(zeta). eq:set-qrr: A_n(q=0) = h_n.
  def:set-frames: varpi_0(0) = 1, t_c = u + O(u^2) in every conifold frame (uniform in (alpha, beta)).
- **Degenerate cases.** n = 0 (ph_0 = 1 on both sides); the exponent -1 produced only from Y^4; I^C_m with
  e = m + 4i' + 1 >= 0; k = 0 term of V is zero (power series in x); empty hatted fibres for j not ≡ 4.
- **Circularity.** prop:con-res uses lem:con-id, lem:con-red, lem:con-cfu, lem:con-relab, fsp-res; none
  uses thm:con-Pb. thm:con-Pb uses (QRR) nowhere; the glue uses it once, at colour 0.
