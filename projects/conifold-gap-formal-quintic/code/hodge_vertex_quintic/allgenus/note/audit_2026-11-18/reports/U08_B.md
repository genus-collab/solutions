## Findings (all minor)

m1. a3_uconv.tex l.35, lem:tra-conv: "The parts depend on the following statements only: (b) and (e) on the homogeneity of lem:set-modhom; ..."
    The list is incomplete: (a), (b2), (b3), (b4), (e) also use lem:tra-frames, cor:tra-slice, lem:tra-twoslot and lem:tra-sum, (b4) and (f1)
    use (b1) and (d), and (e2) uses def:set-graphsum for F-bar. Nothing outside the proved chain is used, so this does not affect validity.
    Similarly s05 l.7 "The proof uses only the results of sec:tra" omits lem:set-modhom and lem:set-dilaton (entering through lem:tra-conv).

m2. s05_reduction.tex l.39, def:red-ML: "in short, F-bar_h|_{-z+K} = F_h o Pi_0 + Reg_h with a regular remainder". For h = 0 the Taylor
    coefficients of order n <= 2 of F-bar_0 vanish while those of F_0 o Pi_0 = -(5/2)(v+Pi_0)^2 log(v+Pi_0) contain log v; for h = 1 the
    order-0 coefficient likewise. The precise statement (restricted to 2h-2+n > 0) just before it is correct and is the one used; the gloss
    should say "up to terms of order < 3 (h = 0), < 1 (h = 1)".

m3. a3_uconv.tex l.57, (e2): "which is F-bar_g(q~) by the definition of F-bar_g (lem:set-dilaton)": the definition is def:set-graphsum
    (with def:set-modcohft); lem:set-dilaton (iii) only reinterprets it. Mis-reference.

m4. a3_uconv.tex l.21, (b4) and l.24 (e2): (b4) is stated for every leg-free stable graph and refers to vertex factors "as in (b3)", but (b)
    assumes n >= 1; for the one-vertex graph n = 0. Likewise (e2) invokes (b2) for every (l,j) including (0,0). Both are trivial
    (no expansion to do), and Theorem R applies (b4) only to edge graphs, where lem:red-genus gives n >= 1.

m5. a3_uconv.tex (b4) vs s05 Step 3: the flat-sum vertex factor carries the unit d^{2h-2+n}, which (b4)'s "vertex factor as in (b3)" lacks.
    Harmless (multiplication by a unit of Q[[u]] commutes with all sums), but Step 3 should say so.

m6. s07_comparison.tex l.24, def:flh-outer (ii) (outside the unit, but a use of it): cites lem:tra-conv (f) for an arbitrary CohFT on a module
    over Q(zeta)((s)), whereas (f) is stated for model correlators with X_i in H^u_+ and kappa in Q[[u]]^x. The computation is identical
    and lem:set-dilaton (iii) already covers that ring, so the citation should be to lem:set-dilaton (iii) (as in lem:flh-dil).

m7. Notation clashes in a3_uconv.tex: D := 3h-3+n (local) against the global \Dq = D = q d/dq of macros.tex; N in (f) is 2h-2+n+m2 while
    "modulo u^N" is used in the same proof; c := 1 - 1/kappa in (f) against the coefficient families c_(k,a), c_{i,beta} of (a), (b2).
    Also (c) says "used in this section" inside an appendix (meaning sec:tra). Cosmetic.

m8. s05_reduction.tex l.56, thm:red-main: "v(0) = 0, v'(0) != 0" repeats def:tra-dressing; harmless. (Side remark, outside the unit:
    s10_main_2.tex l.51, rmk:mai-necessity speaks of "dressings that are not regular in u", which is a contradiction in terms with
    def:tra-dressing; it is a remark about evidence and enters no proof.)
