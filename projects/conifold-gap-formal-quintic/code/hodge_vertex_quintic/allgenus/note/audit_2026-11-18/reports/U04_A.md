### M1 (minor). The clause on the closing relation in prop:gau-quintic (i) is not a statement

* **Where.** `sections/s03_gauge_2.tex`, l. 20 (statement: "the closing relation is then given by \cref{cor:gau-cycsys}") and l. 31 (proof: "the closing relation is \cref{cor:gau-cycsys} (ii) with w = zeta^i s^{-1}").
* **What the text does.** After carrying the four forward relations a = 0,...,3 through Cexp, it says that the fifth (closing) relation (zD + zeta^i/s) Cexp(X_4) = varpi_0 Cexp(X_0) "is then given by" cor:gau-cycsys.
* **Why it is imprecise.** cor:gau-cycsys (ii) is an equivalence: given the four forward relations, the closing relation holds if and only if varpi_0 Cexp(X_0) is annihilated by PF_5 with zD replaced by zD + zeta^i/s. At this point of the text that PF_5-equation is known only for the q-series (cor:set-cyclicPF5 (b)), not for the conifold expansion; so the clause either asserts nothing or asserts something not yet proved.
* **Why it is harmless.** (1) Every later use of prop:gau-quintic (i) that I found uses only a <= 3: thm:gau-R0 (iv) (`s03_gauge_8.tex`, l. 30, "for a <= 3") and cor:gau-rowsNN (i) (l. 43, the logq-free coefficients). (2) The closing relation for the expanded rows is proved independently in thm:gau-R0 (iv) ("as does the closing relation on the left", from Q^{(0)} D_0 = P_c Q^{m,(0)} and thm:gau-R0 (i)) and for all colours in cor:gau-colours. (3) If the closing relation were available in N it would be carried by Cexp directly, since Cexp(C_1 kappa^LR_4) = varpi_0 = kappa_4.
* **Repair.** Reword: "by cor:gau-cycsys (ii) with w = zeta^i/s the closing relation for the expansions is equivalent to PF_5^{(i)}(varpi_0 Cexp(X_0)) = 0; for the rows it is obtained in thm:gau-R0 (iv) and cor:gau-colours."
