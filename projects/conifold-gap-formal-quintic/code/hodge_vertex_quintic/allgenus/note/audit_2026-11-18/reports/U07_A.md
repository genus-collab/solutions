### M2. Regrouping of summable families: one line left out, twice (`conv:tra-forms`, `lem:tra-twoslot`)
`s04_transport.tex` line 30 says that a regrouping "consists of summable families, and the sum of their sums is the total sum". To form "the sum of their sums" for infinitely many subfamilies, and to apply `lem:tra-sum` to it, one needs that the family of sub-sums is itself summable. This is true and takes one line: for a fixed coefficient index and `N`, the finitely many members outside `u^N` lie in finitely many subfamilies, and every other sub-sum lies in `u^N Q[[u]]`, which is closed.
The point is used in the proof of `lem:tra-twoslot`, (i)=>(ii) (`s04_transport_2.tex` line 77):
(a) "the double family over `(alpha,(l,b))`, summable by the first paragraph": the first paragraph treats the family `(E^{alpha beta} frame_alpha (x) frame_beta)`, whereas this family is `(E^{ab}_{kl} frame_alpha(psi) (x) psi'^l H^b)` with `b` unrestricted. It is summable by the same computation (only the first slot needs the bound).
(b) `lem:tra-sum` is applied to `(frame_alpha (x) G_alpha)_alpha`, whose summability is the unstated line above.
Both close immediately. Step (b) can also be avoided: for `a' != 1` the first-slot coefficient of `E` at `psi^{k'}H^{a'}` is `G_{(k',a')}` itself, so `(1 (x) Pi_i)E = 0` gives `Pi_i(G_alpha) = 0` directly.

### M3. `lem:tra-theta`: the domain of `Theta` is not named
`s04_transport.tex` lines 83-87: "`Theta` is bijective, with inverse `sum_r (-N)^r`". The proof establishes bijectivity on finitely supported sequences in `Q[v]` (graded setting) and on all sequences in `Q[[u]]` (`u`-adic setting); these are the domains used in `lem:tra-frames`(a). The statement should say so.

### M4. `lem:tra-coeff`: "every coefficient" should be "every non-zero coefficient"
`s04_transport_2.tex` line 58: "every coefficient `f_j` of `frame(k,a)` has the `v`-exponent ... `>= 1`". By `lem:tra-frames`(b), `f_j = 0` unless the exponent is an integer.
