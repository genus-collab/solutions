## Findings (all minor)

M1 (minor; s06_model_3.tex l. 28, thm:mod-tr Step 1; s01_intro_3.tex l. 9). The proof relies, by citation of
"the sentence preceding DNOPS Thm 8.1", on the curve satisfying DNOPS (2-30)-(2-33). DNOPS (2-33) as printed
(pdf p. 12) reads (sqrt(z1 z2)/2pi) int int B e^{...} = sum_k R^{-1}(z1^{-1})^i_k R^{-1}(z2^{-1})^j_k /
(z1^{-1}+z2^{-1}), without "delta_ij -". That printed identity is false (for i = j the left side has the
regularised delta term; for i != j my computation, check 2b, gives the opposite sign). The correct form is the
PPZ edge (delta_ij - sum_k ...)/(psi1+psi2), as in DBOSS (4-1) and (3-66). The note never uses (2-33)
quantitatively, and the correct identity holds for every compact curve (Eynard; verified off-diagonal through
total degree 3 here), so validity is unaffected; but the input (Q) should cite the edge condition in the
DBOSS form or flag the misprint, as it does for the dilaton sign.

M2 (minor; s06_model_2.tex l. 2 vs l. 32-38). "Throughout lem:mod-laplace, lem:mod-scale, prop:mod-Rtr, kappa is
a rational number", but lem:mod-scale (ii) applies (i) with kappa = k^4, mu = k complex, and lem:mod-laplace
(iii) uses complex k. Statement (i) is for arbitrary non-zero constants, so nothing breaks.

M3 (minor; s06_model.tex l. 20, conv:mod-kernel). "This is the convention of DNOPS (2-26)-(2-29)" follows the
definition of F^TR; DNOPS (2-26)-(2-29) contain no free energy. The free energy is the note's own definition
(= minus EO (4-10)); harmless.

M4 (minor; s06_model_4.tex l. 34, prop:mod-LD; s06_model_5.tex l. 4). q_phi and "TR leg" are defined in
def:mod-ancvec only for phi in L, -log p and Q[v]-combinations. The legs phi^b_k (k >= 1) contain log p times
polynomials in p, pbar and negative powers of p, so they are outside that class; prop:mod-LD and def:mod-weights
use q_{phi^b_k} via the residue pairing at the branch points. The extension is consistent (linear,
branch-independent by Step 1), and every use with thm:mod-tr (s06_model_10 Steps 1-2) has legs in L and -log p.
One sentence extending def:mod-ancvec would close it.

M5 (minor; s06_model.tex l. 5; s01_intro_3.tex l. 9 "applied only to the model curve x = W_v, y = p").
The quoted theorem is applied to (P^1, 4W_v, p), and lem:mod-scale to (kappa W_v, mu p); (Q) mentions f = 4W_v
later in the same item, so this is wording.

M6 (minor; s06_model_3.tex l. 28). The compatibility condition DNOPS Prop 4.3 (1) and "the sentence preceding
Thm 8.1" are redundant: (2-30)-(2-32) and symplecticity are proved directly in prop:mod-Rtr, and (2-33) holds for
compact curves. The parenthetical reason given is that of Prop 4.3 (2), not (1); both hold.

M7 (minor; s06_model.tex l. 41, def:mod-pair). Convergence and Fubini for iterated ray integrals need joint
holomorphy of omega_{h,n} near products of 0 and infinity. The text argues "in each variable"; this follows
because the polar divisor lies in the union of {p_i = varsigma_b} (or from lem:mod-abdks).

M8 (minor; s06_model_3.tex l. 19). "By (i), 1/varsigma_k = zeta^k/varsigma": this is the definition of
varsigma_k, not (i). Wording.

M9 (minor; s06_model_2.tex l. 55 remark; b_signs.tex l. 14). The justification "with legs R^{-1}(psi) the sign is
not a convention: it is the one for which the action preserves the unit" presupposes that the TR CohFT has a
flat unit, which is not shown independently. The real support is DBOSS (2-23), which I checked has the PPZ
sign, together with the declared input (Q). Check 3 confirms the sign at genus 1. No change to validity.
