## 5. Findings (all minor)

**m1 (minor). Step 3 of the proof of Theorem P(a)(i), `s08_connection_4.tex` l.79, `eq:con-bz`.**
The text says it proves `eq:con-bz` "there", i.e. in `A_L(zeta)[[t]][[z]]`, "factor by factor".
The six individual factors `e^{sigma log q/z}`, `Gst(1+5 sigma/z)`, `Gst(1+(sigma - zeta^j)/z)^{-1}`
and their translates are not elements of that ring: their expansions involve `log q`, `log(5L)`,
`log(L - zeta^j)`, `log z` and poles in `z`; only the product of the six translate-ratios is. The
computation is valid in the formal symbol calculus of `def:fsp-stirling`/`lem:fsp-stirfe` (rules
`exp(log a) = a`, `log(a/z) = log a - log z`, `log(Y+theta) = log Y + log(1+theta/Y)`), and it also
silently uses that the Stirling expansion of `Gst(a(sigma+z)/z + m)` at the base point
`a(sigma+z)` (Taylor-expanded, as in the definition of the translate `b_z`) equals that of
`Gst(a(sigma)/z + m + kappa)` at the base point `a(sigma)`; this is the algebraic identity
`log St_{m+kappa}(w) = (1/w + m + kappa - 1/2) log(1 + kappa w) - kappa + log St_m(w/(1 + kappa w))`,
which I checked (it is immediate from the definition of `St_m`, with `1/w' = 1/w + kappa`).
Repair (two lines): prove `eq:con-bz` at each fixed `L in C_*` in `C[[t]][[z]]`, where all logarithms
are complex numbers for the branches fixed on `D_L`, the factor-by-factor computation is literal,
and the translate of each factor is its Taylor expansion at `sigma + z`; both sides of `eq:con-bz`
have coefficients in `A_L` (indeed in `A_L(zeta)`), whose values agree on the infinite set `C_*`, so
they are equal in `A_L[[t]][[z]]`. The identity itself is true (machine check, Section 3.1).
