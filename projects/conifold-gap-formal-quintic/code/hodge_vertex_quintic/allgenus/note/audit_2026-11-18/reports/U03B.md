## 3. Attacks made, and their outcome

### 3.1 Appendix A (a1_cyclicLR.tex)
- **Grading mod 5 and wrap-around** (`lem:set-grading`, l. 7-18). The degree-d term is a symmetric
  polynomial of weighted degree `m = a+b+c-3 >= -3`; only `5 | m` survives `e_1=...=e_4=0, e_5=1`.
  For `a = 4`, `H*H^4 = kappa_4 H^0` with classical value `H^5 = 1`. Degree-0 terms with `n >= 1`
  insertions vanish (positive-dimensional fibre). Holds.
- **Frobenius symmetry** `kappa_a = kappa_{2-a}` for all `a in Z/5` (`lem:set-unitfrob`): checked for
  each residue; it gives exactly `kappa_0 = kappa_2`, `kappa_3 = kappa_4`. Holds.
- **Substitution w -> zeta^i/z and the z-expansion** (`lem:set-periods`): the image map is a ring
  homomorphism because `H^5 = 1`; each `q^d` coefficient is a rational function of degree 0 at
  `z = infinity`, so the expansion in `z^{-1}` matches `def:set-genus0`. `PF5 I_i = 0` is checked
  termwise, including `d = 0`. Extracting `z^5..z^1` from `PF5(I_i/z) = 0` is legitimate: the terms
  `-z^{-k} p_k` have non-positive exponents. Holds.
- **Constants of D on Q[[q]][l]** and "q^0-part is a ring map intertwining D with d/dl": both
  correct; this gives `PF4 p_4 = 1` (the only place `H^5 = 1` enters kappa_3). Holds.
- **Birkhoff recursion** (`lem:set-birkhoff`): only `kappa_a` units and `kappa_0 = kappa_2 = 1` are
  used, not the values to be proved; no circularity. The `k = a` term of `z d_T J_a` vanishes
  because `p^(a)_a = 1`. Holds.
- **Wronskian** (`lem:set-wronskian`): Abel with leading coefficient `L^{-5}` and `D^3`-coefficient
  `-6250 q`; `D L^10 = 6250 q L^15`. Holds.
- **Order-3 lemma over Q((q))** (`lem:set-order3`): `m_{nj} = delta_{nj} mod q`, so the 4x4 matrix is
  invertible over `Q[[q]]`; the coefficient ring of the application (`Q[[q]]`) is inside the
  hypothesis. Holds.
- **Sign of kappa_1** from `kappa_1^2 = (L^5/(C_0^2C_1^3))^2` in the domain `Q[[q]]`, fixed by the
  constant terms. Holds. **kappa_3**: leading coefficients `(kappa_1 C_0 C_1^4)^{-1}` and `L^{-5}`
  give `chi = C_0/C_1`. Holds.
- **Lowered against raised components** (`prop:set-cyclicLR` (ii)): with `v_a = 5 v^{3-a}` the
  raised recursion `z d_T v^{c+1} = kappa_c v^c` becomes `z d_T v_a = kappa_{2-a} v_{a+1}`, equal to
  the stated `kappa_a` by Frobenius symmetry. Consistent.

### 3.2 Appendix B (a2_cyclicPF4.tex) and s03_gauge.tex part 1
- **Adjoint well defined** on `Q[u][D]`: the anti-automorphism respects the Ore relation (the needed
  `D(Q[u]) in Q[u]` holds); `P_4(-D-1) = P_4(D)`. Holds.
- **Alternation of Conc** argued in the free differential ring `K{f,g}`, legitimately an extension of
  `K` as required by part (i). Holds (it also follows from the antisymmetry of Jet).
- **Entries with y_3** (`lem:gau-conc` (iv)): the step uses `C(f,varpi_1) = 0` before
  `lem:gau-iso` (ii); it closes by transcendence of `ell` (U03_A M1). Bracket orders re-derived:
  the u^{-1} terms of `(1-u)Y_2 - uY_3` cancel. Holds.
- **Transposes in `C_x = Wr^T Jet Wr`** and `Jet^{-1} = Wr C_x^{-1} Wr^T` (rows = derivative order,
  columns = solutions): consistent with `def:gau-conc` (iv) and with `Wr` of `lem:set-wronskian`.
- **Exchange-matrix argument** for `(Jet^{-1})_{ab}` (`lem:gau-transv`): index bookkeeping checked;
  confirmed by the explicit inverse.
- **Isotropy/radical** (`lem:gau-iso`): `sh` commutes with D (two sh-derivations equal on generators).
  The radical of C on `S cap K` is `Q varpi_1`. Holds.
- **Special geometry with arbitrary y_0 in K^x** (`lem:gau-sg` (a)): the triangular systems have unit
  diagonals `y_0^2`, `(Dt)^m`. (b): the symplectic completion `x_2, x_3` is checked, and `G^2 = -1`.
  Holds.
- **Degenerate y_0** (`thm:gau-cyc`, "more generally"): `y_0 = varpi_1` or `y_0(0) = 0` are allowed,
  since the identity lives in `K[D]` and `K` is a field. A non-isotropic plane gives `Mcyc != PF4`
  (U03_A), so the hypothesis is needed and is stated. Scale invariance: the five factors acquire
  `mu^{-1}, mu/mu', mu'^2, mu/mu', mu^{-1}`, with product 1. Holds.
- **Identity over K used over R** (`cor:gau-cycsys`): `K[D] -> R[D]` is a ring map, `E = 0` is proved
  coefficientwise (not only as an action), `lem:set-pf5` holds in `R`. In (ii) the automorphism
  `D -> D + w/z` needs neither `Dw = 0` nor anything beyond z invertible. Holds.
- **The conifold expansion as a formal homomorphism** (`lem:gau-confexp`): N is a localised free
  polynomial ring, so only units need checking (`s^{-1}`, `varpi_0`, `varpi_0^2 D tc`). D-compatibility
  holds on all nine generators. For `i_2` the defining concomitant relation is affine in `i_3` with
  unit coefficient `-L^{-5} c_0`, and its image differs from `C(varpi_0,varpi_1) = 0` only in the
  last slot. I also checked independently that N forces `PF4 I_1 = 0` (from
  `D C(c_0,I_1) = I_1 PF4 c_0 - c_0 PF4 I_1`), so C is consistent with `PF4 varpi_1 = 0`. Dropping
  `-5 log 5` from `log q` is the harmless automorphism of `def:set-confexp`. Holds.
- **q d/dq against d/du; the fifth root**: `D = -(1-u) d/du` throughout. `D s = -(1-s^5)/(5 s^4)`
  restricts to the D of `K` on `Q((u)) in Q((s))`. `L -> s^{-1}` is the only root used. Consistent.

## 4. Findings (all minor)

**M1 (= U03_A M1, confirmed).** `a2_cyclicPF4.tex` l. 50 (`lem:gau-conc` (iv)). "is the coefficient of
`u^0` of the second term" tacitly uses `C(f,varpi_1) = 0`, which is stated only in `lem:gau-iso` (ii).
It closes without circularity: the left side is in `Q`, and the right side is of degree <= 1 in the
transcendental `ell` over `K`.

**M2 (= U03_A M2, confirmed).** Several letters are reused: `J` is the exchange matrix (a2 l. 68),
the J-function and `\Jet`; `\cP` is a series in App. A and an operator in `def:gau-conc`; `Y_b`
(a2 l. 50) clashes with the system unknowns; `\cR` is "weighted rings (appendix A)" in `macros.tex`
but a generic ring in the unit. There is no ambiguity in any proof.

**M3 (= U03_A M3, confirmed and slightly extended).** `a1_cyclicLR.tex` l. 5 and 13-17. The formal
setting at `Q = 1`, `t = TH` is left implicit: functions in `Q[T][[e^T]]`, then the substitution into
`Q[[q]][l]`. Also, "Every step is an identity in `Q[[q]]` or `Q[[q]][l]`" understates the rings
used: `Q[[q]][l][[w]]`, `H (x) Q(zeta)[[q]][l][[z^{-1}]]` (`lem:set-periods`) and `Q((q))`
(`lem:set-order3`). Wording only.

**M4 (new).** `s03_gauge.tex` l. 82 (`cor:gau-cycsys` (ii)), proof in `a2_cyclicPF4.tex` l. 122. Read
literally, (ii) shifts `zD -> zD + w` only "in (eq:gau-system) and in PF5". The forward recursion
`Y_{a+1} = zD Y_a/kappa_a` of the equivalence in (i) would then stay unshifted, and that version
is false. The proof (apply the automorphism to the whole chain) gives the intended statement, with
the recursion shifted too. This is how the corollary is used in `prop:gau-quintic` (i) and
`thm:gau-R0` (ii). Repair: add "and in the recursion".

**M5 (new; downstream pointer, outside the unit).** `s03_gauge_2.tex` l. 20 (`prop:gau-quintic` (i)):
"the closing relation is then given by `cor:gau-cycsys`". The corollary yields the closing relation
for expanded rows only once `PF5^{(i)}(varpi_0 Cexp(X_0)) = 0` is known. That fact is not
established at this point. It is supplied later: for colour 0 by `thm:gau-R0` (iii)-(iv) (an
identity in `Q(zeta)[L,L^{-1}]`, transported by `L -> s^{-1}`), and for all colours by
`cor:gau-colours`. No argument uses the closing relation of expanded actual rows before that point:
`thm:gau-R0` (iv) explicitly takes `a <= 3`. A forward reference would make the sentence exact.

Not findings: the value `u^2 det Wr_f = -2` and `C(f_0,y_3) = -9/10` (the text proves only what it
needs). The redundancy of the alternation argument with the antisymmetry of Jet. Whether `ev` is
injective: not claimed and not used, since rows have fixed representatives (`cor:gau-rowsNN`).
