## Findings

### F1 (minor, remark only). rmk:set-qrrsign, s02_setup_4.tex line 52: "That ph_1 = -3/20 is forced, independently of (QRR), is part of lem:gau-HPF."

lem:gau-HPF (iii) forces ph_1 = -3/20 only under its hypothesis that every ph_n lies in Q[[u]] (regularity at
the conifold). For the actual rows ph_n lies in Q[[q]] (def:set-rows); there the HPF equation at n = 1 reads
(4 - 5 theta_u) ph_1 = -3/5, whose solutions are ph_1 = -3/20 + c u^{4/5}, and u^{4/5} = (1 - 5^5 q)^{4/5} is a
power series in q. Equivalently (lem:gau-wkb (ii)) the actual colour-0 amplitude is fixed by the WKB equation
only up to k(z) in 1 + zQ[[z]], and k_1 shifts ph_1 by k_1 u^{4/5}. So ph_1(q=0) is NOT forced independently of
(QRR); its value -3/20 comes from (QRR) (h_1), and that the actual ph_1 is the conifold-regular one is part of
Theorem C, whose proof uses (QRR). The remark therefore does not provide an independent corroboration of the
twist sign (-5 zeta^i against +5 zeta^i: -3/20 against -11/60). No proof uses the remark.
(For the record, the sign is correct by an independent check: with legs R^{-1}(psi), Mumford's formula gives,
for a factor Lambda^vee(w) = c_top(E^vee ⊗ w) in the vertex weight, the leg factor exp(sum_k b_k (psi/w)^{2k-1}),
so R = exp(-sum b_k (z/w)^{2k-1}); the tangent weights enter 1/e(N^vir) through H^1(f^*T) in the numerator, the
twist through e(R^0)/e(R^1) with H^1 = E^vee ⊗ 5 lambda_i in the denominator; hence weights a = lambda_i -
lambda_j and a = -5 lambda_i in h_w, exactly as in eq:set-hw.)
Repair: "ph_1 = -3/20 is what lem:gau-HPF (iii) forces for a conifold-regular colour-0 function; so the sign
-5 zeta^i is the one compatible with D_1 = 0".

### F2 (minor; conditionality bookkeeping). def:set-rows (a), (b) and R_k in End(H) ⊗ Q(zeta)[[q]]; prop:set-cyclicLR hypothesis.

def:set-rows defines R as "the R-matrix of the semisimple CohFT ... in Givental's convention" and asserts (a)
symplecticity, (b) the flatness equation z d_T w + mu_i w = H*w along the line, and that the coefficients lie
in Q(zeta)[[q]] (no log q, no negative powers). These are properties of Teleman's R-matrix, i.e. part of the
quoted inputs, and they are used essentially: (b) gives prop:set-cyclicLR (iii), the WKB form
cor:set-cyclicPF5 (b), lem:gau-colrows, thm:gau-R0; (a) enters def:mai-Fcf. In sec:int-inputs, (b) appears only
parenthetically under (QRR) ("Givental's convention ..."), (T) is stated as "Omega is the graph sum with the
R-matrix of thm:set-qrr", and prop:set-cyclicLR is stated under "Assume (M) and (G0)" although (iii) needs (b).
Not a gap of substance (standard content of Teleman's theorem / GJR18 Thm 4.1), but the list of inputs should
say it. Useful precision: (b), R|_{z=0} = 1 and the q^0 value (QRR) determine R uniquely in
1 + z End(H) ⊗ Q(zeta)[[q]][[z]]: in the frame Psi = (e~_i), R~ = Psi^{-1} R Psi satisfies
[U', R~_{k+1}] = Psi^{-1} d_T Psi R~_k + d_T R~_k; off-diagonal parts are determined since mu_i - mu_j are units,
and the diagonal parts satisfy d_T (R~_k)_ii = -(Psi^{-1} d_T Psi R~_k)_ii (Psi^{-1} d_T Psi antisymmetric as
Psi^T eta Psi = 1), so are determined up to a constant of D, fixed by the q^0 part. Existence is also formal:
C_1 (Psi^{-1} d_T Psi R~_k)_ii = (Psi^{-1} D Psi R~_k)_ii lies in q Q(zeta)[[q]] because D Psi does, so D^{-1}
of it exists in Q(zeta)[[q]] (no log q), and the q^0 values of the diagonal can be prescribed arbitrarily
(at q^0 the equation forces R~(q=0) diagonal, consistent with (QRR)). Hence R is determined, and exists in
1 + z End(H) ⊗ Q(zeta)[[q]][[z]], from the genus-0 data, (b) and (QRR) alone; (T) is needed only to identify
R.omega with Omega. This confirms the intro's claim that Theorem C uses R only through (b), R|_{z=0} = 1 and
(QRR), and suggests defining R this way, with (T) stated as "Omega = R.omega for this R".

### F5 (minor). thm:set-mirror and thm:set-G0 (d): meaning of J(TH,z) and <<.,.,.>>(TH) at Q = 1.
With Q = 1 and t = T H, T = l + Ĩ_1/C_0, the defining sums over n contain sum_n (dT)^n/n!, which does not
converge in Q(zeta)[[q]][l] (it is not a polynomial in l). The intended meaning is via the divisor equation,
with e^{dT} := q^d exp(d Ĩ_1/C_0) (def:set-genus0 defines e^T this way); step (d) of the translation and
lem:set-grading use exactly this. Repair: one sentence defining J(TH,z) and <<...>>(TH) at Q = 1 through the
divisor equation (descendant form), i.e. J(TH,z) = e^{TH/z}(z + sum_{d>=1} e^{dT} ev_*(E'_d/(z-psi))).

### F6 (minor). def:set-fq: regularity at lambda_i = zeta^i of the localisation formula.
Only the H^1 term is discussed. The other denominators (moving parts of H^0(f^*T_P4), node smoothings) are
weights of the form (a lambda_i + b lambda_j)/d - lambda_k (a+b = d, k != i,j), (a/d)(lambda_i - lambda_j)
(a != 0), (lambda_i - lambda_j)/d_1 + (lambda_i - lambda_k)/d_2, and (lambda_i - lambda_j)/d - psi. At
lambda = zeta none vanishes: a convex combination of two distinct fifth roots of unity lies strictly inside the
unit circle unless it is one of them; and |zeta^i (1/d_1 + 1/d_2)| > |zeta^j/d_1 + zeta^k/d_2| unless j = k,
when the two sides are distinct multiples of distinct roots. So the definition is fine (this is LP's
definition), but the sentence "the second term ... has an invertible top Chern class" is not the whole
justification.

### F7 (minor). def:set-rows (b), cor:set-cyclicPF5 (b), proof of prop:set-cyclicLR (iii): the formal exponential.
"R(z) e~_i e^{u_i/z}" multiplies a power series in z by a series in z^{-1} (u_i contains l); the algebra is not
specified. Repair: B := Q(zeta)[[q]]((z))[E, E^{-1}], E transcendental, D E := z^{-1} zeta^i L E; B is a
differential Q[[q]]-algebra with z constant, v := R e~_i E solves z d_T v = H*v iff (b) holds, PF5(fE) =
(PF5^{(i)} f) E, and E is a non-zero-divisor, so C_0 v_0 = zeta^i L Phi_i E solving PF5 gives
PF5^{(i)}(L Phi_i) = 0 — which is exactly the WKB-amplitude statement used in lem:gau-colrows and thm:gau-R0.

### F8 (minor). def:set-graphsum.
(a) The pairing must be non-degenerate (unimodular) over the base ring for eta^{-1} to exist; not stated (true
in all uses: eta = 5·permutation over Q-algebras, identity in the colour basis).
(b) The sum defining F̄_h runs implicitly over n with 2h - 2 + n > 0.
(c) Cont_Gamma[E,q;Omega] for a general E presupposes E symmetric under exchange of slots (otherwise it depends
on edge orientations); E_R is symmetric; s07_comparison.tex line 23 adds the hypothesis explicitly.
(d) "stated there over Q, they hold verbatim over every commutative Q-algebra" is asserted without argument.
For the rings where the PPZ propositions are actually applied (Q(zeta)((s)), Q(zeta)[[q]]: domains of
characteristic 0) a clean argument is base change: embed the ring in an algebraically closed field of
characteristic 0; the R-action commutes with base change and the axioms are equalities of classes. Fine as a
remark; not a gap.
(e) def:set-rows (c) ("legs carry R^{-1}(psi)") is vacuous for the leg-free sums of this definition; it only
fixes the convention.
