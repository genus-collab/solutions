## 2. Item-by-item verdicts (quoted statement vs what the manuscript takes)

1. **Sec. 0.2, (1)** -- VERIFIED. Used in lem:set-pushins (iii) proof (s02_setup_5.tex l.37) as the gluing map along which C_Gamma is pushed forward; integrals: int_{Mbar_g} xi_Gamma* X = int_{M_Gamma} X. Matches.
2. **Def. 0.3 (iii)** -- VERIFIED. (iii) is exactly the unit axiom (pull-back along forgetting the point carrying 1, and Omega_{0,3}(v_1,v_2,1)=eta(v_1,v_2)); used in lem:set-dilaton (i) (s02_setup_8.tex l.6, l.20) in exactly this form. "Def. 0.3 (i)-(iii)" = CohFT with unit, as in PPZ.
3. **(5)** -- VERIFIED (equivalent form). Manuscript (s02_setup_4.tex l.70): R(-z)^*R(z)=1. PPZ: R(z)R^*(-z)=1. Equivalent because R=1+O(z) is invertible in End(V)[[z]] and a one-sided inverse is two-sided; also equivalent to def:set-rows (a) R(-z)^T eta R(z)=eta (with R^*=eta^{-1}R^T eta for the matrix convention of Rem. 2.1). Strictly, (5) numbers the series and the condition is the unnumbered display after it, but PPZ themselves call (5) "the group (5)"; no issue.
4. **Rem. 2.1 and (6)** -- VERIFIED. With R e_l = sum_j R^j_l e_j (the convention of Rem. 2.1), sum_{jk}[sum_{ls}(R^{-1})^j_l(z)eta^{ls}(R^{-1})^k_s(w)] e_j(x)e_k = sum_{ls} eta^{ls} R^{-1}(z)e_l (x) R^{-1}(w)e_s = (R^{-1}(z)(x)R^{-1}(w)) eta^{-1}. So the manuscript's E_R(psi,psi') = (eta^{-1} - (R^{-1}(psi)(x)R^{-1}(psi'))eta^{-1})/(psi+psi') (s02_setup_4 l.72), and the matrix form (eta^{-1}-R^{-1}(psi)eta^{-1}R^{-1}(psi')^T)/(psi+psi') of def:tra-edge (s04_transport_4 l.21) and sec:sgn-R, are PPZ's (6) with z=psi, w=psi': R^{-1} (not R), arguments +psi, +psi' (not -psi). The coordinate form of the symplectic condition in Rem. 2.1 is M(z) eta^{-1} M(-z)^T = eta^{-1} for M the matrix of R, i.e. R(z)R^*(-z)=1: self-consistent. Uses "Rem. 2.1" for N_X(-psi',psi')=N_X(psi,-psi)=0 when X(-z)^TX(z)=1 (s10_main l.71, s02_setup_8 l.50, s07_comparison l.33, s07_comparison_2 l.80): the bivector form of the condition gives exactly this; checked.
5. **Def. 2.2 with the convention after it** -- VERIFIED. Legs R^{-1}(psi), edges as in (6), factor abs(Aut Gamma)^{-1}, vertices Omega; "formed with R^{-1} [p. 20]" and "as in Givental and Teleman [p. 20]" point to the remark on p. 20, which says this. Def. 2.2 (iii) is the edge item, as cited in lem:set-pushins (iii) proof. Note: Def. 2.2 does not write the push-forward xi_Gamma*; Cont_Gamma is declared to lie in H^*(Mbar_{g,n}) and footnote 6 refers to Sections 0.2-0.4, where strata classes are push-forwards by xi_Gamma. The manuscript's reading (push-forward along (1)) is the intended one. See finding M4 for the "Givental and Teleman" part.
6. **Prop. 2.4** -- VERIFIED (left action, R_b(R_aOmega)=(R_bR_a)Omega). Enters through the proof of Prop. 2.14 (the step R_a(R_b Psi) = (R_aR_b) Psi in (11)).
7. **Def. 2.5 with the convention after it** -- VERIFIED. lem:set-pushins (ii) (s02_setup_5 l.11-13, proof l.31-35) quotes the formula verbatim (1/m!, p_{m*}, T(psi_{n+1})...T(psi_{n+m})), T in z^2V[[z]], and reads T(psi) as sum_{c>=2} psi^c (class with argument T_c): this is PPZ's stated abuse of notation. The finiteness remark (m <= 3h-3+n) is correct and consistent with Rem. 2.6.
8. **Prop. 2.8** -- VERIFIED; used only through the proof of Prop. 2.14 (last equality of (11)).
9. **Prop. 2.9** -- VERIFIED; used through the proofs of Prop. 2.14 and of Prop. 2.12 (the manuscript's bookkeeping names only 2.14; finding M1). Hypothesis T_a, T_b in z^2V[[z]] holds in the use inside 2.14 (R_b^{-1} z[1-R_a^{-1}1] is O(z^2) because R_a=1+O(z)).
10. **Prop. 2.12 with (8)** -- VERIFIED. Statement: R T_b Omega (= R.Omega by Def. 2.13, T_b = t_R) is a CohFT with the same unit 1. Uses: lem:set-pushins (iii) (R.Omega a CohFT with unit), lem:set-dilaton (ii) (unit of M_0(z/varsigma^4).omega^m_v is varsigma^{-1} sum zeta^i e~_i, the unit of omega^m_v), lem:flh-inputs (i), cor:mai-gap Step 2 (unit of M.C(omega) is that of C(omega)), sec:tra-dress (t_{R^vec} = T_b). All within hypotheses (CohFT with unit; R symplectic with R(0)=1). Formula (8): the manuscript (s02_setup_5 l.25) writes pi^*(psi_j^d) = psi_j^d - delta_j pi^*(psi_j^{d-1}) with delta_j the divisor of curves with a genus-0 component carrying only j and N+1: this is PPZ's (8) for the vertex moduli space Mbar_{h,N} (any Mbar_{h,N} occurs as a vertex space), the same divisor. It is a pull-back formula (not a push-forward formula). Independent check: (8) is equivalent to pi^*psi_j = psi_j - delta_j together with psi_j delta_j = 0, both standard; the manuscript's derivation of psi_j delta_j = 0 from (8) at d=1,2 is algebraically correct ((psi-delta)^2 = psi^2 - delta(psi - delta) forces psi delta = 0), as are the swap argument (psi_{N+1}delta_j = 0) and the induction in (i). lem:set-pushins (i)-(iii) verified line by line.
11. **Def. 2.13** -- VERIFIED. T(z) = z1 - zR^{-1}(z)(1) = t_R (macros.tex l.33; s02_setup_4 l.75; b_signs l.14); R.Omega = R T Omega (translation first). Leaf point q_R = R^{-1}(z)(-z1) = -z1 + t_R: identity checked. Sign: independent rank-one sanity check (work/L1/rank1_unit_check.py): V=Q, eta=1, Omega the trivial TFT, R=exp(az+...): the degree-1 part of (R T Omega)_{0,4}(1,1,1,1) integrates to -4a (legs) + 3a (edges) + s.a (translation, kappa_1) which is 0 exactly for PPZ's sign s=+1 and -2a for the opposite sign. So the dilaton-leaf sign +psi(1-R^{-1}(psi)1) of sec:sgn-R is the unit-preserving one, as the manuscript says.
12. **Prop. 2.14** -- VERIFIED, including the order of factors: R_a.(R_b.Omega) = (R_aR_b).Omega. In cor:mai-gap (s10_main_2 l.23-25) X := C(R~)M^{-1}, so C(R~) = XM and C(R~).C(omega) = X.(M.C(omega)) is Prop. 2.14 with R_a=X, R_b=M. Hypotheses: C(omega) is a TFT with unit over Q(zeta)((s)); M and X are symplectic for the colour pairing (identity matrix), M(0)=X(0)=1 (X symplectic follows from M and C(R~) symplectic; checked: X(-z)^TX(z) = M(-z)^{-T}M(z)^{-1} = (M(z)M(-z)^T)^{-1} = 1); M.C(omega) has unit 1 by Prop. 2.12, so X.(M.C(omega)) is defined. The symplecticity of C(R~) comes from D=1 (thm:gam-D), outside L1.
13. **"Identities between finite sums; hold verbatim over every commutative Q-algebra / over Q(zeta)((s))"** (s02_setup_4 l.79; s10_main_2 l.27) -- VERIFIED up to minor points (M1, M2). All PPZ proofs involved (2.3, 2.4, 2.7, 2.8, 2.9, Lemma 2.11, 2.12, (8), 2.14) consist of (a) geometric identities in H^*(Mbar,Q) (pull-back/push-forward of strata classes, base change for forgetful maps, (8)), extended linearly to H^*(Mbar,Q)(x)_Q A, and (b) algebra in End(V)[[z]] that only uses R(0)=1 (so R^{-1} exists) and invertibility of eta; translation sums are finite for degree reasons. Rings where PPZ is applied in the note: Q(zeta)[[q]] (thm:set-GT, def:mai-Fcf), Q[[u]] (sec:tra-dress, lem:flh-comm), Q(zeta)((s)) (def:set-outer, cor:mai-gap), the model coefficients in varsigma and C (def:set-modcohft, lem:set-dilaton (ii), thm:mod-tr). eta is invertible over Q in all of them (flat: 5 x permutation; colour: identity; TR frozen basis: -1/5 identity).

## 4. Findings (all minor; no fatal, gap or fixable finding in L1)

**M1 (minor) -- incomplete bookkeeping of the PPZ results that enter.**
s01_intro_3.tex l.9 says "Props. 2.4, 2.8 and 2.9 enter only through the proof of Prop. 2.14", and the parenthetical of cor:mai-gap (s10_main_2.tex l.27) lists only the proofs of Props. 2.4, 2.8, 2.9, 2.12, 2.14 as holding verbatim over Q(zeta)((s)). But the proof of the quoted Prop. 2.12 opens with "By Proposition 2.9, T_aR Omega and RT_b Omega are equal CohFTs", so it uses Prop. 2.9 and, for "CohFTs", Props. 2.3 and 2.7, and it uses Lemma 2.11 implicitly (p. 26: "we will implicitly use"). Closes: these proofs are of the same formal kind (geometric identities in H^*(Mbar,Q) plus algebra in End(V)[[z]]), so they also hold verbatim over any commutative Q-algebra; the input list should add Props. 2.3, 2.7, Lemma 2.11 (through the proof of Prop. 2.12) and say Prop. 2.9 also enters through Prop. 2.12. No effect on validity.

**M2 (minor) -- non-degeneracy of eta over a ring.** def:set-graphsum (s02_setup_4 l.70), lem:set-pushins (s02_setup_5 l.5), def:flh-outer (s07_comparison l.19) take "a free module of finite rank with pairing eta over a commutative Q-algebra"; PPZ require eta non-degenerate, and the note uses the inverse bivector eta^{-1}. Over a ring one needs eta to be a perfect pairing (det eta a unit). Holds in every application (eta = 5 x permutation matrix in the flat basis, identity in the colour basis, -1/5 identity in the TR frozen basis). Add "perfect" / "with invertible Gram matrix".

**M3 (minor) -- "R'.(M.omega)" for a non-endomorphism.** def:set-outer (s02_setup_8 l.50): "the outer graph sum is the leg-free part of R'.(M.omega) in the sense of def:flh-outer". R' maps the colour space to H and R'(0) = Psi_Q != 1, so R'.(-) is not PPZ's Def. 2.13, and def:flh-outer defines only contractions Cont_Gamma[E_O, q_O; Omega], not a CohFT O.Omega. The intended object is (Psi_cf)_*(X.(M.omega)) with X = Psi_cf^{-1}R'; cor:mai-gap Step 2 does exactly this rigorously (via lem:flh-frame), and def:set-outer states that no statement of its section uses the symplecticity of R'. Wording only.

**M4 (minor, routed to the (T)/(QRR) audit) -- the p. 20 sentence on Givental and Teleman.** s02_setup_4 l.79 ("as in Givental and Teleman [p. 20]"), b_signs.tex l.14 ("also the convention of Givental and Teleman by the remark after it"), s01_intro l.12. PPZ p. 20 does say "the same convention is used by Givental and Teleman in their papers" (inverse of R in all formulas). This is an unproved one-line assertion about other papers; it does not by itself establish that the action in Teleman's Theorem (2) of (1.15) and in GJR18 Thm 4.1, with Givental's R normalised as in def:set-rows (b) (columns R e~_i e^{u_i/z} solve the QDE), is PPZ's Def. 2.13 with that same R. That identification is the content of (T)+(QRR) and must be checked in Teleman/GJR18 (PPZ p. 35, footnote 10, records a sign difference in Teleman's Euler field, a reminder that conventions differ there). As a quotation of PPZ the citation is accurate; the note does not rest on it beyond (T). No L1 defect.

**M5 (minor) -- "the sign is not a convention" (s06_model_2.tex l.55; sec:sgn-R).** The remark asserts that with legs R^{-1}(psi) the dilaton sign of Def. 2.13 is the only one for which the action preserves the unit. PPZ prove only that T_b works (Prop. 2.12); "combined in a unique way" (p. 26) is prose. The claim is true whenever R_1 1 != 0 (e.g. the rank-one computation above: the opposite sign gives -2a != 0 at (0,4); in general the difference is a nonzero translation, whose z^2 term R_1 1 violates the (0,4) unit axiom for a semisimple TFT), but it is neither proved in the note nor needed: (Q) declares the DOSS15 action "with its dilaton leaves taken with the sign of Def. 2.13" as part of the input. Whether DBOSS12 Thm 4.1 holds with that sign is for the topological-recursion audit.

## 5. Conclusion

Every PPZ statement quoted in (Q) exists in v3 under the cited number and says what the note takes from it, in the same conventions: legs R^{-1}(psi); edge (eta^{-1} - R^{-1}(psi')eta^{-1}R^{-1}(psi'')^t)/(psi'+psi'') with +psi arguments; factor 1/abs(Aut Gamma); translation T(z) = z1 - zR^{-1}(z)1 applied before R (= T_b of Prop. 2.12); left action R_a.(R_b.Omega) = (R_aR_b).Omega; (8) a pull-back formula, correctly used. All hypotheses (CohFT with unit, R symplectic with R(0)=1, T in z^2V[[z]]) hold at every place of use, and the extension from Q to the coefficient rings of the note is legitimate. Only minor findings (M1-M5).
