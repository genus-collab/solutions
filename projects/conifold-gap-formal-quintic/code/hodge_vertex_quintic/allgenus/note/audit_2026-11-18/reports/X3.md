## 2. Independent checks of the conventions

### 2.1 Pairing, lowered and raised components, symplectic conditions

* eta(H^a,H^b) = 5 delta_{a+b=3}, from localisation sum_i 5 zeta^{i(a+b+1)}/(5 zeta^{4i}). The inverse matrix is
  eta^{ab} = delta/5, and as matrices eta = 25 eta^{-1} (def:set-fq, conv:tra-forms).
* Lowered components Y = eta J. The invariant pairing on lowered components is eta^{-1}, on vectors eta. So the
  symplectic condition reads Q(-z)^T eta^{-1} Q(z) = 1 for rows (lem:set-M0sympl, lem:flh-inputs(iii),
  lem:mai-Psi) and R(-z)^T eta R(z) = eta for vector-form R (def:set-rows(a), def:tra-edge).
  For a gauge P on lowered components, P(-z)^T eta P(z) = eta (prop:gau-symp, (Sp)) is equivalent to the
  eta^{-1} form because eta is proportional to eta^{-1}. So the two conditions are interchangeable, as
  conv:tra-forms says. Every occurrence I traced uses the right one.
* Self-duality "eta A = A^T eta" (def:gau-onepole) and "eta^{-1}A^m symmetric" (lem:set-M0sympl step 2) are the
  same condition, for the same reason.
* P^vec = eta^{-1} P eta, d = (P_0)_{33} = (P_0^vec)_{00}: checked ((eta^{-1})_{03} eta_{30} = (1/5)*5).

## 4. Findings (all minor)

### m1. The letter Delta-hat is used for two different TFTs (minor; notation)
b_signs.tex l.32 (sgn-var "Flat and hatted") writes "Delta-hat_i^{1/2} = zeta^{4i} x-hat with x-hat = varpi_0"
for the hatted TFT of the formal quintic (def:set-hat). Elsewhere Delta-hat_i^{1/2} := zeta^{4i}varsigma denotes
the model TFT:
* def:set-modcohft, s02_setup_7.tex l.37;
* tab:sgn-model row "model TFT", b_signs.tex l.56;
* lem:flh-inputs(iv), s07_comparison_2.tex l.60;
* thm:mod-tr Step 3, s06_model_3.tex l.32.

The two values differ: varpi_0 against varsigma/s = tau^{-1/4}, with ratio d. A reader who carries "Delta-hat"
from the appendix into lem:flh-inputs(iv) gets the wrong TFT. Repair: write x-hat zeta^{4i} without the letter
Delta-hat in b_signs.tex l.32, as def:set-hat does.

### m2. "Only the B_{2k} enter" is imprecise for Gamma_inf (minor)
b_signs.tex l.34 says the Bernoulli numbers and polynomials are those of def:fsp-stirling, and that "only the
B_{2k}, k >= 1, enter, so the conventions xi/(e^xi-1) and xi/(1-e^{-xi}) give the same formulas". This holds
for h_w, Gamma_St and Stirling's series. It does not hold for:
* Gamma_inf in thm:int-gamma(b) and thm:gam-ginf, which involves the Bernoulli polynomials B_{5l+1}(1/5);
* beta_n(theta) in lem:fsp-dlmf5118.
For these the other convention gives B_n(theta+1) = B_n(theta) + n theta^{n-1}. For example the coefficient of
z^5 would change from B_6(1/5)/6 by (1/5)^5. In addition, B_1 = -1/2 is used in the proof of lem:fsp-stirfe.

The mathematics is unaffected, because def:fsp-stirling fixes DLMF 24.2.3 and the proof of lem:fsp-dlmf5118
uses it consistently. Repair: restrict the sentence to h_w and the Stirling series, and say that Gamma_inf uses
the DLMF convention of the polynomials.

### m3. In def:set-outer, eta^{-1} must be read as a bivector, not as the lowered-component matrix (minor; reading hazard)
s02_setup_8.tex l.46-48 (def:set-outer) and s07_comparison_2.tex l.78 (thm:flh-main Step 1) write
N(psi,psi') = 1 - (R'^{-1}(psi) (x) R'^{-1}(psi')) eta^{-1}, while R' is given by its matrix Q M^{-1} on lowered
components. In lowered components the inverse bivector has the matrix eta = 25 eta^{-1}. Read as the matrix
(1/5)delta, the second term would be off by 1/25 and N(0,0) would be 24/25 instead of 0. Even so, the
antisymmetrised numerator still vanishes on the antidiagonal and would give a power series silently.

The note means the bivector: def:set-graphsum l.75 defines eta^{-1} in V (x) V, and the matrix forms in
rmk:flh-fail(iii) and sgn-R use eta^{-1} correctly as R'(-psi)^T eta^{-1} R'(-psi'). Repair: add "(the inverse
bivector, of matrix eta in lowered components)" in def:set-outer, or write the matrix form
R'^{-1}(psi) eta R'^{-1}(psi')^T.

### m4. "Normalised stationary phase" names two objects (minor; terminology)
def:fsp-wick (a_formalsp.tex l.38-42) and sgn-sp (b_signs_2.tex l.65) call SP° "the normalised stationary
phase"; its constant term is a_0(c), and SP-hat = SP°/a_0(c). By contrast:
* the intro (s01_intro_2.tex l.53: "the normalised stationary-phase series at sigma = L ... c_0(L) = 1");
* def:gam-c (s08_connection_3.tex l.19) and def:con-PhiSP (s08_connection_4.tex l.27), which add
  "with constant term 1";
* lem:con-form(d), prop:gam-rational and thm:con-Pb ("their normalised series coincide" for amplitudes that
  differ by a constant factor);
all use "normalised" for SP-hat. Each place is unambiguous in context, since the formulas say SP-hat, but the
word changes meaning. Repair: call SP-hat the "reduced" (or "unit-normalised") series, or add "divided by its
constant term" in the intro.

### m5. The index reading of DNOPS (2-31) is not justified in the text, although the note's own (2-32) forces it (minor; presentation)
sgn-R (b_signs.tex l.14) says that the action of DNOPS §2.3 is "the same" as PPZ's, and also that DNOPS's R is
the transpose of Givental's. prop:mod-Rtr then reads R_TR^{-1}(vartheta^{-1})^j_i with "upper index the row".
The text does not say why this reading, and not its transpose, is the one under which the quoted theorem
applies.

The note itself contains the decisive check. prop:mod-Rtr(ii) verifies (2-32) under this reading. Under the
transposed reading, (2-32) fails at order t^3 (Section 2.4: 13191/80000 against 11571/80000), so the
hypotheses of DOSS Thm 4.1 would fail. Under the note's reading, the hypothesis (2-32) of the quoted theorem
holds. Repair: add this one-line remark to prop:mod-Rtr or to sgn-R.
