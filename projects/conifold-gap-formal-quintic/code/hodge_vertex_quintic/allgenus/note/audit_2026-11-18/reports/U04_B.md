## Findings

### Finding 1 (minor) — `prop:gau-quintic` (i), closing relation
`s03_gauge_2.tex` l.20 (statement) and l.31 (proof).
The statement says that Cexp carries the forward relations (a = 0..3) of arbitrary elements X_a of
N((z)) tensor Q(zeta) to the expanded forward relations and that "the closing relation is then given by
`cor:gau-cycsys`"; the proof says "the closing relation is `cor:gau-cycsys` (ii) with w = zeta^i s^{-1}".
But `cor:gau-cycsys` (ii) is an equivalence: given the forward relations, the closing relation holds
if and only if varpi_0 Cexp(X_0) solves PF5 twisted by w. For arbitrary X_a satisfying only the forward
relations the closing relation is false in general, and the extra hypothesis is not stated.
How it closes: for the rows (the only case used) C_0 X_0 = sum_n A^act_n(L) z^n with
A^act_n in Q(zeta)[L, L^{-1}] (`thm:gau-R0` (iii)), the twisted PF5 equation is an identity in the
differential ring Q(zeta)[L^{±1}] (it holds in Q(zeta)[[q]] by `cor:set-cyclicPF5` (b) and
L -> (1-5^5 q)^{-1/5} is injective), hence holds after L -> s^{-1}; alternatively `thm:gau-R0` (iv)
obtains the closing relation of the expanded rows from the identity with Pc Q^m. Neither route is
available at the point where the proposition is stated, and no later proof uses the clause in the
imprecise form (`thm:gau-R0` (iv) and `cor:gau-rowsNN` quote (i) only for a <= 3).
Repair: replace the clause by "the closing relation is then equivalent, by `cor:gau-cycsys` (ii) with
w = zeta^i s^{-1}, to PF5^{(i)}(varpi_0 Cexp(X_0)) = 0; for the rows it is proved in `thm:gau-R0` (iv)".
