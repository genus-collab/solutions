### F1 (minor). def:mai-Fcf: rationality of the colour-0 row representatives is not cited
s10_main.tex:81 ("By lem:gau-colrows, Q^{(i)}_{a,n} = zeta^{i(a+1-n)} Q^{(0)}_{a,n} with Q^{(0)}_{a,n} in N").
cor:gau-rowsNN(i) (s03_gauge_8.tex:36) and thm:gau-R0(iii) only give A^act_n in Q(zeta)[L,L^{-1}], so the
representatives of the colour-0 rows are a priori in N (x) Q(zeta), not in N; lem:gau-colrows says nothing on
rationality. The Galois argument "sigma_k(Q^{(i)}_{a,n}) = Q^{(ki)}_{a,n}" needs Q^{(0)}_{a,n} in N.
Closure: thm:con-glue gives D_0 in 1 + zQ[[z]], so A^act = A^can D_0^{-1} with A^can_n in Q[L,L^{-1}]
(thm:gau-R0(ii)) gives A^act_n in Q[L,L^{-1}]; the forward recursion has rational coefficients. (Or use
D_0 = 1, thm:gam-D.) Even without it, only F_g^cf in Q(zeta)((s)) would be lost, and Theorem A returns
F_g^cf to Q((u)). Repair: cite thm:con-glue at this point.

### F2 (minor, interpretive). "The conifold expansion of F_g" depends on the chosen representative F_g^N
s01_intro_2.tex:29 and s10_main_2.tex:7 speak of "the conifold expansion of the genus-g potential"; it is
defined (def:mai-Fcf) as C_{alpha,beta} of a specific element F_g^N of N with ev(F_g^N) = F_g, and the text
says that injectivity of ev is neither needed nor proved (s10_main.tex:81). Without injectivity of ev on the
l-free subring, another representative of F_g could have a different conifold expansion, so Corollary B is a
statement about F_g^N. The definition is explicit and canonical (explicit row representatives, explicit graph
sum), so nothing is false; but the intro should say "of the representative F_g^N" or prove injectivity.
Remark: injectivity on the l-free subring N_0 follows if the differential Galois group of PF4 over C(q) is
Sp(4) (Zariski-dense monodromy of the hypergeometric operator): an isotropic pair (C_0, I_1) then has
transcendence degree 7 over C(q), log q is transcendental over the Picard-Vessiot field (Sp(4) has no G_a
quotient; Ostrowski-Kolchin), and the 7 generators c_0..c_3, i_0..i_2 are algebraically independent over Q(L).
This is an extra external input, not needed for the stated results.
