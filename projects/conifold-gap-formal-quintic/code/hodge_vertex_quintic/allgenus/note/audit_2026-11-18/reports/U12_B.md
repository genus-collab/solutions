## Findings (all minor)

m1. def:flh-outer (i), s07_comparison.tex L23: "symmetric under the exchange of its two slots" should read
"under the exchange of its two slots together with psi and psi'" (as in def:set-outer L50 and def:tra-edge:
E(psi,psi')^T = E(psi',psi)). The edges used (E_O, E_{R'}, E_Pc) are symmetric only in the combined sense; this is
all the contraction needs.

m2. def:flh-outer (ii), L24: the convergence of the Taylor expansions for a general CohFT over A is attributed to
lem:tra-conv (f), which is formulated only for flat model correlators in the u-adic setting (X_i in H^u_+,
kappa in Q[[u]]^x). For general Omega over A it follows in one line from lem:set-dilaton (i) (the series
sum_b binom(N+b-1,b) eps^b, eps in uQ[[u]], and the edge-degree bound), as in the proof of lem:set-dilaton (iii).
Mis-attribution only.

m3. lem:flh-hat, s07_comparison_2.tex L9, L12: "hat-1 = s1" mixes coordinates. hat-R' acts on hatted lowered
components, in which hat-1 = hat-H^0 has components (0,0,0,5,0); the formula q_{hat R'}(zh) = R'^{-1}(s^4 zh)(-zh s1)
is right because hat-R'^{-1} = R'^{-1}(s^4 zh) Lambda_s^{-1} and Lambda_s^{-1} sends the hatted components of hat-H^0 to
the flat components of s H^0. A reader who inserts the flat vector s1 into hat-R'^{-1} gets s^{-2} q_{R'} and the count
fails by s^{n_l}. One sentence fixes it.

m4. Paragraph after lem:flh-inputs, L63: "the identities at z^0 in (iv) are proved in N (x) Q(zeta) itself" -- only the
unit identity sum_j Delta_j^{-1/2}(Psi_Q)_{aj} = 5 delta_{a3} is; Psi_Q = P_0 Psi^m and the isometry of Psi_Q are
derived in Q(zeta)((s)) from (ii) at z^0. Harmless (no injectivity of ev is needed for either).

m5. thm:flh-main Step 2, L80: "E_{R''} is the edge of [PPZ, Def. 2.2]" -- PPZ's edge is for R(0) = 1 on one space;
R'' maps the colour space to H with R''(0) = Psi_Q. Meant: the formula of def:flh-outer (iii); equivalently
E_{R''} = E_X with X = Psi_Q^{-1} R'' a genuine PPZ edge (computation of lem:flh-frame). Wording.

m6. rmk:flh-fail (i), L100: "Dn = c(z)1 scalar" forces D_0 in Q(zeta)[[z^5]] (from D_i(z) = D_0(zeta^{-i}z)); for
Pc c(z)^{-1} to be a "graded regular gauge" (thm:gau-U is over Q) one also needs c in Q[[z]] (thm:con-glue).
Remark only.

m7. Notation clashes in the unit: E is both the edge bivector and the number of edges (Step 1, L78; lem:flh-hat L16);
N is the numerator in Step 1 and the exponent in lem:flh-hat; the ring letter cA of def:flh-outer is also the local
ring of lem:tra-E and the amplitude letter of lem:gau-wkb. Cosmetic.

No point of this unit is fatal, a gap, or needs a repair.
