## 3. Findings (all minor)

m1. **omega in T_Gamma is the conifold-expanded TFT, not stated explicitly** (minor).
thm:mai-main (s10_main.tex:11), thm:int-main (s01_intro_2.tex:15), def:set-outer (s02_setup_8.tex:46)
take "omega the TFT of def:set-frob", which is defined over Q(zeta)[[q]] (Delta_i^{1/2} = zeta^{4i}C_0/L).
T_Gamma in Q(zeta)((s)) requires Cexp_{alpha,beta}(Delta_i^{1/2}) = zeta^{4i} s varpi_0; cor:mai-gap
(s10_main_2.tex:27) says "with omega expanded in the frame as in def:set-outer", but def:set-outer
does not say it (only the preamble of sec:set-hat says the rows are expanded, and lem:flh-inputs (iv)
and def:set-hat use the expansion implicitly). Repair: add "expanded in the frame (Delta_i^{1/2} -> zeta^{4i} s varpi_0)"
in def:set-outer and the two statements.

m2. **H^j = j copies of H vs the flat basis class H^j** (minor). lem:tra-conv (e2),(e3) (a3_uconv.tex:24-28, 53-61)
write <(y°)^l H^j>_g and "<(y°)^0 H^0>_g := < >_g" with H^j meaning j insertions of H = H^1; but H^j,
j = 0..4, is the flat basis (H^0 = 1 the unit). Same in thm:red-main Step 4 context. Harmless but
confusing at exactly the place where c_g is produced. Repair: write H^{\otimes j} or H^{(j)}.

m3. **"sum over all colourings" for M.omega** (minor). def:set-outer (s02_setup_8.tex:46): "the vertex
tensors are the full tensors of M.omega, so T_Gamma contains the sum over all colourings". M.omega is
not colour-diagonal (M mixes colours), so the colouring language of def:set-graphsum ("Colours") does
not apply literally; what is meant is that the full tensors are used. Repair: drop "colourings".

m4. **What "the conifold expansion of F_g" is** (minor, presentational). cor:int-gap (s01_intro_2.tex:29)
calls F_g^cf "the conifold expansion of the genus-g potential". By def:mai-Fcf it is Cexp of one
