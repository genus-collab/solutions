   gam_2 "compare" only.)

N3 lem:fsp-res (iii), last sentence: alternative closure of U17 F3 by a generating function. For f_0 = P_+(X)
   + P_-(1/X) and E = e^{-f_0/zh}, E^{(j)} = E p_j with Laurent polynomials p_j over k[zh^{-1}], p_0 = 1,
   p_{j+1} = p_j' - zh^{-1} f_0' p_j. In k[zh^{-1}][X^{+-1}][[h]] both sum_j p_j h^j/j! and
   exp(-(f_0(X+h)-f_0(X))/zh) equal 1 at h = 0 and satisfy d_h G = d_X G - zh^{-1} f_0'(X) G, which fixes the
   h-coefficients recursively; substituting h = chi (u-adically convergent) gives
   E(X+chi) = e^{-f_0/zh} e^{-(f_0(X+chi)-f_0)/zh}, the convention of (ii). (Same conclusion as U17 F3.)
