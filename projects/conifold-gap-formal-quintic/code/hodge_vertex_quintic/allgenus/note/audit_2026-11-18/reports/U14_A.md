hypotheses. I re-derived every displayed formula on which the argument depends and found no gap.

point and the formal residue at the pole) are applied to one and the same decomposition of the hatted
1-form (lem:con-red), the model identities (lem:con-S2, lem:con-id (a), the model residues) are correct,
