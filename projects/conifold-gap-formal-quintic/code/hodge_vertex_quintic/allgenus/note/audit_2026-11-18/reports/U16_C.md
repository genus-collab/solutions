## Overall verdict

The unit is mathematically sound. Every proof was checked step by step and every displayed formula on
which an argument depends was re-derived by hand; the key identities were also checked independently by
small computations (below). Nothing found affects Theorem A, Corollary B, Theorem C or Theorem Gamma. The
findings are all minor: one statement (lem:fsp-param (i)) is false as literally worded ("subring" must be
"Q-subalgebra"), but every use is over a Q-algebra; one functional (E_{zC} with a q-dependent covariance on
R_n(A_0)[[q]], and with it S_q[F;b] and SP_c[F;b] for amplitudes whose z-order is not bounded below
uniformly in q) is used in cor:fsp-movebase, lem:fsp-pg(iii) (q-version), lem:fsp-dq and lem:fsp-generic
without being defined; the obvious definition works and all proofs go through with it. The rest are
imprecisions.

All uses of the unit's lemmas elsewhere in the manuscript were checked against their hypotheses
(list at the end); all are within hypotheses.

## Findings (all minor)

M1 (minor). E_{zC} for a q-dependent covariance on R_n(A_0)[[q]] is not defined.
 Location: def:fsp-ring, "Parameters" (a_formalsp.tex l.16); used in lem:fsp-pg (iii) last sentence
 (a_formalsp_2.tex l.69, 78), cor:fsp-movebase (a_formalsp_3.tex l.15-31: E_{-z H_c^{-1}}[P] for
 P ∈ R_n(A_0)[[q]], and S_q[F;b], SP_c[F;a] for a ∈ A_0[[x-c_0]]((z))[[q]]), lem:fsp-dq, lem:fsp-generic (iii),
 lem:fsp-param (iv).
 Problem: "coefficientwise in q" makes literal sense only when C (equivalently F, c) is q-free. In
 cor:fsp-movebase the critical point c and H_c depend on q and the amplitude a ∈ A_0[[x-c_0]]((z))[[q]] need not
 be an amplitude over A = A_0[[q]] in the sense of def:fsp-wick (e.g. a = sum_d q^d z^{-d}: no uniform lower
 bound for the z-order), so SP_c[F;a] is not covered by the definition. Likewise the exponential identity in
 the proof of cor:fsp-movebase mixes weight-convergent and q-adically convergent exponentials, for which
 lem:fsp-ring(ii) states the rules only in the weight topology.
 Repair (checked): for P = sum_d q^d P_d, P_d ∈ R_n(A_0), put E_{zC}[P] := sum_d q^d E_{zC}[P_d], where
 E_{zC}[P_d] ∈ A_0[[q]]((z)) ⊂ A_0((z))[[q]]; the sum converges q-adically. On R_n(A) ⊂ R_n(A_0)[[q]] this agrees
 with the original E_{zC} (the coefficient of z^k q^e is in both cases a finite sum over the weight-k monomials).
 Linearity, compatibility with summable sums, A_0((z))[[q]]-linearity and lem:fsp-pg(iii) (prove it for each
 q-free P_d, then sum) all hold; the topology "for each q^d and each weight bound, eventually constant" makes all
 exponentials in the proof converge and the exponential rules hold there. No downstream change.

M2 (minor; statement false as worded). lem:fsp-param (i), a_formalsp_3.tex l.36: "If the Taylor coefficients ...
 lie in a subring A' ⊂ A and det H ∈ A'^x, all coefficients of SP_c[F;a] lie in A'."
 Problem: the universal polynomial of (eq:fsp-univ) has rational coefficients (1/r!, and 1/beta! if Taylor
 coefficients mean derivatives). Counterexample: A' = Z ⊂ A = Q, F = -t^2/2 + t^3, a = 1, det H = -1: [z^1] = 15/2.
 Repair: "Q-subalgebra A' ⊂ A". All uses (s09_gamma_2 l.67 "compare", rational data over Q, A_L, C) are over
 Q-algebras, so nothing downstream changes.

M3 (minor). lem:fsp-param (iv), a_formalsp_3.tex l.42: the hypotheses that c is a non-degenerate critical point
 of F over A and that a ∈ A[[x-c]]((z)) (q-, i.e. eps-free; needed for the weight-finiteness argument) are not
 stated. With them, the proof is complete. The one use (lem:fsp-stirling, Step 2: F = a log x - x at x = a,
 g = log(x/a), amplitude (x/a)^{-1}) satisfies them.

M4 (minor). lem:fsp-dq, last sentence, a_formalsp_3.tex l.55, 59: "For holomorphic data with a holomorphic
 family c(q) the same identities hold for the coefficient functions."
 Problem: in the holomorphic setting S_q[F;a] contains exp((F(c(q)) - F_0(c_0))/z), which has no z-expansion
 with holomorphic coefficients unless F(c(q)) is constant; the clause makes sense only after dividing by the
 symbol [e^{F(c(q))/z}], i.e. as d/dq N + (z^{-1} d_qF(c(q)) - (1/2) d/dq log det H_c) N = SP_{c(q)}[F; z^{-1}d_qF a +
 d_q a] for N = SP_{c(q)}[F;a]. The proof "follows from param (ii) at every point" is a sketch (it works: at each
 q_1 take the formal identity in C((z))[[q-q_1]], divide by the invertible prefactors, and use param (ii)).
 The clause is not used: both uses of lem:fsp-dq (lem:con-S2 over Q[[v-1]], lem:fsp-generic over A_L[[eps]])
 are formal.

M5 (minor). lem:fsp-ring (iii), a_formalsp.tex l.25: "perfect matchings of the multiset {1^{alpha_1},...}" must
 mean matchings of the |alpha| labelled copies (else <t_1^4>_C = 3 C_11^2 would read C_11^2). The recursion and the
 generating function fix the meaning, and lem:gam-deltasrc uses the labelled count correctly.

M6 (minor). lem:fsp-ring (iv) states E_{zC}[fP] = f E_{zC}[P] only for f ∈ A((z)); the A[[y]]((z))-linearity of
 E^t_{zC} (E^t[y^gamma P] = y^gamma E^t[P], extended to summable sums) is used in the proof of lem:fsp-pg (iii)
 and in lem:fsp-fubini ("linearity of E^v over functions of w and z") without statement. It is immediate from the
 definition on monomials.

M7 (minor). cor:fsp-movebase proof, a_formalsp_3.tex l.23: applying lem:fsp-ift needs Hess F(c_0) ∈ GL_n(A);
 only grad F(c_0) ∈ (q)^n is checked. Immediate: Hess F(c_0) = H_0 + O(q), H_0 ∈ GL_n(A_0).
