## Overall verdict

Appendix C and Section 5 are sound. I checked every inference of the proof of lem:tra-conv (all parts a-f)
and of Theorem R / cor:red-can, opened every cited statement and checked its hypotheses at the point of use.
I found no fatal point, no gap, nothing needing repair. What remains are minor imprecisions (listed below):
a loose "depends only on" sentence, the case n = 0 (vertex graph) not formally covered by (b1)-(b4), a
vector-of-components vs. element-of-H^u slip, an unused claim in (d), an informal "in short" in def:red-ML,
and notation overloads. The Model Lemma enters Theorem R exactly in the form def:red-ML (i)-(ii) that
thm:mod-MLK proves (homogeneous elements of K in the graded setting); the hypotheses (Sp), (A), v'(0) != 0,
delta in u^2 Q[[u]] are each used where needed and are supplied for P_can by prop:tra-can / lem:tra-T.

No circularity: the proof of lem:tra-conv uses lem:tra-sum, lem:tra-frames, cor:tra-slice, lem:tra-twoslot,
lem:gau-vanshape, lem:set-modhom, lem:set-dilaton, and (e3) ML_g as a hypothesis; none of these uses
lem:tra-conv in its own proof (lem:tra-T and cor:tra-edges use lem:tra-conv (b3), (c), but lem:tra-conv
does not use them; its last sentence mentioning lem:tra-T (d) and lem:gau-diag is an application remark).

## Findings (all minor)

m1. a3_uconv.tex l.35 (lem:tra-conv): "The parts depend on the following statements only: (b) and (e)
on the homogeneity of lem:set-modhom; (f) on the dilaton equation ..." This list is incomplete: (a), (b2),
(b3), (b4), (e) also depend on lem:tra-frames (b)-(d), cor:tra-slice, lem:tra-twoslot and lem:tra-sum, and
(c) on lem:gau-vanshape. None of these uses lem:tra-conv in its proof, so there is no circularity; the
sentence should read "beyond the results of Section 4 before lem:tra-T". Severity minor.

m2. a3_uconv.tex l.13 and l.21 (lem:tra-conv (b), (b4)): part (b) assumes n >= 1, but (b4) is stated for
"a leg-free stable graph", which includes the vertex graph whose single vertex has n = 0 slots. For that
case the statement is trivially true (and the vertex graph is treated separately in (e)), and (b4) is used
only for edge graphs (thm:red-main Step 3), where n_w >= 1 by connectedness / lem:red-genus. Repair: say
"with at least one edge" in (b4). Severity minor.

m3. s05_reduction.tex l.39 (def:red-ML): "in short, Fbar_h restricted to -z+K equals F^m_h o Pi_0 + Reg_h"
is informal: for h = 0, 1 the functions F^m_0, F^m_1 contain log v and only derivatives of order >= 3,
resp. >= 1, are meaningful, and the identity is between Taylor coefficients at the base point. The precise
content is the preceding sentence; nothing uses the "in short" form. Severity minor.

m4. a3_uconv.tex l.22 (lem:tra-conv (c)): "YC(v(u)) and J^C lie in H^u": YC is a vector of lowered
components, an element of Q((z))[[u]]^5, not of H^u = H (x) Q((z))[[u]]; only J^C = eta^{-1} YC is an
element of H^u. Also "this section" in an appendix means Section 4. Severity minor.

m5. a3_uconv.tex l.23 and l.51 (lem:tra-conv (d)): the first claim, wt(k,b) >= 3/5 for k >= 2, is proved
but never used (grep: only occurrence besides the appendix is the evidence appendix). Harmless. Severity
minor.

m6. Notation overloads: Cont_Gamma in def:red-flatsum (with the d-factors at vertices) vs.
Cont_Gamma[E, q; Omega] of def:set-graphsum (without); D = 3h-3+n in lem:tra-conv vs. the matrix D in
def:tra-dressing (ii) and the series Dn; d (dilaton factor) vs. the derivative symbol. All are explained
locally; no ambiguity in the proofs. Severity minor.

m7. s05_reduction.tex l.58-59 (thm:red-main (b)): "If moreover ML_g holds" suggests that (b) also needs
ML_h for h <= g-1; the proof of (b) (Step 4) uses only ML_g (i), (ii). Harmless. Severity minor.

No fatal point, no gap, no fixable point found in this unit.
