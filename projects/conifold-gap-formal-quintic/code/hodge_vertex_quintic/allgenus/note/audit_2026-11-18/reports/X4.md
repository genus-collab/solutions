## 1. Verdict

Within the scope of this unit (rings, expansions, formal-series legitimacy) I found **no fatal error and no gap**. The
algebraic framework is sound and, in several places, more careful than usual:

* The ring N, its derivation, the evaluation ev and the conifold expansion C_{alpha,beta} are well defined, and C is a
  homomorphism of differential rings (lem:gau-confexp; the only non-formal input, Conc(varpi_0,varpi_1)=0, is
  lem:gau-iso (ii), and I re-checked it numerically).
* Every object to which C is applied is an explicit element of N (x) Q(zeta) (rows: cor:gau-rowsNN; Delta^{1/2}; Psi_Q;
  F_g^N), and the note never transfers to the conifold side an identity known only after ev (symplecticity of R is
  deliberately not transferred; the unit/isometry identities at z^0 are proved in N itself; the closing relation of the
  cyclic system on the conifold side is recovered from Q D = P_can Q^m, not from ev).
* No infinite sum over powers of z with growing negative powers of s is ever formed as a single element: everything
  carrying zhat = z/s^4 is a power series in z over Q(zeta)((s)) (coefficient of z^n in s^{-4n}Q(zeta)[[u]]), and every
  graph contraction is a finite sum; where a flat/hatted relabelling mixes s-powers (lem:con-relab, lem:con-key,
  prop:con-res) finiteness of the fibres is proved in both orders.
* The fractional powers of s and the coefficients in Q(zeta) disappear from T_Gamma for a structural reason: T_Gamma = F_Gamma
  (thm:flh-main), an identity in Q(zeta)((s)) whose right side lies in Q((u)) because the flat term is a finite sum of
  rational model correlators C v(u)^e with e in Z (lem:set-modhom (c), lem:tra-F) and of entries of the rational gauge
  P_can (thm:gau-U (d)). The s-adic topology of Q(zeta)((s)) restricts to the u-adic topology of Q((u)), so the
  convergent Taylor expansions of thm:flh-main Steps 3-4 are compared correctly.
* lem:tra-conv is used within its hypotheses at each use (cor:tra-edges: (c); lem:tra-T: (b3),(c); lem:tra-F: (d),(f1);
  thm:red-main: (b2),(b4),(d),(e) with v'(0)=1 and delta in u^2 Q[[u]]; thm:flh-main Step 4: (f1) with kappa = d,
  d(0)=1; def:flh-outer (ii)/lem:flh-dil rely on lem:set-dilaton (iii), which is stated for Q(zeta)((s))).

What remains are **minor** points (Section 3): a mis-citation in the Galois descent of F_g^N (the rationality of the colour-0
representatives needs thm:con-glue, not lem:gau-colrows); the representative-dependence of "the conifold expansion of

### m2. "The conifold expansion of F_g" depends on the chosen representative; ev is in fact injective
*Location.* def:mai-Fcf (s10_main.tex l.61-81, last sentence "Whether ev is injective on N is neither needed nor
proved"); s02_setup_8.tex l.52; s07_comparison_2.tex l.63; s01_intro_2.tex l.29,36.
*Point.* Theorem A and Corollary B are proved for the specific representatives fixed in cor:gau-rowsNN and the specific
element F_g^N; as long as ker(ev) is not known to lie in ker(C_{alpha,beta}), "the conifold expansion of F_g" is a
statement about that representative, not about the function F_g in Q[[q]]. (Within the proof nothing goes wrong:
C(F_g^N) does not depend on the choice of the symmetrised edge formula, because C(R~) is symplectic by D = 1.)
*Remark that closes the interpretive question.* ev is injective. Indeed injectivity is equivalent to the algebraic
independence over Q of L, l and C_0, DC_0, D^2C_0, D^3C_0, I~_1, DI~_1, D^2I~_1. l is an indeterminate over Q[[q]], and L is
algebraic over Q(q), so it suffices that the seven series C_0^{(j)} (j<=3), I~^{(j)} (j<=2) are algebraically
independent over C(q). The differential Galois group of PF4 over C(q) is Sp_4(C) (Beukers-Heckman: Zariski closure of the
hypergeometric monodromy; Schlesinger for Fuchsian equations). The field generated by C_0, I_1 and their derivatives
is the fixed field of the stabiliser of an isotropic pair, of dimension 10-7 = 3, so it has transcendence degree 7;
log q is not in the Picard-Vessiot field (Sp_4 has no quotient G_a), so adjoining it raises the degree to 8; hence
C_0^{(0..3)}, I~^{(0..3)} generate a field of transcendence degree >= 7, and as I~^{(3)} is rational in the others
(concomitant relation) the seven are independent. So F_g^N is the unique preimage of F_g, and F_g^cf is canonical.
Suggest a remark (or a rewording of Cor. B as a statement about F_g^N). Confidence in the injectivity argument: high,
but it uses external theorems; the note correctly does not rely on it.

### m3. What is proved is a formal change of frame, not a statement about the analytic continuation of F_g
*Location.* Abstract (main.tex l.18: "Near the conifold point ... we factorise the R-matrix as R = R' M_0(tau zhat)",
"is regular at the conifold point", "the conifold gap for the formal quintic"); s01_intro.tex l.18, l.26; s01_intro_2.tex
l.27-36; s01_intro_3.tex l.31.
*Assessment.* C_{alpha,beta} sends the large-radius isotropic pair (C_0, I_1) to the conifold pair (varpi_0, varpi_1)
with rational (alpha,beta) and L to s^{-1}. It is not analytic continuation: the analytic continuation of C_0 to a
neighbourhood of u=0 is a period with a non-zero component along y_3 = varpi_1 log u + rho_3 (Conc(C_0, varpi_1) != 0,
because the vanishing period continued to large radius has a (log q)^3 component; equivalently the D0 and D6 central
charges pair to +-1), with transcendental coefficients, and I_1 does not continue to varpi_1; L continues with monodromy.
So the analytic continuation of F_g = ev(F_g^N) is in general not an element of Q((s)). What the note proves is purely
algebraic: the element F_g^N in N, evaluated on the jets of the isotropic pair (varpi_0, varpi_1) instead of (C_0, I_1),
lies in c_g t_c^{2-2g} + Q[[u]]. This is the standard B-model meaning ("holomorphic limit in the conifold frame"), and
it is the notion of CGYZ26 (Def. 1.2 there, as recalled in b_signs_2.tex), so Corollary B is the right statement;
but the abstract and introduction should say explicitly that the "expansion at the conifold point" is this formal
frame change applied to the polynomial representative, and that no statement about the analytic function F_g near
u=0 is made ("What is not claimed" (2) says "away from u=0", which does not cover this). Similarly "Near the conifold
point we factorise the R-matrix" means: the frame-expanded rows factorise. Not a gap; a precision of claim.
*Side remark (strengthening).* The restriction to rational (alpha,beta) is not needed: for fixed g each u- and s-coefficient
of C_{alpha,beta}(F_g^N) - c_g t_c^{2-2g} is a polynomial in (alpha,beta) (varpi_0(0)=1, D t_c(0)=-1 make c_0, W map to
units whose inverses have polynomial coefficients), and it vanishes on Q^2 for the coefficients of s^k, k<0, and of
fractional powers; by Zariski density Corollary B holds for all (alpha,beta) in C^2 (the same for each T_Gamma).

1. *N, ev, C_{alpha,beta}, compatibility with D.* Correct (Section 2).
