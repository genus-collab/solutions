## Overall verdict

I found no fatal problem and no gap in this unit. Every statement is proved as stated, by valid inferences from
statements earlier in the note (or from (QRR), (M), (G0) through Section 2), and each cited statement is used within its
hypotheses. The forward references (cor:gau-rowsNN inside thm:gau-R0 (iv); thm:gam-D in def:gau-Fg) are not circular:
cor:gau-rowsNN (i) uses only thm:gau-R0 (iii), lem:gau-colrows, lem:gau-conc and prop:gau-quintic; thm:gam-D uses
thm:con-glue, thm:con-Pb, prop:gam-hatflat, thm:gam-main and cor:gau-colours, none of which uses (F_g) or thm:flh-main.
The findings below are all minor: implicit routine steps, imprecise wording, and one mis-citation outside the unit
of a fact the unit does not supply.

## Verdicts per statement

### lem:gau-poly (s03_gauge_7.tex 3-16) -- VERIFIED
(i) r_n is built from ph_0..ph_{n-1} (polynomials by induction) by theta_u, multiplication by (1-u), u^j and constants,
so it is a polynomial. lem:gau-HPF (iii) (valid as all ph_n lie in Q[[u]]; the factor (1-u) of lem:gau-HPF (ii) is a unit)
gives [u^k]ph_n = 0 for k > deg r_n with 5k != 4n. So deg ph_n <= max(deg r_n, 4n/5).
(ii) z^m = u^{4m/5} zh^m is killed by (4/5) theta_zh - theta_u, so every O_e, and hence the left side of HPF, commutes with
multiplication by a series in z. The fractional-power argument is correct: the coefficient of zh^{n_0} is
(element of Q[[u]]) + k_{n_0} s^{4n_0}, and Q((s)) = (+)_{r=0}^{4} s^r Q((u)) with 4n_0 not divisible by 5.
(iii) The induction on N is correct: both sides have the same r_N, so (1-u)(4N-5theta_u)delta = 0 and delta = delta_0 u^{4m}
when N = 5m, which is absorbed by z^{5m}Phi = u^{4m} zh^{5m}(1+O(zh)). The formula
f_m(Phi k) = sum_{m'=0}^{m} k_{5m'} f_{m-m'}(Phi) (f_0 = 1) was re-derived; it is unitriangular in the k_{5m}.
The final sentence follows because (iii) produces a Q[[u]]-solution for every choice of free coefficients.

### lem:gau-wkb (s03_gauge_7.tex 18-36) -- VERIFIED (minor F3, F4)
(i) The z^0 part w^5 - 1 - q(5w)^5 = L^5(1-5^5 q) - 1 = 0. The z^1 coefficients and T^w_1 were re-derived by hand and
checked by machine (wkb.wl).
(ii) ker T^w_1 = k L (L is a unit; the constants of D are k on k[[q]] and on k((s))). The induction B_n = k_n L is correct,
and k is unique since A is a unit. In the rational colour-0 case, k_n = B_n/L lies in Q[[q]] (or Q((s))) and in k, so in Q.
(iii) sigma_i o (zD+L) = zeta^{-i}(zD+zeta^i L) o sigma_i, and the homogeneity of degree 5 of PF5 (apart from its constant
term) gives sigma_i o PF5^{(0)} = PF5^{(i)} o sigma_i.

### lem:gau-colmod (s03_gauge_7.tex 38-50) -- VERIFIED
T^{(i)}_k[G(zeta^{-i}t)] = zeta^i [T_k G](zeta^{-i}t). The five relations hold, the closing one using zeta^{5i} = 1, and
uniqueness is prop:set-modrows (b) (pivot 4n zeta^{4i}). (ii) follows from prop:set-modrows (b) by stripping the formal
exponential. (iii) holds for every v: varsigma does not depend on z. Checked by machine (tsys.wl).

### lem:gau-colrows (s03_gauge_7.tex 52-63) -- VERIFIED (minor F1)
Step 1: [z^0](L Phi_i) = C_0 eta(1, e~_i)/zeta^i = L, with eta(1,e~_i) = Delta_i^{-1/2} = zeta^i L/C_0, consistent with the

### thm:gau-R0 (s03_gauge_8.tex 3-31) -- VERIFIED (minor F2, F5, F6)
(i) The chain rule holds in Q((s)): varsigma(u)^5 = v(u), so 5 varsigma^4 d_u varsigma = v'. U_i(v(u)) = -5/4 zeta^i u^{4/5}/tau
= u_i - u_i^c (lem:set-canon), so the gauge equation gives z d_u X + (d_u u_i) X = A X. Multiplying by -(1-u) gives all
five relations (zD + zeta^i L)X_a = kappa_a X_{a+1}, the closing relation included. In the hatting, the exponent of s in
entry (b+k,b) is 4k+nu_b-nu_{b+k} = 5 mu(b,k), with mu in Z (nu_a = -a mod 5) and mu >= (4k-4)/5 (nu in {-1..3}).
This agrees with lem:mai-Ghat.
(ii) L Phi^can = varpi_0 (P_can Q^{m,(0)})_0 since nu_0 = 0. cor:gau-cycsys (ii) applies: R = Q((s))((z)) extends
(Q((u)), D), z is invertible, (y_0,y_1) = (varpi_0,varpi_1) is admissible in thm:gau-cyc, and its kappa is that of
prop:gau-quintic. lem:gau-HPF (i) then gives HPF. The coefficients lie in Q[[u]] (P_can rational, tau^{(1-nu_b)/4} and
varpi_0 in Q[[u]], Fs_b rational). ph_0^can = 1 by lem:gau-diag (hypotheses (N0) and p_{0,1}(0) = 1 hold for P_can).
lem:gau-poly (i) applies.
(iii) Q(zeta)[L^{+-1}] with DL = (L^6-L)/5 is a differential ring. Both embeddings are injective differential
homomorphisms (L(q) - 1 has q-order 1). q = (1-L^{-5})/5^5 lies in Q[L^{-1}] for both embeddings, so PF5^{(0)} has
coefficients in Q[L^{+-1}][z] and the transport of the WKB equations is valid. lem:gau-wkb (ii) is used within its
hypotheses. A^act_n is the image of a unique Laurent polynomial.
(iv) Row 0: A^act D_0/varpi_0 = A^can/varpi_0 = L Phi^can/varpi_0 = (P_can Q^{m,(0)})_0. Rows 1..4 agree by the forward
relations, which hold on the left by the construction of the representatives and prop:gau-quintic (i), and on the right
by (i). The closing relation on the left follows since D_0 is a unit constant in u. No circularity (see Overall verdict).

### cor:gau-rowsNN (s03_gauge_8.tex 33-44) -- VERIFIED (minor F4)
The logq-free subring of N is D-stable: D i_2 = i_3 is solved from the concomitant relation, linear in i_3 with
coefficient c_0 J_{03} = -u c_0, a unit. The logq part is Conc(c_0,c_0) = sum c_a J_ab c_b = 0 since J is antisymmetric
(explicit matrix in lem:gau-conc (iii)). The representatives evaluate correctly: ev of the representative of
(C_1 kappa^LR_a)^{-1} is right, and colour i satisfies the colour-i forward relations in N. (ii) and (iii) are immediate.
Rows' representatives are fixed by the automorphism of def:set-confexp (it fixes L, c_j, W and commutes with D).

### cor:gau-colours (s03_gauge_8.tex 46-66) -- VERIFIED
Rot_i(PY) = (Rot_i P)(Rot_i Y), Rot_i(Y d(z)) = Rot_i(Y) d(zeta^{-i}z), Rot_i P_can = P_can (graded, prop:gau-graded (iv)),
Rot_i Q^{m,(0)} = Q^{m,(i)} (lem:gau-colmod (iii)), Rot_i Q^{(0)} = Q^{(i)} for the representatives (cor:gau-rowsNN (iii)).
Uniqueness: Q(zeta)((s))[[z]] is a domain and Q^{(i)}_0 != 0 (constant term zeta^i s^{-1}/varpi_0).

### def:gau-Fg (s03_gauge_8.tex 68-71) -- VERIFIED
[z^n]D_i = zeta^{-in}[z^n]D_0 gives D == 1 mod z^{3g-2}. It is used as the hypothesis of thm:flh-main exactly in this
form (Step 1 of thm:flh-main shows that only R' mod z^{3g-2} enters). The forward appeal to thm:gam-D is not circular.

## Findings (all minor)

### F1 (minor). The passage from "e^{u_i/z} z L Phi_i solves PF5" to "PF5^{(i)}(L Phi_i) = 0" is left implicit.
Location: sections/s03_gauge_7.tex l.20 (definition of WKB amplitude, parenthesis), l.62 (lem:gau-colrows Step 1);
sections/s03_gauge_8.tex l.28 (thm:gau-R0 (iii), "A^act is a colour-0 WKB amplitude by cor:set-cyclicPF5 (b)").
lem:gau-wkb defines a WKB amplitude by PF5^{(i)} A = 0. The cited cor:set-cyclicPF5 (b) states only that the formal product
e^{u_i/z} z L Phi_i solves PF5, in an unspecified differential algebra. Here u_i lies in Q(zeta)[[q]][logq] and the formal
exponential is a symbol. Deducing PF5^{(i)}(L Phi_i) = 0 needs the exponential to be a non-zero-divisor.
Repair (checked): work in A := Q(zeta)[[q]]((z))[E, E^{-1}] with D E := z^{-1} zeta^i L E. This is a differential
Q[[q]]-algebra with z constant. v = R(z) e~_i E solves z d_T v = H*v by def:set-rows (b), so PF5(C_0 v_0) = 0 by
cor:set-cyclicPF5 (b). Since PF5(E f) = E PF5^{(i)}(f) and E is a unit, PF5^{(i)}(C_0 Q^{(i)}_0) = 0, that is
PF5^{(i)}(L Phi_i) = 0. Alternatively, use prop:set-cyclicLR (iii) and conjugate the operator identity of
cor:set-cyclicPF5 (b).

### F2 (minor). "A^act_n in Q(zeta)[L,L^{-1}]" is an identification through an injective map.
Location: s03_gauge_8.tex l.13 (thm:gau-R0 (iii)), l.28.
A^act_n is a priori in Q(zeta)[[q]]. The statement means that it is the image of a unique Laurent polynomial under
L -> (1-5^5 q)^{-1/5}, and this Laurent polynomial is what (iv) and cor:gau-rowsNN expand. The proof establishes this
correctly (injectivity), but the statement should say so.

### F3 (minor). In lem:gau-wkb, the derivation D on k((s)) and the element q are not specified.
Location: s03_gauge_7.tex l.20.
The derivation should be D = -(1-s^5)/(5s^4) d/ds, the k-linear extension of lem:gau-confexp, with q := (1-s^5)/5^5.
"DL = (L^6-L)/5" determines D only among continuous k-derivations. Part (iii) also needs zeta in k; this is implicit,
because i != 0 forces k >= Q(zeta).

### F4 (minor). Colour-0 representatives are rational only after thm:con-glue; def:mai-Fcf mis-cites lem:gau-colrows.
Location: s03_gauge_8.tex l.13, l.36 (thm:gau-R0 (iii), cor:gau-rowsNN (i): the representatives lie in N (x) Q(zeta),
because A^act_n is in Q(zeta)[L^{+-1}] and D_0 is in Q(zeta)[[z]]). Downstream: sections/s10_main.tex l.81
(justification of def:mai-Fcf): "By lem:gau-colrows, Q^{(i)}_{a,n} = zeta^{i(a+1-n)} Q^{(0)}_{a,n} with Q^{(0)}_{a,n} in N".
Neither lem:gau-colrows nor cor:gau-rowsNN shows that the colour-0 representatives lie in N. That needs
A^act_n in Q[L^{+-1}], which follows from D_0 in Q[[z]] (thm:con-glue) and A^can_n in Q[L^{+-1}], or from D_0 = 1
(thm:gam-D). Repair: cite thm:con-glue there, or add the sentence to cor:gau-rowsNN. No result depends on it: the
proof of cor:mai-gap gets F_g^cf = sum T_Gamma, and its rationality, from Theorem A without Galois descent.

### F5 (minor). Ambiguous referent in thm:gau-R0 (i), and frame dependence suppressed in the notation.
Location: s03_gauge_8.tex l.11: "this is a power series in u whose coefficients are power series in zh". It is true
both for G^ (whose u^n-coefficients are in fact polynomials in zh, lem:mai-Ghat) and for the product G^ R^m(tau zh).
Location: l.13, l.70: Phi^can, A^can and D_0 depend a priori on the frame (alpha,beta) through P_can and varpi_0.
The notation and def:gau-Fg suppress this. It is harmless because thm:gam-D proves D_0 = 1 in every frame.

### F6 (minor, outside the unit). Wording in s02_setup.tex l.14.
The text says that lem:gau-colrows and cor:gau-rowsNN are "proved later, independently of this section". Both use
Section 2: def:set-rows, prop:set-cyclicLR, cor:set-cyclicPF5 and (QRR) (thm:set-qrr). The intended and correct
claim is that their proofs do not use the definitions that cite them (def:set-hat, def:set-outer). I checked that no
circularity arises: def:set-confexp defines N and the conifold expansion C without using cor:gau-rowsNN.
