## Overall verdict

The unit is sound. Every displayed formula on which the argument depends was re-derived by hand, and the three
places where the unit leans on its reading of the quoted inputs (Q) (the index convention of DNOPS (2-31), the
normalisation of the formal Laplace reading, the sign of the dilaton leaves) were tested independently:

1. Genus 0, three points (by hand): the TR correlator omega_{0,3} = -(1/5) sum_b vs_b^{-2} prod_l dp_l/(p_l-vs_b)^2
   computed from the kernel of conv:mod-kernel agrees with (-5)^{-1} sum <H^aH^bH^c>_0 dxi_a dxi_b dxi_c
   (TFT values vs_i, xi~^i = -1/(vs_i(p-vs_i))).
2. Genus 1, one point (work/U09/g1check.wl, v=1): omega_{1,1} computed directly from the recursion with the kernel K_b
   agrees to 20 digits, at three test points, with sum_a [<tau_0 H^a>_1 dxi_{a,0} + <tau_1 H^a>_1 dxi_{a,1}], the
   correlators computed from the Givental graph sum with R = M0(z) (leg term, loop term with 1/|Aut|=1/2, translation
   term T_2 = M_1 1 with int_{M_{1,2}} psi_2^2 = 1/24). With the opposite dilaton sign the two sides disagree.
   So the dilaton-sign convention adopted in (Q) and in prop:mod-Rtr(ii) is the correct one, at least at genus 1,
   and the factor (-5)^{h-1} is right at h=0,1 (for h>=2 it follows from the lambda^{h-1} rescaling, checked by hand).
3. Index convention of (2-31) (work/U09/leaf.wl): M0 has a non-symmetric second coefficient (antisymmetric part of
   M_2 of size ~0.145, work/U09/m2.wl), so R versus R^T matters from degree 2 on. With the manuscript's reading
   R^{-1}(psi)^j_i = M0(-psi)_{ij} and PPZ legs, the leaf of a vertex of colour j,
   sum_i sum_k [psi^{D-k}] R^{-1}(psi)_{ji} d(D_x^k xi~^i), has poles only at vs_j for D = 1,2,3 (as the structure of
   TR -- residues of the kernel at vs_j -- requires), whereas the transposed reading produces principal parts at the
   other branch points for D = 2,3. This supports the reading.
4. (2-32) holds identically at the level of forms: with G := sum_k Delta_k^{-1/2} xi^k one has
   G dx_kappa = (1/5) sum_l (sum_k vs_k^{l-2}) p^{2-l} dp = dp = dy, so -theta^{-1} L[dG] = L[G dx_kappa] = L[dy]
   by lem:mod-laplace(i). This is a two-line alternative to the proof of prop:mod-Rtr(ii) and confirms the index
   structure "sum over the lower index k at the fixed point vs_i".
5. Leg dictionary (work/U09/ld.wl, v = 2): eq:mod-LD verified numerically for all b,a in Z/5 and k,d in {0,1,2}
   (75 x 5 pairings incl. log branches), and D_x phi^b_{k+1} = -phi^b_k for k <= 2.

No fatal point, no gap. Five minor points (definitional scope, stated generality, implicit specialisation).
The external inputs themselves (the content of DOSS Thm 4.1, DNOPS (2-30)-(2-35), Prop. 4.3(1), the sentence
before Thm 8.1, EO Thms 4.2/4.6) cannot be checked from the page; they are declared in (Q) and used within the
hypotheses as stated there (compact rational curve, simple branch points, dy != 0 there, Bergman kernel, distinct
critical values, R symplectic, (2-32)).

## Findings (all minor)

### F1 (minor) -- "TR leg" and q_phi used outside the class for which they are defined
Location: s06_model.tex l.57-60 (def:mod-ancvec), l.71 (second form of thm:mod-tr); s06_model_4.tex l.24
(def:mod-legs), l.34 (prop:mod-LD: "the TR leg of v^m z^k H^b ... is -rho_b^{-1} v^m phi^b_k").
def:mod-ancvec defines q_phi and "TR leg" only for phi in L = Q[v][p] + Q(v)[x], phi = -log p, and Q[v]-linear
combinations; def:mod-pair defines residue pairings with omega_{h,n} only for these. But most phi^b_k lie outside
this class: e.g. phi^1_1 = I(log p) = -x log p + p^4/16 - v/p, and phi^0_k (k >= 2) contain p^4 log p. So the
sentence of prop:mod-LD calling -rho_b^{-1} v^m phi^b_k "the TR leg" of v^m z^k H^b, and the identity
q_{phi^b_k} = -rho_b z^k H^b, use q_phi outside its definition, and the second form of thm:mod-tr is not stated for
such legs. The pairings <phi^b_k, dxi_{a,d}> themselves are well defined (eq:mod-LD defines them by residues with
any branch; Step 1 proves branch independence), and the later use (s06_model_10 Step 1) applies thm:mod-tr only to
legs -Lambda(Y) in L (Y in K_0) and to -log p. Repair: either call these "formal legs" or extend def:mod-pair to
the span of p^j (log p)^l by residues at the branch points with any branch (independent of the branch by Step 1 of
prop:mod-LD and lem:mod-xfun); eq:mod-tr then gives the second form for them verbatim.

### F2 (minor) -- stated generality of kappa
Location: s06_model_2.tex l.2 versus l.32, l.38, l.18.
The preamble says that throughout lem:mod-laplace, lem:mod-scale, prop:mod-Rtr kappa is a non-zero rational; but
lem:mod-scale(i) is stated for constants kappa, mu (and applied in (ii) with kappa = k^4, mu = k complex), and
lem:mod-laplace(iii) for k in C^x. Harmless (the proofs are for arbitrary non-zero constants); the preamble should
say "kappa in C^x; the cases used are 4 and 1".

### F3 (minor) -- prop:set-modrows(b) applied over C
Location: s06_model_3.tex l.13 (Step 3 of the proof of prop:mod-Rtr).
prop:set-modrows(b) and its uniqueness are stated for F_b in Q(zeta)[[t]]; here F_b = eps_b J_{nu_b}(1; vs^4 theta)
has complex coefficients ((-5 kappa)^{-1/2} times elements of Q(zeta), by the expansion of p^m dp in w). The
computation (z d_v of e^{U/z} vs^c F(t)) and the pivot argument are formal and hold verbatim over C; one sentence
would close it.

### F4 (minor) -- free energy of a general curve; f in Q(v)[X]
Location: s06_model.tex l.20; s06_model_4.tex l.4.
conv:mod-kernel defines F^TR_h by residues of the multivalued Phi_{ydx} for a general curve; independence of the
branch/constant needs zero residues of omega_{h,1}, proved only for the model curve (lem:mod-xfun), which is the
only curve used (cor:mod-trF says so). In lem:mod-xfun "f in Q(v)[X], v != 0 fixed" tacitly needs the coefficients
regular at the fixed v. Both harmless.

### F5 (minor) -- complex v versus the formal root of def:set-modcohft
Location: s06_model.tex l.62-71; s06_model_3.tex l.32 (Step 3).
Omega^mod_v is defined with a formal fifth root vs of v; thm:mod-tr is a statement for complex v != 0, and Step 3
identifies the frozen basis (branch points vs_j = zeta^{-j} vs for the vs fixed in def:mod-curve) with tilde e_j of
TFT^m_v defined with the same vs. The specialisation of the formal root to that complex root is implicit; it is
harmless (correlators are polynomial in the coefficients; by lem:set-modhom they are C v^e with e in Z when C != 0,
so they depend only on v), but should be said.
