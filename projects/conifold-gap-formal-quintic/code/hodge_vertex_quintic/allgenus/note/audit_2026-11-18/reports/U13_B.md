## 4. Adversarial checks that came out clean

1. Rings. c is defined at complex L in C_*, then read as an element of A_L. The two readings agree by
   lem:fsp-univ (i) with the evaluation A_L -> C at L in C_* (a ring homomorphism since L, L^5-1 != 0).
   The constant S_0(L) and the factor A_St(L,0) (logarithms, square roots) never enter the normalised
   series (lem:fsp-const, linearity); the Taylor data used are in A_L.
2. Branches. Changing the branches on D_L changes S_0 by the constant 2 pi i sum zeta^j n_j and
   exp(S_1) by +-1; both invisible in SPh. Checked.
3. z against -z. The sign of every odd term is pinned by the machine checks: c_1 = -3L^4/20 gives
   ph_1 = -3/20, the value forced by HPF (lem:gau-HPF (iii)); with z -> -z one would get +3/20.
4. lem:con-form (d): the parity of e (six odd integers) is not needed; criticality alone forces e = 0.
   The reflection is applied with log y_j := log(zeta^j - sigma) - log z and log(-y_j) :=
   log(sigma - zeta^j) - log z, which is the convention of def:fsp-stirling; their difference is constant
   on D_L because D_L contains no zeta^j.
5. lem:fsp-generic (iii) used in Step 1 requires b in A_sigma[[z]], not merely in A_L[[t]][[z]]; this is
   why chi must lie in Q[sigma,z]. In Step 2, the only chi that occur under (zD+L) are in Q[sigma,z]
   (q is applied last, on the left). Checked.
6. lem:fsp-transl (b): the coefficientwise polynomiality in the homotopy parameter holds for p = z,
   the derivative identity dB/ds = sum p_i(d_i B + z^{-1}(d_i F) B) re-derived, lem:fsp-ibp applies over A_L[s].
7. lem:fsp-generic (i)-(iii) re-derived: sigma_eps' = delta(sigma_eps); the Hessian ratio times the
   half-density ratio is (delta r)(sigma_eps)/(delta r)(L) = sigma_eps^2/L^2, root sigma_eps/L; the
   epsilon-derivative gives (L/z)N + (DL/L)N + DN = N^sigma/z.
8. Degenerate order n=0: PF5^L(L) = L(L^5 - 1 - 5^5 q L^5) = 0. Checked.
9. V: each u-order finite; terms in z^{-1}Q[z^{-1}]; the k=0 term correctly absent.

## 5. Findings (all minor)

m1. s08_connection_4.tex, l. 79 (proof of thm:con-Pa (i), Step 3, eq:con-bz). "we prove (con-bz) there
   [in A_L(zeta)[[t]][[z]]], factor by factor". The six factors (e^{sigma log q/z}, Gamma_St(1+5 sigma/z),
   1/Gamma_St(1+(sigma-zeta^j)/z)) and two of the multipliers (prod_k (5 sigma/z + k), which has z^{-5},
   and z/(sigma+z-zeta^j)) are formal Stirling expressions with the symbols log q, log(5 sigma),
   log(sigma - zeta^j), log z and negative powers of z; they are not elements of A_L(zeta)[[t]][[z]].
   Only the product of the multipliers, q prod(5 sigma + kz)/((sigma+z)^5 - 1), and the ratio
   exp((S(L+t+z)-S(L+t))/z) A_St(L+t+z,z)/A_St(L+t,z) live there. Repair (checked): take logarithms;
   lem:fsp-stirfe at Y = 5 sigma/z + k (1/Y = z/(5 sigma + kz) in z A_L[[t,z]]) and at
   Y = 1 + (sigma - zeta^j)/z (1/Y = z/(sigma + z - zeta^j) in z A_L(zeta)[[t,z]]) gives each
   difference "log(translate) - log(original) - log(multiplier)" = 0, the symbol parts cancelling
   (log z between numerator and denominator, and log q + 5 log(5L) - sum log(L - zeta^j) = S'(L) = 0
   in total); what remains is an identity in A_L(zeta)[[t,z]]. Machine check to z^7: bz.wl.
m2. s08_connection_3.tex, l. 50 (prop:gam-rational, step (5)). "The Taylor coefficients of order at
   least 1 of S_0, S_1, S_{2k} are rational in sigma and carry no branch": the order-1 coefficient of
   S_0 is S_0'(sigma) = lambda + 5 log(5 sigma) - sum log(zeta^j - sigma), which carries branches (its
   value at L is 0 only by the choice of lambda, as the next sentence says). Read "of order at least 2 of
   S_0 and at least 1 of S_1, S_{2k}". No consequence.
m3. s08_connection_3.tex l. 21 and s08_connection_4.tex l. 61. c(z;L) = SPh_L[S_0;A_St] and
   c<chi> := SPn_L[S_0; chi A_St]/A_St(L,0) divide by A_St(L,0) = e^{S_1(L)}, which is not in A_L (a square
   root of 5L/(1-L^5)); over A_L the definition must be read as SPn_L[S_0; chi A_St/A_St(L,0)], whose
   amplitude has Taylor data in A_L. Equivalent by linearity; notational only.
m4. s08_connection_3.tex l. 61 (lem:con-form (a)): "the critical points are x^5 = u" is stated without a
   ring (log(1-x^5) = log(1-u) as formal series); it is used only through (c), X = 1 critical over Q[[u]],
   which is proved. Informal only.
