## 1. The conifold symbol

**Definition.** In the normalised canonical basis e~_i = Delta_i^{1/2} e_i the rows Q_a^{(i)}(z) = eta(H^a, R(z) e~_i) = Sum_n Q^{(i)}_{a,n} z^n have val_s Q^{(i)}_{a,n} = -4n + c_a - 1, c = (0,-1,3,2,1), independently of the colour, with NON-ZERO leading coefficient for all 5 x 5 x 7 triples (a, i, n), n <= 6 (checked; no accidental vanishing). Write zhat := L^4 z = z/s^4. Then Q_a^{(i)}(z) = s^{c_a - 1} (R^_a^{(i)}(zhat) + O(s^5)), and the CONIFOLD SYMBOL is the matrix of leading coefficients

    R^_{a,n}^{(i)} := [s^{-4n + c_a - 1}] Q^{(i)}_{a,n},    R^_a^{(i)}(zhat) = Sum_n R^_{a,n}^{(i)} zhat^n,

one number per (a, n, i): the limit s -> 0 of the R-matrix in the rescaled Givental variable. Its identity row has a clean independent description: f(zhat) := R^_0^{(0)}(zhat) is the series of top L-degree coefficients of the WKB rows psi_{0,n}/L (top degree exactly 4n, rows_check.log), f = 1 - (3/20) zhat + (9/800) zhat^2 + (11571/80000) zhat^3 - (2415609/6400000) zhat^4 + (47735919/128000000) zhat^5 + (52989974037/25600000000) zhat^6 + ..., the first five non-trivial coefficients being exactly those listed in rows_check.log and f_6 confirmed at both primes from that log's residues.

**Symplecticity.** On the eta-pairs (a, b) with a + b = 3 mod 5 one has c_a + c_b = 2, so the prefactors s^{c_a - 1} s^{c_b - 1} cancel and the symplecticity of Q in s-series implies Sum_ab R^_a^{(i)}(zhat) eta^{ab} R^_b^{(j)}(-zhat) = delta_ij as constant series: checked through zhat^6.

**Covariance.** From P_a^{(i)}(z) = zeta^{ia} P_a^{(0)}(zeta^{-i} z) and Delta_i^{1/2} = zeta^{4i} x (the s-scaling carries no phase), Q^{(i)}_{a,n} = zeta^{i(a+1-n)} Q^{(0)}_{a,n}, hence

    R^_a^{(i)}(zhat) = zeta^{i(a+1)} R^_a^{(0)}(zeta^{-i} zhat).

The phase zeta^{ia} of the P-rows does NOT hold for the normalised rows (tested: false); the extra zeta^i is Delta_i^{-1/2}. In the flat basis, R^_{ab,n} := Sum_i R^_{a,n}^{(i)} R^_{b,0}^{(i)} vanishes unless a + b = n - 2 mod 5: zhat^n shifts the flat degree by n mod 5, the Z/5 remnant of the degree grading of the formal quintic.

**Teleman's colour-colour form; diagonal or not.** Let R~_{ji}(zhat) := eta(e~_j, R^(zhat) e~_i) = Sum_ab R^_{b,0}^{(j)} eta^{ba} R^_a^{(i)}(zhat) = Sum_n C_n zhat^n (rows j, columns i). Then C_0 = 1 (symplecticity at zhat^0), and R~ is NOT diagonal: every off-diagonal entry of C_1 is non-zero,

    C_1[0][.] = [1/20, (z + 3z^2 + z^3)/25, -(3 + 2z + 2z^2 + 3z^3)/25, (-1 + 2z - z^2)/25, (-1 - z + 2z^3)/25]    (z = zeta),

and R~ is Z/5-circulant with a phase: C_n[j+1][i+1] = zeta^{-n} C_n[j][i], so R~_{ii}(zhat) = R~_00(zeta^{-i} zhat). The full matrices C_0, ..., C_5 in Q(zeta) are in the log (C_6 off-diagonal entries exceed the reconstruction bound and print as residues; its diagonal entry C_6[0][0] = -2932381683/25600000000 is a large-bound reconstruction at 2^89-1 confirmed by its residue at 2^61-1, s.5). The matrix logarithm log R~ = Sum_k mu_k zhat^k is a symplectic logarithm, mu_k^T = (-1)^{k+1} mu_k for k <= 6 (mu_1, mu_3 symmetric, mu_2 antisymmetric), with

    mu_1[0][.] = C_1[0][.],
    mu_2[0][.] = [0, (-27 - 18z + 18z^2 + 27z^3)/1000, (-45 + 9z - 36z^2 - 18z^3)/1000, (-54 - 9z - 27z^2 - 45z^3)/1000, (-9 + 18z + 45z^2 + 36z^3)/1000],
    mu_3[0][.] = [-3/200, (-243 - 243z^2)/5000 - (579/8000) z, (243/5000)(z + z^3) - (951/40000) z^2, (-243 - 243z)/5000 - (579/8000) z^3, 951/40000 + (579/8000)(z + z^2) + (951/40000) z^3],

the other rows following from the circulant phase. In particular mu_2 != 0: the symbol has a non-zero antisymmetric EVEN part, so R~ is not exp(odd) even as a matrix. Consequence: the conifold-point limit of the R-matrix is a genuinely colour-mixing constant symplectic matrix. A scalar factorisation (Hodge class of any weights, covariant or not, constant or q-dependent) acts colour by colour and can never absorb it; this is the structural reason behind the negative results of SCALAR_SCAN and DIAG_SCAN (s.5).

  The symbol agrees with the QRR constant of the true class exactly through zhat^2 (equivalently p_1 = 9/5 agrees) and departs at zhat^3 by 37/250. The odd part of log f would correspond to p_1 = 9/5, p_3 = 6534/125, p_5 = -1251936/3125 (wt_0: 9/5, -126/125, -1/3125); no sub-multiset of c wt_i (c in {+-1, +-5, +-1/5}; 930 candidates) and no multiset of size <= 6 built from 171 Galois-orbit/rational blocks has these power sums, while the control search recovers wt_0 from its own power sums. ## 2. The frozen conifold-point CohFT

At the conifold point the TFT freezes: Delta_i^{1/2} = zeta^{4i} s f_0(s^5) -> zeta^{4i} s, so the constant TFT omega^ with Delta^_i^{1/2} = zeta^{4i} (Delta^_i = zeta^{3i}; the overall s^2 per genus-1 unit of Euler characteristic is the scale s^{-10} = t_c^{-2} of the genus-2 pole), unit 1^ = Sum_k zeta^{-4k} e~_k. The frozen theory R^.omega^ is a constant rank-5 CohFT (the s-leading part of the formal quintic at genus 2; rows R^, no u-corrections). Its graph sum (fq_msum.GraphSummer on constant series: FrozenRows with Q = R^, x = 1) reproduces the leading coefficient of every stable graph: 1807/3072000, -811/256000, -57/64000, 843/1024000, 3/51200, 87/32000, 9/12800 (graphs 1..7: genus-2 vertex; genus-1 loop; genus-0 double loop; g1-g1; g0 loop - g1; theta; dumbbell), total 1/1200 = the leading coefficient of F_2 (FORMAL_QUINTIC s.3-4). So the pole structure of the formal quintic at genus 2 is exactly the genus-2 potential of this constant CohFT, graph by graph.

## 3. The leading-order frame problem for zhat-constant M_0

**The system.** A q-dependent factorisation R = R' M with M = M_0(zhat) = exp(mu_1 zhat + mu_2 zhat^2 + mu_3 zhat^3), mu_k constant symplectic (mu_1, mu_3 symmetric, mu_2 antisymmetric in the normalised basis; 40 entries), is the constant-M family of SYMPLECTIC_SCAN at m_k = mu_k s^{-4k}. By the valuation bookkeeping there (weight-w monomials sit in s^{-10+4w} Q(zeta)[[u]]), the substitution moves the weight-w part of the s^{-10+4w} coefficient to s^{-10}, so the leading coefficient of the colour-summed graph Gamma becomes

    L_Gamma(mu) = c_Gamma + [weight 1 of s^-6](mu_1) + [weight 2 of s^-2](mu_1 mu_1, mu_2) + [weight 3 of s^+2](mu_1^3, mu_1 mu_2, mu_3),

a polynomial of weight <= 3 (the sub-leading orders s^{-9}, ..., s^{-1} after the substitution are NOT considered here). The symplectic fit had omitted m_1^3 and m_1 m_2; the fit was therefore redone over all 991 monomials of weight <= 3 (1 + 40 + 120 + 150 + 680) from 1010 + 20 random symplectic M at the orders s^{-10}, ..., s^2 (Vandermonde rank 991, 0 inconsistent surplus rows, all 91 (graph, order) coefficients verified on the 20 extra points). The weight structure is exact: s^{-10}, s^{-5}, s^0 pure weight 0; s^{-6} and s^{-1} weight 1 (s^{-1} is linear in m_1 for the edge graphs and absent for the vertex graph; it is not a zero order); s^{-2} weight 2; s^{+2} PURE weight 3 (the class -w mod 5 separates the weights); s^{-9}, -8, -7, -4, -3, +1 identically zero; m_3 appears only at s^{+2} and only for graphs 1, 2, 4; m_2 (via m_2 and m_1 m_2) only for graphs 1, 2, 4, 5; graphs 3, 6, 7 have no mu_2, mu_3 dependence at all. L_Gamma has 481, 261, 96, 491, 236, 46, 66 monomials (graphs 1..7), is cubic in mu_1 and LINEAR in (mu_2, mu_3) jointly, and Sum_Gamma L_Gamma = 1/1200 identically.

**Key check (the frozen theory).** The same leading coefficients are, by the group property R.omega = R'.(M.omega) applied pointwise in q, the genus-2 graph coefficients of the frozen theory with rows R^ M_0^{-1} and colour-mixing vertex CohFT M_0.omega^. At random mu the frozen graph sums equal L_Gamma(mu) for all seven graphs (total always 1/1200), and an independent 991-monomial fit of the frozen graph sums (1010 + 20 random mu) reproduces EVERY coefficient of every L_Gamma. This validates the substitution m_k = mu_k s^{-4k}, and every step of the valuation bookkeeping, at the polynomial level.

**The tautological solution.** Take mu_k = [zhat^k] log R~ (k = 1, 2, 3), i.e. M_0 = R~ + O(zhat^4) in the colour-colour form. Then R' = R^ M_0^{-1} tends to the constant orthonormal change of basis between the flat and the frozen colour bases, R~' = 1 + O(zhat^4), so at genus 2 every edge factor (total psi-order <= 2 per edge) and every kappa-leaf (orders <= 4) of R' vanishes, every edge graph is zero and L_1 = F_2 of M_0.omega^ = 1/1200: L_1 = 1/1200, L_2 = ... = L_7 = 0 (from the polynomials and from the frozen sum). The whole leading pole sits in the vertex graph, with vertex CohFT M_0.omega^ -- the formal analogue of the local P2 / conifold picture, but with a colour-mixing, non-Hodge vertex class.

**The solution set of {L_2 = ... = L_7 = 0} in A^40.** For fixed mu_1 the system is a 6 x 25 linear system in (mu_2, mu_3) whose coefficient rows for graphs 3, 6, 7 vanish; at generic mu_1 the (mu_2, mu_3)-matrix has rank 3 (rows 2, 4, 5) and the system is inconsistent, so solutions require mu_1 in W := {L_3 = L_6 = L_7 = 0} in A^15 (three non-constant cubics, so dim W <= 14). At the tautological point the Jacobian of (L_2, ..., L_7) has rank 2 (only the one-edge graphs 2, 4 have non-zero gradients: for a graph with >= 2 edges every edge factor is O(delta) under a perturbation delta of R~', so the term vanishes to second order), Zariski tangent space of dimension 38; the fibre over mu_1^taut has coefficient rank 2 and is consistent: a 23-dimensional affine space of (mu_2, mu_3), all solving the system (Jacobian rank 3 at its random points). Since the rank of the (mu_2, mu_3)-matrix is >= 2 on a neighbourhood of mu_1^taut (lower semicontinuity), the local dimension of the solution variety there is at most dim W + 23 <= 37 < 38, so the tautological point is a SINGULAR point. At random points of W (found on random affine 3-planes by resultants, eliminant degree 27) the (mu_2, mu_3)-rank is 3 and consistent (fibre dimension 22), the Jacobian of (L_3, L_6, L_7) has rank 3 (W smooth of dimension 12 there) and the full Jacobian rank 6: the solution variety has local dimension 15 - 3 + 22 = 34 at its generic points. The solutions are in no sense isolated; the question is whether any STRUCTURED solution exists.

**Covariant diagonal class** (M_0 = diag(exp(a_1 zeta^{-i} zhat + a_3 zeta^{-3i} zhat^3)), mu_2 = 0 forced): the seven restricted polynomials are 5 x the SCALAR_SCAN leading columns (T0_-10, T1_-6, T2_-2 colour sums, 5 T3_2, 5 T4_2), e.g. graph 2: -811/256000 - (343/12800) a_1 + (89/384) a_1^2 - (5/288) a_1^3 - (5/48) a_3. Eliminating a_3 with graph 2 leaves five cubics in a_1 with gcd 1: NO solution. This is the colour-summed version of SCALAR_SCAN s.5 and is now also proved exactly over Q from that table (referee).

**Diagonal class** (M_0 = diag(exp(x_i zhat + y_i zhat^3)); a diagonal antisymmetric mu_2 vanishes, so 10 effective parameters): in the Fourier modes X_a = Sum_i zeta^{ai} x_i, Y_a likewise, every graph is c + alpha X_1 + beta Y_3 + delta X_0 X_2 + eps X_1^2 + phi X_3 X_4 + gamma C(X) with the single cubic C = X_0^2 X_3 + 2 X_0 X_1 X_2 + X_0 X_4^2 + X_1^3/3 + 2 X_1 X_3 X_4 + X_2^2 X_4 + X_2 X_3^2 (the Z/5 grading; all cubic parts proportional). The six edge equations are affine in (Y_3, X_0 X_2, X_3 X_4, C) with a CONSTANT 6 x 4 coefficient matrix of rank 4, whose two left null vectors give the conditions -243/22000 - (229/5500) X_1 = 0 and 557/176000 + (239/44000) X_1 = 0 (the X_1^2 terms cancel): two coprime LINEAR conditions on X_1. NO solution in the diagonal class (any value of (X_0 X_2, X_3 X_4, C) is realisable over the closure, so this is the only obstruction). The diagonal classes therefore contain no solution at all, consistent with the tautological solution being non-diagonal.

**Circulant class** (Z/5-covariant full M_0: (mu_k)_{i+1,j+1} = zeta^{-k} (mu_k)_{ij}, i.e. (mu_k)_{ij} = zeta^{-ki} c_k(j - i) with c_1(-d) = zeta^d c_1(d), c_2(-d) = -zeta^{2d} c_2(d), c_3(-d) = zeta^{3d} c_3(d); 8 parameters c_1(0..2), c_2(1..2), c_3(0..2)); it is the fixed locus of the colour-shift symmetry of the system and contains the tautological point (log R~ is circulant), c_1^taut = (1/20, (z + 3z^2 + z^3)/25, -(3 + 2z + 2z^2 + 3z^3)/25). Graphs 3, 6, 7 involve only c_1. Their common zero set in c_1-space is

    the LINE ell = c_1^taut + t d,  d = (1, (z + z^3)/2, (z + z^2)/2), i.e. d(j) = (zeta^j + zeta^{3j})/2,

(gcd of degree 1 on random plane sections, all three cubics vanish identically along ell, all section points collinear; as a mu_1-direction D_{ij} = (zeta^{j-2i} + zeta^{i-2j})/2, a symmetrised rank-2 tensor of flat vectors), together with exactly ONE further point

    X = c_1^taut + (2/5)(1, (1 + zeta^{-1})/2, (1 + zeta^{-2})/2) = (9/20, -(4z + 2z^2 + 4z^3)/25, (2 - 2z - 2z^2 + 2z^3)/25),

found by blowing up along ell (chart v = lambda w; eliminant of degree 46 with squarefree part of degree 2: w(X) and the exceptional w = 0; two coordinate choices and two seeds agree), so X is the unique point off ell over the algebraic closure. The pure-c_1 cubics vanish to order 1 along ell generically and to orders 2 (graph 3) and 3 (graphs 6, 7) at the tautological point. Fibres in (c_2, c_3) (graphs 2, 4, 5; affine): over c_1^taut rank 2 (fibre dimension 3), over generic points of ell rank 3 (dimension 2), over X rank 3 (dimension 2), always consistent. So the circulant solution set consists of two 3-dimensional pieces through the tautological point -- the closure of the 2-dimensional fibres over ell, and the special 3-dimensional fibre over c_1^taut itself -- plus a 2-dimensional family over X. These are the only structured solutions found; whether any of them has a geometric meaning (a conifold-point vertex class with a Hodge-type or Gamma-type origin) is OPEN.

**Per-coloured version** (diagonal M_0 only; colour mixing makes coloured graphs meaningless). The frozen theory with colmask gives the 115 coloured leading coefficients as polynomials in (x_i, y_i) on 61 monomials of weight <= 3 (75 random diagonal M_0, rank 61). ## 4. Relation to the earlier scans

FORMAL_QUINTIC s.3 located the pole in the growth psi_n ~ L^{4n} of the rows; the symbol R^ is precisely that growth, packaged as a constant symplectic series in zhat = L^4 z, and the frozen theory R^.omega^ is the leading part of the formal quintic. SCALAR_SCAN s.5 (q-dependent covariant scalars a_1 ~ L^4, a_2 ~ L^12) is the covariant per-coloured diagonal class of s.3, reproduced here together with its colour-summed version; DIAG_SCAN's class, colour-summed and q-dependent, has no solution either; SYMPLECTIC_SCAN's constant M cannot touch the leading coefficient at all (weight 0), and its weight bookkeeping is what turns the constant-M fit into the leading system here. The new element is that the leading problem does have solutions -- a 34-dimensional variety, singular at the tautological point M_0 = R~ -- and that all of them are colour-mixing: the Z/5-circulant ones form an explicit 3 + 3 + 2-dimensional set, the diagonal ones do not exist.
