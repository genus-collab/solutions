## 3. Result at genus 2 (the question asked)

Reason (exact, at genus 2; referee-verified by fresh code). After Cf, val P_{a,n}^{(i)} = -4n + (0,-1,3,2,1)_a, val E_{mn} = -4(m+n) - 2, val Dil(d) = -4(d-1), val Delta^{g_v-1} = 2(g_v-1); hence EVERY decoration of every genus-g graph has valuation exactly -10(g-1): the pole comes from the growth psi_n ~ L^{4n} of the rows, not from Delta (which vanishes).
