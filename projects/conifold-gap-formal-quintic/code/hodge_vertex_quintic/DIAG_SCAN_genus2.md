# Formal quintic, genus 2: scan of the diagonal colour-dependent constant scalar frames (2026-11-04)

Folder: hodge_vertex_quintic/. Follows FORMAL_QUINTIC_genus2.md (engine, conventions, conifold frame Cf with alpha = beta = 0) and SCALAR_SCAN_genus2.md (the covariant scalar family, the vertex shortcut, the valuation bookkeeping). New code: fq_diag_lib.py (the ten-parameter frame, local monomials, exact F_p linear algebra), fq_diag_report.py (Cf stage, valuation checks, tables, comparison with the covariant scan), fq_diag_solve.py (the polar systems), driver genus2_diag.py -> logs/genus2_diag.log (both primes 2^89-1 and 2^61-1, 1232 s, no failing check). No existing module was changed. Everything is an exact finite computation (F_p with rational reconstruction; Q(zeta)-valued entries reconstructed from their Galois conjugates). Evidence at genus 2 only; AI-checked only.

## 1. Set-up

**The family.** SCALAR_SCAN_genus2.md showed that no constant Z/5-covariant scalar phi_i(z) = phi_0(zeta^{-i} z) regularises any coloured edge graph at genus 2. Here the covariance is dropped: the factorisation is R = R' diag(phi_i(z)) with

    phi_i(z) = exp(a_1^{(i)} z + a_2^{(i)} z^3),    i = 0..4,

ten independent q-constants (only these orders enter at genus 2, since the total z-order of a genus-2 decoration is <= 3g-3 = 3; odd powers keep each phi_i symplectic). The frame: rows R' = R phi_i^{-1}, i.e. the z-coefficients of the rows at colour i are multiplied by c_n^{(i)} = [z^n] exp(-a_1^{(i)} z - a_2^{(i)} z^3) (c_1 = -a_1, c_2 = a_1^2/2, c_3 = -a_1^3/6 - a_2, all of the colour's own constants); vertex CohFT phi_i . 1 at colour i, with no colour mixing, so the vertex integrals are those of SCALAR_SCAN s.1 with (a_1, a_2) replaced by (a_1^{(i)}, a_2^{(i)}): I(1; 0) = a_1^{(i)}/2, I(1; 1) = 1/24, I(2; empty) = (a_1^{(i)})^3/10 + a_2^{(i)}/48, I(2; 4) = 1/1152. The frame is R-action by the group element diag(phi_i), so the colour-summed total is independent of all ten parameters and equals F_2; only the split into coloured terms moves.

Relation to the earlier frames. The covariant scalar is the 2-parameter subfamily a_k^{(i)} = zeta^{-i(2k-1)} a_k; on it everything below reduces to SCALAR_SCAN. In particular the point frame F5 is the origin (all ten constants 0), the Hodge frame F4 is the covariant point (-1/12, 1/360) and the true-class frame F0 the covariant point (-3/20, -7/2500). The implementation calls fq_phi.phi_inv_coeffs and fq_phi.PhiVertexTable with colour argument 0 and the colour's own values (fq_phi hard-codes the covariant phases only through its colour argument; VertexTable caches per instance, so five instances do not collide); at the covariant pattern the rows and vertex tables coincide with fq_phi.phi_frame (checked).

**Polynomial structure.** Write x, y for a_1 of the first and second colour of a colouring tuple, p, q for a_2 (a-weight 1 for a_1, 3 for a_2). The weight count of SCALAR_SCAN s.1 is colour-blind: a vertex of colour c and psi-deficit D_v has weight exactly D_v in colour c's constants, a row coefficient of z-order n has weight <= n in its colour's constants, an edge E_{mn} has total weight <= m+n+1, a dilaton leaf Dil(d) <= d-1, and the sum over a decoration is 3g-3 = 3. So a coloured term is a polynomial of total a-weight <= 3 in the constants of its colours: at most the 5 monomials 1, x, x^2, x^3, p for one colour, the 12 monomials 1, x, y, x^2, xy, y^2, x^3, x^2 y, xy^2, y^3, p, q for two (three colours do not occur at genus 2).

Refinement per colour (exact, all orders). For a mixed edge joining colours c, c' write N(z, w) = eta^{-1} - R'^{-1}(z) eta^{-1} R'^{-1}(w)^T, so that E(z, w) = N(z, w)/(z+w); N_{ab} has weight <= a in colour c's constants and <= b in colour c''s. Since N(z, -z) = 0 (symplecticity of the frame rows), both expansions

    E_{mn} = Sum_{k<=n} (-1)^k N_{m+1+k, n-k} = Sum_{k<=m} (-1)^k N_{m-k, n+1+k}

are valid; the second bounds the weight in colour c by m, the first the weight in colour c' by n. Hence the weight of a coloured term in the constants of colour c is at most

    Sum_{v of colour c} (3 g_v - 3 + val_v) + #(edges with both ends of colour c),

val_v the number of half-edges at v (the half-edge orders cancel against the vertex deficits, each leaf costs 1, and a monochrome edge keeps its +1). For the mixed colourings of graphs 4, 5, 7 this gives weight <= 1 in each colour, so only 1, x, y, xy can occur; for the mixed theta graph it gives 0 in each colour, so no a-dependence at all; for monochrome graphs it gives the full 3. The observed zero pattern of s.3 is exactly this bound, with no cancellation involved. The 12-monomial basis was used throughout as a valid superset.

**Interpolation.** The unchanged graph sum gsum_q.genus2_sums was evaluated at 15 random points of F_p^10 (seed p mod 1000003 + 1), at the all-zero point, at a covariant random point through fq_phi.phi_frame, and in the frames F5, F4, F0 (20 sums per prime, 12-35 s each). Per coloured graph the monomial coefficients were solved from the first m = 5 or 12 points (exact F_p inverse of the m x m monomial matrix, applied to the NEls); the remaining 10 or 3 points are consistency checks.

**Valuation bookkeeping (Cf, s = 1/L, u = s^5).** As in SCALAR_SCAN s.1, now colour by colour: each factor c_k^{(i)} raises the valuation of a row coefficient by 4k, each unit of psi-deficit at a vertex of colour i by 4, so the a-weight-W part of every decoration sits at valuation >= -10 + 4W and its Cf exponents lie in the class -W mod 5. Exact version (from the L-powers of the numerator keys, no truncation): the exponent classes of the Cf image of a weight-W coefficient are {0}, {4}, {3}, {2} for W = 0, 1, 2, 3 and the maximal L-degrees are 10, 6, 2, -2. Hence the polar part of a coloured term is

    const_{-10} s^{-10} + const_{-5} s^{-5} + Sum_c a_1^{(c)} (X_{c,-6} s^{-6} + X_{c,-1} s^{-1}) + Sum_{c<=c'} a_1^{(c)} a_1^{(c')} Q_{cc',-2} s^{-2}:

the a_2's never enter a polar term, the constants are the point-frame values, the s^{-6} and s^{-1} coefficients are linear forms in the a_1's and the s^{-2} coefficient a quadratic form. Because the classes 0, 4, 3, 2 are distinct, a coloured term is a u-series if and only if its weight-1, weight-2 and weight-3 parts vanish separately.

## 4. The solves (identical at both primes)

**Full system** {all negative powers of s of all 110 coloured edge graphs = 0}. Its s^-10 and s^-5 equations are a-independent: their left-hand sides are the point-frame constants of s.3, non-zero for 110 of 110 coloured edge graphs at both orders. The system is therefore inconsistent, and no further row reduction was needed or done: the leading pole of every coloured edge graph is independent of any constant diagonal scalar, colour-dependent or not, exactly as the bookkeeping of s.1 predicts (an a-dependent term first appears at s^-6).

**Weak system** (the a-dependent polar coefficients only). The s^-6 and s^-1 coefficients give 220 linear forms in (a_1^{(0)}, ..., a_1^{(4)}), 180 of them non-zero (the 40 zero ones are the mixed theta colourings); the s^-2 coefficients give 90 quadratic forms. Gaussian elimination of the linear part over F_p: rank 5, reduced row echelon form {a_1^{(0)} = a_1^{(1)} = a_1^{(2)} = a_1^{(3)} = a_1^{(4)} = 0}. The rank is already 5 from the s^-6 forms alone, from the s^-1 forms alone, from the 30 monochrome coloured edge graphs alone, from the 80 mixed colourings alone, and from any single edge graph 2..7 with all its colourings. This overdetermination is structural: by colour-shift covariance a monochrome edge graph at colour c contributes the equation (X_{-6}) zeta^c a_1^{(c)} = 0 (e.g. graph 2: -343/64000 zeta^c a_1^{(c)} = 0), one per colour, already of rank 5. The kernel of the linear part is spanned by the five a_2^{(i)}, which no polar coefficient involves, and the quadratic forms vanish identically on it. Exact solution set of the weak system: a_1^{(i)} = 0 for all i, a_2^{(i)} arbitrary (dimension 5). Colour dependence therefore buys nothing: the solution set is that of the covariant scan, colour by colour.

**u-series statement** (all orders, to O(s^80)). Over the 115 coloured graphs the weight-1 series coefficients (class 4; 2780 non-zero) have rank 5 in the a_1's, forcing a_1^{(i)} = 0; given that, the p and q series coefficients (class 2; 240 non-zero, 160 of them from edge graphs, since the monochrome graphs 2 and 4 carry a_2 terms) have rank 5 in the a_2's, forcing a_2^{(i)} = 0. The weight-2 coefficients (95 non-zero) are irrelevant once a_1 = 0. The only diagonal constant scalar frame, covariant or not, in which every coloured genus-2 term is a u-series is the point frame (all ten constants zero); the Hodge frames F4 and F0 are not (their s^-6 and s^-2 terms are the linear and quadratic forms above at a_1^{(i)} = -zeta^{-i}/12 and -3 zeta^{-i}/20). The same holds over the 110 coloured edge graphs alone. The leading coefficients that imply this conclusion (the s^-6 forms of any one edge graph, and the p-entries of graphs 1, 2, 4) are the hand- and fresh-code-verified entries of s.3.

## 5. Conclusion

At genus 2 and the zeta point, every coloured stable graph with an edge has the a-independent pole s^-10 = t_c^-2 of FORMAL_QUINTIC s.3 in every frame R = R' diag(exp(a_1^{(i)} z + a_2^{(i)} z^3)) with constant a's; a constant diagonal scalar, whether covariant or colour-dependent, can only add polar terms at s^-6, s^-2 and s^-1 (linear and quadratic in the a_1's; the a_2's are never polar), and it adds none exactly when all a_1^{(i)} vanish. Every coloured term is a u-series only in the point frame. The mixed colourings are at most bilinear in the constants of their two colours, and the mixed theta colourings do not depend on them at all, by the per-colour weight bound of s.1.
