# All-genus regularity of edge graphs for local P2 x C^2: recommended pathway

This synthesises four proposed pathways and the critiques written against them. I ran no scripts for this note. Checks marked "reported" were run by the pathway and critique agents, and I have not rerun them.

## 1. Target theorem

**Theorem R_P2.** The setting is K_P2 x C^2 on the fibrewise torus T_F, with w = (E, -e1, -e2) and E = e1 + e2. Take the frame-B Hodge-vertex graph sum (R' = R_Z h_w^-1, mode B), built from the code's rows in the conifold polarisation (XX = XX_cf, XX(0) = -2/9). Then for every g >= 2, every genus-g stable graph with an edge is regular at y = -1/27 once decorations are summed. It should also be regular colouring by colouring.

**What it is conditional on:**
- Lemma A for h <= g-1. This is on the Hodge side and is proved modulo literature (2D Toda, round 2, proof of record R3). The weights (E, -e1, -e2) are the conifold class at x = e1/e2, so it transfers verbatim with c -> E.
- F2 to all orders: the frame-B identity row at t_c = 0 equals E/(E+z). This is load-bearing and is exact only to z^9.
- Three lemmas still to prove: the closure lemma (stage 2), Lemma H2 (stage 3) and the regular-colour lemma (stage 5).

**Not claimed.** The theorem is about the code-defined graph sum. Two further statements need a local-P2 frame-B formula (the analogue of Prop 13.2), which has no written proof; steps (b) and (d) have no analogue of Lemma 13.3. Those statements are:
- the identification of the graph sum with F_g^cf;
- the single-vertex pole being (-3)^(g-1) gap_g. This would prove BS's conjecture rmk:cfgap, so it needs a separate referee pass.

## 2. Recommended route

**Stage 0. Hodge side and counting (reused).**
- Reuse s.13.1 (i)-(v), Theorem C and Lemma A verbatim, with -x c^3 -> 3 e1 e2 E^3.
- New: bookkeeping only. The rescaling rho = E v/t_c should become an explicit lemma, with v = (1+3t)/(E t). Colour 0 is the singular colour only on the real Galois branch.
- The counting step is independent of rank. I checked this by reading s.13.1, not by computation.

**Stage 1. Identity row (KEY FORMULA_P2).** Main route is the integrable pathway's closed form, derived from BS's s-coordinates:
- r_B = Sum_l rho_l (-E v z)^l E / prod_{i=1}^{l+1} (E + i z), where the rho_l are Airy-Gaussian moments.
- The measure is ds/(s3 sqrt(Disc)). The sqrt(Disc) ds/s3^3 displayed in BS looks like a typo; it agrees with the data at leading order only.
- Reported: an exact match through u^6 and z^9 at three weight pairs.
- From this formula the pole set {-E/j : j <= k+1} with simple poles, and regularity at z = 0, are visible directly. The s.5.3 regularity argument becomes trivial.
- Lemma Z-infinity is t_c = E v Phi(v), a genus-0 period identity. It is reported exact to Del^11 and still needs a written proof, for example via the Picard-Fuchs equation.
- After that, Prop 5.3 and Theorem 5.4 (s.5.2-5.5) give L1 verbatim.

Fallback route, if BS genus-0 integrals are ruled out (see Q1). Use the Frobenius/WKB port of s.3.10, with two fixes:
- The P2 transport point w = 0 is a regular singular point. P_tr ~ -E^2/w, so the claim "P_tr(0) != 0" is false. Work with (1/w) Op instead.
- Prove the simple-pole (Norlund) lemma. It is reported for n <= 9.

**Stage 2. Closure lemma (new; this is where "on shell" lives).**
- Claim: at c1 = C1cf, the rows (P0, P1, P2) satisfy z d/dt_c v = (A_c - L) v, with A_c independent of z and eta-self-adjoint.
- Proof route: this is a universal identity in BS's differential ring. Transport it from c1_LR by algebraic independence (BS prop:algindABC = BFGW Lem 4.2).
- "On shell" should be stated as: XX is the Riccati solution that is regular at Delta = 0. XX(0) = -2/9 is then the indicial value, and the higher coefficients follow from it, provided there is no resonance (to be checked).
- Consequences: symplecticity, K_0 = 1, orthogonality and E_j0 = E_0j^T. This replaces the Laplace arguments in s.4.8, s.8.5 and s.9.4.
- Caution: symplecticity holds exactly off shell as well (reported at XXSHIFT = 1/10). So the on-shell content is the absence of a z-even correction to A_c, not the pairing.

**Stage 3. H-row and H^2-row.**
- H-row: Prop 6.1 verbatim. Reported exact through t_c^7.
- New Lemma H2: sigma^(-1) is independent of psi, and sigma^(k) lies in (Lam^+_{k+1})^3.
  - Constants are allowed, because Prop 9.2 and stage 4 tolerate them.
  - The real danger is psi-linear remainders in the H^2-row.
  - A12^-1 = O(t_c) supplies the order shift. The lemma then reduces to one closed-form identity: the psi-linear coefficient, which comes from XX times sigma_1^(-1) = E/t_c, cancels.
- Route B (BS's exact s3 and s3^2 insertion identities) would turn this into finitely many scalar identities in A_cf, D_cf and E_cf.

**Stage 4. E_00 by a residue lemma (replaces the rank-3 flow of s.7).**
- Set N = kappa - <Y(psi), Y(psi')>. Symplecticity gives N(psi, -psi) = 0, all poles are simple and N decays at infinity.
- Partial fractions then give: [lam_i lam_l'] of N/(psi+psi') = il/(E(i+l)) [lam_i lam_l'] N.
- Counting gives F3, the support (i-1)+(l-1) <= k, and rank one at t^0.
- The argument is independent of rank. Checked by hand against the conifold tables of s.7.5-7.6 at t^0 and t^1.
- Keep the rank-2 flow only as a cross-check.

**Stage 5. Regular colours (R) and legs.**
- Need an all-order proof that the regular rows are analytic at the conifold. Either:
  - prove the form cPP_k = sq^-1 N_k(L)/(E+3L)^{k+1}, via BS finite generation; or
  - use regular-root transport, which is non-singular at w = 0.
- Then Prop 9.2-9.4 and Cor 9.5 apply verbatim.

**Stage 6. Assembly.** As in s.13.1.

**Hardest step.** F2 to all orders, independently of BS: a Mellin-Barnes connection coefficient of the 3F2 operator, with Gauss multiplication. A critic found that the local Dirichlet integral gives exactly E/(E+z) with no h_w tail. That is a consistency check, not a proof, because the frame identification is the open part.

After F2, the hard points are the closure lemma and Lemma H2. On the evidence I would not rank the rank-3 flow among them.

## 4. Alternatives and why they rank lower

- **Direct WKB/Meijer-G port.** Viable as the stage-1 fallback, but it needs the regular-singular repair. Its "Lemma P = t_c-flatness" is mis-formulated: the edges do not depend on C1i, and only XX matters. It also leans on BS cfasym for F2 without checking the normalisation.
- **"Universality" (rank N).** The framing overclaims; it is plausible only for N <= 3. Its reason why the flow holds ("z d/dt_c is the flat derivative") is wrong, because the change of gauge depends on z. What it really needs is the closure lemma.
- **Common local model (2D Dirichlet).**
  - A literal gauge equivalence with the conifold is falsified: ranks differ, and the stored per-graph ratios are not universal.
  - Its degeneration lemma is wrong as stated. Three boundary curves meet at one corner (not normal crossings), and there are spurious Gamma(eps+1-m) poles whose cancellation is unproved.
  - What survives is the residue lemma (adopted in stage 4) and the Gauss-multiplication consistency check.
- **Finite generation / HAE alone.** It cannot give regularity graph by graph, because the vertex graph does not depend on F. It is useful only as the transfer tool of stage 2.
