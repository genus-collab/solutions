     rho C1i N1 = E/t_c + (E^2/9)/(E+z) + O(t_c), with [t_c^m] in span{1/(E+jz): j <= m+1} and no constant term.

   - The polarisation matters: regularity needs XX(0) = -2/9, and the rank-one limit and leaf supports also need the next Taylor
     coefficient of XX on the conifold section.
