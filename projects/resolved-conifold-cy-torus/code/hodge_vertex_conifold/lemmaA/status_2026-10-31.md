## 1. What the regularity rests on
- Theorem LC (refereed): F1-F3 and the leaf structure, to all orders in t and z. One analytic input: Prop. 3.15 (Laplace's method plus
  Stirling's series with a cited remainder bound).
- Theorem C (string equation), homogeneity (dilaton), and the string/dilaton reduction of Conjecture M to M_irr.
- M_irr(h_v) is needed only at singular vertices of genus h_v >= 2, and only for h_v <= g-1. Reason: a genus-g stable graph with an edge and
  no legs has no genus-g vertex (g = b_1 + Sum g_v; a tree has two valence-1 vertices, and a genus-0 one is unstable). This is elementary and
  is contained in Route D's Lemma 5.3, refereed sound in round 1.
- The frame-B formula (Givental-Teleman reconstruction, as the owner pointed out) is NOT needed for the regularity of the graph terms
  themselves. It identifies them with the terms of the conifold x C^2 GW potential. Together with Brini-Schuler's closed form (H) (Prop. 3.1),
  it shows that the single-vertex graph carries exactly gap_g. A paragraph proving the frame-B formula by citation was being written and
  refereed at wrap-up (workflow wf_de5bc7e9-d4c), for leaf_structure_proof.md s.13.3 and gap (G3).
